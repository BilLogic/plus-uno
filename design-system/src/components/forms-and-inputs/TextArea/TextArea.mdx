## Usage

Use a Text area for free text over several lines: session notes, feedback, a
reason. Use a Text field for one short answer, and a Rich text editor only when
the text needs formatting.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Parts

1. **Label.** Names the field. A required marker and an info tooltip sit beside it.
2. **Box.** 80 high at least, growing with `rows`, and scrolls past it.
3. **Control.** The native textarea. It resizes vertically.
4. **Caption.** A message on the left and, with `maxLength`, a count on the right.
5. **Save and Cancel** (subtle only). Shown below a subtle text area while it is edited.

## Best practices

### Hint at the answer, not the control.

A placeholder shows what a good answer looks like.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<TextArea label="Session notes" rows={1} placeholder="What happened in the session?" />}
    doCaption="Hint names the answer."
    dontExample={<TextArea label="Session notes" rows={1} placeholder="Type here" />}
    dontCaption="Hint describes the control."
/>

### Show an error on the field itself.

The field carries the invalid state. Use `isInvalid` only when the form cannot go on.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<TextArea label="Note for the tutor" rows={1} placeholder="Add a note for the tutor" isInvalid errorMessage="Add a note before you send." />}
    doCaption="The field shows the invalid state."
    dontExample={<TextArea label="Note for the tutor" rows={1} placeholder="Add a note for the tutor" />}
    dontCaption="A default field gives no sign of the problem."
/>

### Disable instead of hiding.

A disabled field tells people the option exists but is unavailable.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<TextArea label="Session notes" rows={1} defaultValue="Notes are locked" isDisabled />}
    doCaption="Visible but disabled."
    dontExample={<TextArea label="Session notes" rows={1} defaultValue="Notes are locked" />}
    dontCaption="An enabled field that cannot take input."
/>

### Put help in the caption.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<TextArea label="Session notes" rows={3} caption="What went well, and what to try next time." />}
    doCaption="Put help in the caption, where it stays after the person types."
    dontExample={<TextArea label="Session notes" rows={3} placeholder="Write what went well and what to try next time" />}
    dontCaption="Help in the placeholder disappears when the person starts typing."
/>

### Match `rows` to the expected answer.

One row is a short note; six rows is a paragraph. The box starts at 80 whatever `rows` says.

### Set a count only for a real limit.

Set `maxLength` when something downstream truncates; do not add a limit just to show a count.

### Use subtle for editing in place.

`appearance="subtle"` shows Save and Cancel while editing and does not save when focus leaves the field, so a click elsewhere in the middle of a paragraph does not publish half a thought.

### Let a warning accept the value.

`warningMessage` draws the warning edge and `successMessage` the success edge, each with its message. The value is accepted in both.

## Accessibility

- **The label is associated whether you pass `id` or not**, and `isLabelHidden`
  clips it out of sight without removing the name.
- **An error is announced.** `isInvalid` sets `aria-invalid` and renders the
  message with `role="alert"`; a warning, a success message and standing help are
  described. A count is part of the description.
- **Focus is a ring**, with no change of fill.
- **Save and Cancel are real buttons**, reached in the tab order after the field.
  Esc cancels, and Ctrl or Cmd with Enter saves.
