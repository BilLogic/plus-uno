## Usage

Use `CloseButton` wherever a surface closes as a whole — the corner of a
Modal, the end of a Section message or a Flag. For an action that
removes one item from a set rather than closing a surface, use a plain
`IconButton` with its own icon and label instead.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Parts

1. **Container.** A subtle Icon button, 32 at default spacing and 24 at compact.
2. **Glyph.** The `xmark` glyph at 16, always drawn.

## Best practices

### Sit top-right in its container.

The close button anchors the top-right corner of the modal, panel or message it closes.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<div style={surface}><span>Your session moved to 4 pm.</span><CloseButton label="Dismiss update" /></div>}
    doCaption="Top-right of the message."
    dontExample={<div style={surfaceStacked}><span>Your session moved to 4 pm.</span><CloseButton label="Dismiss update" /></div>}
    dontCaption="Bottom-left, under the content."
/>

### Name what it closes.

Use "Close" for a surface that blocks, like a Modal, and "Dismiss" plus what goes away for a message: "Dismiss update", "Dismiss warning". The × glyph is the same either way, so `label` is the only thing that tells a screen reader user what just went away.

```jsx
<CloseButton label="Dismiss update" onClick={dismiss} />
```

### Use inverse only on colored grounds.

Inverse tone is for the declared colored and dark grounds. Neutral surfaces use default.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<div style={surface}><span>Your session moved to 4 pm.</span><CloseButton label="Dismiss update" /></div>}
    doCaption="Default tone on a neutral surface."
    dontExample={<div style={surface}><span>Your session moved to 4 pm.</span><CloseButton tone="inverse" label="Dismiss update" /></div>}
    dontCaption="Inverse tone on a neutral surface: the glyph disappears."
/>

### Close a whole surface, not one item.

A close button dismisses the surface it sits on. To remove one item from a set, use an `IconButton` with its own icon and label.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<div style={surface}><div style={surfaceList}><span>Maya Chen joined your group.</span><span>Sam Lee joined your group.</span></div><CloseButton label="Dismiss update" /></div>}
    doCaption="One close button for the whole message."
    dontExample={<div style={surface}><div style={surfaceList}><span style={itemRow}>Maya Chen joined your group.<CloseButton label="Dismiss Maya Chen" /></span><span style={itemRow}>Sam Lee joined your group.<CloseButton label="Dismiss Sam Lee" /></span></div></div>}
    dontCaption="A close button on each item inside the message."
/>

### There is no disabled state.

A close control stays available even while the surface it closes is in some
other state (loading, in error). Don't pass a disabled prop through — it
isn't one of `CloseButton`'s props.

</div>

## Accessibility

- **A real, always-focusable button.** Reached with Tab, activated with
  Enter or Space, same as any `IconButton`.
- **The accessible name is the whole contract.** The × glyph is decorative;
  `label` is what a screen reader announces, so it must say what closes.
- **Inverse tone doesn't change behavior**, only contrast on a dark or
  colored ground — pick it when the default subtle hover would be
  invisible there.
