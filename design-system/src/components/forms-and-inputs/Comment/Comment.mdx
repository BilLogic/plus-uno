## Usage

Use a comment for one entry in a threaded discussion: a note on a session, a reply in a review thread. For a single text entry with no author or thread context, use Text area.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Parts

1. **Avatar.** Sits beside the comment's content.
2. **Header.** The author, the time, an edited marker and, when restricted, who the comment is visible to.
3. **Content.** The comment body.
4. **Actions**, or **saving text** in their place while a change is in flight.
5. **Nested replies.** Sit below, indented, at one heading level deeper through `headingLevel`.

## Best practices

### Nest replies through `nestedReplies`, not by composing comments yourself.

Passing replies through `nestedReplies` opens a heading context at `headingLevel`, so any `Heading` inside the reply tree nests one level deeper automatically. Composing comments directly without this prop skips that step.

```jsx
<Comment
    author="Jane Park"
    time="2h ago"
    content="Looks good to me."
    nestedReplies={<Comment author="Sam Lee" time="1h ago" content="Agreed." />}
/>
```

### Swap `actions` for `savingText` with `isSaving`; do not hide them yourself.

Set `isSaving` rather than conditionally rendering `actions`. It keeps the saving state in one prop instead of two places making the same decision.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Comment author="Ari Patel" time="2h ago" content="Can we move Thursday's session to 5 pm?" actions={<Button appearance="subtle" spacing="compact" text="Reply" />} isSaving savingText="Saving your reply…" />}
    doCaption="While the reply saves, the saving text replaces the Reply action."
    dontExample={<Comment author="Ari Patel" time="2h ago" content="Can we move Thursday's session to 5 pm?" actions={<Button appearance="subtle" spacing="compact" text="Reply" />} />}
    dontCaption="The Reply action stays live, with nothing to say a reply is saving."
/>

### Show who wrote it and when.

Every comment names its author and time. Anonymous, undated comments cannot be trusted or followed up.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Comment author="Maya Chen" time="2 h ago" content="Moved your 3 pm session to 4 pm." />}
    doCaption="Author and time are both present."
    dontExample={<Comment author="Someone" time="recently" content="Moved your 3 pm session to 4 pm." />}
    dontCaption="No name, no time."
/>

### Write the comment as a plain sentence.

Sentence case, normal punctuation. Capitals and stacked marks read as shouting.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Comment author="Maya Chen" time="2 h ago" content="Can you add the due date?" />}
    doCaption="A plain question."
    dontExample={<Comment author="Maya Chen" time="2 h ago" content="ADD THE DATE!!!" />}
    dontCaption="All caps, stacked marks."
/>

### Ask for one clear thing.

Name the action and the detail. An open "Thoughts?" leaves the reader guessing.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Comment author="Maya Chen" time="2 h ago" content="Can you confirm the 3 pm slot?" />}
    doCaption="One action, one detail."
    dontExample={<Comment author="Maya Chen" time="2 h ago" content="Thoughts?" />}
    dontCaption="No action named."
/>

## Accessibility

- **A real article.** Each comment is an `<article>`, giving assistive technology a landmark per entry in the thread.
- **Replies nest their headings correctly.** Because `nestedReplies` opens its own heading context, a reply thread's headings read in the right outline order rather than all landing at the same level.
- **Restricted visibility is stated, not just implied.** `restrictedTo` renders as visible text in the header, so who can see the comment is not communicated by color or icon alone.
