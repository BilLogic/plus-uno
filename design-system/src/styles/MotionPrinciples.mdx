<FoundationSection title="Four principles">

Four principles follow, and each carries the question a reviewer asks. Nearly everything a tutor does in a day is Productive. One moment in a term is Expressive.

Every Do and Don't below is the same scene with one rule broken, so the difference is the lesson. Each draws its paths from start to landing first, then plays; use the speed control to watch at ½× or ¼×.

<FoundationSubsection title="Show what changed">

Where it came from, where it went, and where you are now.

*Ask: Can someone point to where the thing went?*

<Canvas of={PrinciplesStories.ShowWhatChanged} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Always responsive">

Something answers the person at once. The first visible answer to a press, hover or toggle is its color or press state. It starts in the same frame and is 90% done by about <SpringMs token="appearance.fast" />. A moving part, such as a Toggle's thumb, follows on `spatial.fast`.

Nothing waits for an animation to finish. Any motion can be interrupted mid-way, switched to Reduced mode by the system setting, and paused if it runs longer than 5 s.

*Ask: Does anything wait for an animation to finish? Can someone stop it?*

<Canvas of={PrinciplesStories.AlwaysResponsive} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Move like real things">

Motion keeps the speed of the hand that started it, carries the weight of the thing that moves, and settles where it belongs.

*Ask: Does the weight fit the object and the input?*

<Canvas of={PrinciplesStories.MoveLikeRealThings} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Quiet by default, bold when it counts">

How often something happens sets how loud its motion is, never to zero at page level. A change seen dozens of times a day moves quietly, usually a short fade or a flat slide. From the keyboard, controls and Popups open with no animation; layers and pages keep their motion. Reduced mode keeps one short fade, so a change is still noticed.

Expressive marks the end of something worth finishing, a few times a week at most. Never a control, a field, or anything done dozens of times a day.

*Ask: Does it happen many times a day? Then make it quieter.*

<Canvas of={PrinciplesStories.QuietByDefault} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Sound">

Sound is rare and never the only signal. The rules are on <DocsLink id="foundations-sound-when-to-play">Sound · When to play</DocsLink> and <DocsLink id="foundations-sound-settings-and-accessibility">Settings and accessibility</DocsLink>.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Before you add motion">

1. What does it explain? Where something came from, where it went, or what is new.
2. How often will someone see it? Dozens a day means quiet.
3. Does it answer at once, and can it be interrupted?
4. Would someone notice it every time? If yes and it is not a milestone, quiet it down.

If the answer to the first question is "nothing", leave it out. A new kind of motion is a question for the design team.

</FoundationSection>
