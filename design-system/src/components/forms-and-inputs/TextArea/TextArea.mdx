## When to use

Use a Text area for free text over several lines: session notes, feedback, a
reason. Use a Text field for one short answer, and a Rich text editor only when
the text needs formatting.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Usage notes

### Put help in the caption.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<TextArea label="Session notes" rows={3} caption="What went well, and what to try next time." />}
    doCaption="Put help in the caption, where it stays after the person types."
    dontExample={<TextArea label="Session notes" rows={3} placeholder="Write what went well and what to try next time" />}
    dontCaption="Help in the placeholder disappears when the person starts typing."
/>

### Rules

- **Match `rows` to the expected answer.** One row is a short note; six rows is a
  paragraph. The box starts at 80 whatever `rows` says.
- **A count is for a real limit.** Set `maxLength` when something downstream
  truncates; do not add a limit just to show a count.
- **`appearance="subtle"` is for editing in place.** It shows Save and Cancel
  while editing and does not save when focus leaves the field, so a click
  elsewhere in the middle of a paragraph does not publish half a thought.
- **A warning accepts the value.** Use `isInvalid` only when the form cannot
  go on.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Accessibility

- **The label is associated whether you pass `id` or not**, and `isLabelHidden`
  clips it out of sight without removing the name.
- **An error is announced.** `isInvalid` sets `aria-invalid` and renders the
  message with `role="alert"`; a warning, a success message and standing help are
  described. A count is part of the description.
- **Focus is a ring** in `border.focused`, with no change of fill.
- **Save and Cancel are real buttons**, reached in the tab order after the field.
  Esc cancels, and Ctrl or Cmd with Enter saves.

</div>
