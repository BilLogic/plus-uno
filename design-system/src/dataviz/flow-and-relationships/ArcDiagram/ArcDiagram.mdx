## When to use

Use `ArcDiagram` for weighted connections across a small, ordered set of items,
such as subjects students take together, lessons that lead into one another or
units that share skills. Keep the node count small enough that every label fits
along the axis.

For larger or un-ordered networks, use `NetworkGraph`. For weighted connections
around a circle, use `DependencyWheel`. For quantities flowing through stages,
use `SankeyDiagram`.

## Data shape

`data` is an array of `[from, to, weight]` rows where `from` and `to` are node
names and `weight` sets arc thickness.

```jsx
<ArcDiagram
    data={[
        ['CMU', 'Lincoln Middle', 14],
        ['CMU', 'Roosevelt K-8', 9],
        ['Pitt', 'Roosevelt K-8', 11],
        ['Pitt', 'North High', 16],
    ]}
/>
```

## Color and accessibility

- **Per-node color.** Nodes cycle through the [categorical order](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-color--docs) starting at `--ds-chart-categorical-1` and repeat past eight; color separates nodes and encodes no value.
- **Position, thickness and labels carry the reading.** Every node is named on the axis and arc thickness shows weight, so the chart reads without hue.
- **Filter weak links.** Many arcs from one node stack into a dense fan; filter out minor connections before rendering.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** Renders a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation. Name the chart in its surrounding Section or Card header.
- **Contrast.** Colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens ([Foundations](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-foundations--docs)).

</div>

## States

`ArcDiagram` has no empty, loading or error state. Mount a `Skeleton` matching the chart's height, a compact `EmptyState` naming the missing data, or a `SectionMessage` (`appearance="danger"`) with a retry action:

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={400} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Subject links could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No subject links yet" description="Links appear once students take more than one subject." />
    : <ArcDiagram data={data} />}
```
