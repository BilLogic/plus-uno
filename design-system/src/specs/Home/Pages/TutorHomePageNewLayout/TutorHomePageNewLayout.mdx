### How the blocks take the grid

Every block is a direct child of the content grid, placed on whole columns by the formulas in `homeGrid.js`. With today's tokens the spans come out as below.

- **Greeting** is a page-level Section with a tabs header; **My Badge**, **Your Impact** and **the skills chart** are Cards; **Personalized Recommendations** is a Section holding a row of lesson Cards.
- The greeting and My Badge, and the skills chart and Your Impact, are a main column and a side column. The side takes its preferred span (3 for the badge, 4 for Your Impact), widened until it reaches `--section-min-width-side`; the main column keeps `--section-min-width-main`. When either row cannot split, both stack, and My Badge and Your Impact share a row of cards instead.
- Personalized Recommendations shows the most of 4 · 3 · 2 · 1 lesson cards per view that keeps each card at `--card-min-width-md`, and pages a view at a time.
