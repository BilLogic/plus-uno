The Popover family is the set of floating layers that never block the page: Menu, Popup, Flag and Tooltip. They float with no scrim. An outside click or Escape closes them, and each takes a fixed width tier. The examples on this page follow the toolbar's Color mode switch.

### Purpose

Popover names the pattern and the token collection for four floating layers. A **Menu** lists actions. A **Popup** shows a small aside anchored to a control. A **Flag** reports what just happened. A **Tooltip** names a control in one line. In code, the Popup is the `Popover` component and the Flag is the `Toast` component.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

### When to use

- Use a Menu to choose one action from a short list.
- Use a Popup for a small aside anchored to a control, such as a definition, a preview or a short list. A Popup is never a form.
- Use a Flag to report an outcome that does not need a decision.
- Use a Tooltip for one line of help on a control.
- For a decision that must be made before going on, use a [Modal](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-modal--docs). It takes an [Overlay](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-overlay--docs) and a scrim, and the Popover family never does.
- For a task that needs the page in view, use a [Drawer](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-drawer--docs).

</div>

### Placement

Every layer stays at least 8 from the screen edge and flips when there is no room. It sits 4 from its trigger. Width hugs content inside its tier, and height hugs up to the room left, then scrolls.

### Rules

- Menu rows inset 4. Popup and Flag pad 16. Tooltip pads 4 and 8.
- Width hugs content inside a tier: Menu 160 to 320, Tooltip up to 240, Popup 320, 400 or 480, Flag 400.
- Stack Flags 16 apart, bottom-left.
- The Popup body is text or a short list, never a form. Anything with inputs, steps or a long scroll belongs in a modal.
- Give a Menu row a short label. When a row needs more, add a description line and let the row grow. Never put a sentence in the label.
- A Popover layer never brings a scrim and never blocks the page. An outside click or Escape closes it.
- Do not put required content in a Popup. Anything the person must read to finish the task belongs on the page.

</div>

### Accessibility

- A Popup's trigger is a control that announces the Popup: it carries <code>aria-haspopup</code> and an <code>aria-expanded</code> that tracks the open state.
- A Popup is not a dialog. Focus does not move into it, and anything focusable inside it is reached by continuing to Tab.
- An outside click and Escape close a Popup.
- A hover-only Tooltip leaves out the keyboard. Open it on hover and focus.
- A Flag is announced without taking focus. Success and info are polite, and warning and error interrupt a screen reader.
