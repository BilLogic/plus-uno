## When to use

Use `VennDiagram` when the question is about membership in more than one
group: students tutored in both Math and Reading, tutors certified in more than
one SMART competency, students who joined both the fall and spring cohorts.

Keep it to two or three sets; past that, the overlaps cannot all be drawn to
scale. For the sizes of the groups rather than their overlap, use `PieChart`
or `TreemapChart`. For membership across many groups, use `HeatmapChart`. For
links between individual items, use `NetworkGraph`.

## Data shape

`data` is an array of `{ sets, value, name }`. `sets` and `value` are required:
list each set alone, then every overlap you want drawn. `height` defaults to 400.

```jsx
<VennDiagram
    data={[
        { sets: ['Math'], value: 42, name: 'Math' },
        { sets: ['Reading'], value: 35, name: 'Reading' },
        { sets: ['Math', 'Reading'], value: 12, name: 'Math and Reading' },
    ]}
/>
```

A set's `value` is the whole circle, overlap included, not the "only" part.

## Color and accessibility

- **Color is assigned for you.** Each set's circle takes the next color in the categorical order, slot 1 first. Overlaps show where circles cross.
- **Area carries the values, roughly.** Readers see that an overlap is small or large, not its exact size. Print the counts in the names or the surrounding copy when they matter.
- **Name the regions.** Color alone does not say which circle is which; give every entry a `name` so the label and the tooltip carry it.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** The chart is a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type, per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation through the data. The chart takes no title of its own, so name it in the Section or Card header around it.
- **Contrast.** Chart colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens.

## States

`VennDiagram` draws what it is given and has no empty, loading or error state of its own. The caller chooses what to mount in its place:

- **Loading:** a `Skeleton` the height of the chart.
- **Empty:** a compact `EmptyState` saying what data belongs here.
- **Error:** a `SectionMessage` with `appearance="danger"` and a way to retry.

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={400} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Enrollment could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No enrollment yet" description="Overlaps appear once students enroll in more than one subject." />
    : <VennDiagram data={data} />}
```
