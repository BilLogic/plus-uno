<FoundationSection title="Overview">

Every color is a semantic token named for the job it does: page text, field edge, or primary button fill. Each token resolves per mode, so components adapt to light, dark and increased contrast automatically.

```css
color: var(--ds-text-danger);
background: var(--ds-background-danger-subtler);
```

Figma holds them in `color / semantics` (modes Light, Dark, Light IC and Dark IC); `color / primitives` stay hidden. See <DocsLink id="foundations-tokens-color">Tokens: Color</DocsLink> for the full collection.

<Canvas of={ColorsStories.Overview} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="How the tokens are built">

Primitives hold raw ramps and stay hidden; bind only semantic tokens. Names read from general to specific: property, role, emphasis, state. In Figma, parts join with slashes (`color/background/danger/bold/hovered`); in CSS, drop `color` and join with dashes behind `--ds-` (`--ds-background-danger-bold-hovered`).

<FoundationSection title="Modes">

PLUS ships four modes: light and dark, each at standard and increased contrast. `auto` follows OS `prefers-color-scheme` and `prefers-contrast`.

- **Dark mode** steps raised and overlay surfaces lighter as they rise, because shadows are subtle on dark grounds.
- **Increased contrast** pushes text to 7:1 and borders, icons and the focus ring to 4.5:1. Tints, surfaces and the Backdrop stay unchanged.
- **In code**, set `data-color-mode` (`light`, `dark`, `auto`) and `data-contrast-mode` (`no-preference`, `more`, `auto`) on `<html>`.
- **In Figma**, set the frame mode to Light, Dark, Light IC or Dark IC.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="States">

Hover and press step along the token's own ladder. Focus is always a ring, never a fill (<DocsLink id="foundations-visual-border">Border</DocsLink>). AI color tokens live on <DocsLink id="ai-ui-foundations-color">AI UI › Color</DocsLink>.

- **Rows, menu items and sidebar items** take `color/background/neutral/subtle/hovered` and `/pressed`.
- **Selected elements** take `color/background/selected` (`selected/hovered` and `/pressed` on interaction), paired with a second cue such as a check mark or border. Never use brand, link or focused tokens for selection.
- **Fields** take `color/background/input/hovered` with `color/border/input` on hover; on focus they keep their fill and add the focus ring.
- **Icons** have no hovered or pressed color tokens; show interaction with a subtle neutral background behind the icon.
- **Avatars and images** whose fill cannot change use `color/interaction/hovered` and `color/interaction/pressed` overlays.

```css
.my-element:hover {
  background-color: var(--ds-background-neutral-subtle-hovered);
}
.my-element:active {
  background-color: var(--ds-background-neutral-subtle-pressed);
}
.my-element.is-selected {
  background-color: var(--ds-background-selected);
}
```

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Partner pairs and contrast">

Every foreground token pairs with a ground that clears WCAG AA across all four modes: 4.5:1 for text (7:1 in IC) and 3:1 for icons, borders and the focus ring (4.5:1 in IC).

- **Bold fills** take `color/text/inverse` and `color/icon/inverse`, except bold warning which takes dark `color/text/warning/inverse`.
- **Status tints** take the role's text token, or its `-bolder` step on `-subtler` and `-subtle` grounds.
- **Surfaces** take `color/text/default`, `subtle` or `subtlest`.

<FoundationSubsection title="Categorical order">

Assign series in slot order without skipping. Group past 5–6 series into "Other".

<RefTable
    head={['Slot', 'Hue', 'Light', 'Dark', 'Light IC', 'Dark IC']}
    rows={[
        ['1 (brand)', 'lagoon', '600', '500', '850', '300'],

        ['6', 'purple', '700', '700', '900', '500'],

        ['8', 'blue', '800', '700', '1000', '500'],
    ]}
/>

- **Brand leads:** slot 1 matches `--ds-chart-brand` so a 1-series and 6-series chart share their lead color.
- **Neighbors alternate lightness:** adjacent slots alternate light and dark steps so series stay distinct in grayscale and under color-vision deficiency.
- **No mixing with status:** yellow and red are reserved for warning and danger; never mix categorical and status colors in one chart.
- **Separators and hover:** keep a gap or `--ds-border-inverse` seam between touching marks. Hovered marks take their `-hovered` token; fade unselected series with `--ds-opacity-disabled`.

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">

- Foreground/background partner pairs clear WCAG 2.1 AA in all four modes (4.5:1 text, 3:1 UI/icons/borders; 7:1 and 4.5:1 in IC).
- Never convey meaning by color alone: pair status colors with an icon or text, selection with a check mark or 2 px border, and chart series with labels, shapes or a table.
- Warning icons and borders use `color/icon/warning` and `color/border/warning` rather than the yellow fill so they clear 3:1 on light surfaces.
- See <DocsLink id="foundations-accessibility">Accessibility</DocsLink> for cross-cutting contrast and focus rules.

</FoundationSection>
