## When to use

Use `BulletChart` when each number has a goal: session completion rate against
the weekly target, attendance against the program goal, a SMART competency
score against its benchmark. Stack several metrics in one chart and the reader
scans down for the bars that fall short of their ticks.

For values with no target, use `BarChart`. For one headline value against a
target, shown as a dial, use `GaugeChart`. For progress toward a target over
time, use `LineChart`.

The scale is fixed at 0 to 100, so every metric must be a percentage or a score
out of 100.

## Data shape

`categories` is an array of row labels. `data` is an array of `{ y, target }`,
one per category in the same order. `height` defaults to 200; give each row
roughly 60 to 80 px.

```jsx
<BulletChart
    height={250}
    categories={['Session completion', 'Attendance', 'Reflection rate']}
    data={[
        { y: 78, target: 85 },
        { y: 92, target: 90 },
        { y: 64, target: 75 },
    ]}
/>
```

## Color and accessibility

- **Color is fixed.** Every bar takes `--ds-chart-brand`, the target takes `--ds-chart-gray-boldest`, and the bands take the danger, warning and success backgrounds. Nothing is assigned per row.
- **Position carries the value.** The bar's end and the tick's place on a shared axis say how far each metric is from its goal, without hue.
- **The bands say "poor, fair, good" by color alone.** Red and green bands are the pair most often confused, and the chart prints no band labels. Say what the bands mean in the surrounding text, and do not rely on them to flag a row.
- **The bands do not move.** They sit at 60 and 80 for every metric. When a metric's thresholds differ, the bands mislead; put that metric in its own chart or in `BarChart`.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** The chart is a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type and axes, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation through the data. The chart takes no title of its own, so name it in the Section or Card header around it.
- **Contrast.** Chart colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens.

## States

`BulletChart` draws what it is given and has no empty, loading or error state of its own. The caller chooses what to mount in its place:

- **Loading:** a `Skeleton` the height of the chart.
- **Empty:** a compact `EmptyState` saying what data belongs here.
- **Error:** a `SectionMessage` with `appearance="danger"` and a way to retry.

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={250} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Goals could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No goals set" description="Progress appears once a target is set." />
    : <BulletChart categories={categories} data={data} height={250} />}
```

- For values with no target to measure against, use `BarChart`.
- For one value against a target as a dial rather than a bar, use `GaugeChart`.
- For progress over time toward that target, use `LineChart`.
- For percentages against a full track with no target, use `SmartBarChart`.
