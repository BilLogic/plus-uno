## Usage

Use Search when a person knows what a record is called. Search finds by name; <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-finding-things-filtering--docs">Filtering</a> narrows by attribute, so the two keep separate controls and separate counts.

## Best practices

### Pick the mode by where the answer lands.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Active when the rows are in hand and few, focused when the person jumps to a record from the page they are on, basic when the search is heavy enough to wait for Enter. One mode per field.

### Name the scope in the placeholder.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

"Search students". The label is hidden, so the placeholder is what a person reads and what names the collapsed icon.

### Add a scope filter when the search covers more than one kind of record.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The filter sits before the field with "All" first and selected, and each scope carries its count. A search over one kind of record has no scope filter.

### Show the count in the results header, zero included.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

"2 of 6 students", "0 of 6 students". The count lives in the results header, in words, and updates as the rows narrow.

### Answer no matches in the results body.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

An <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-states-empty--docs">Empty state</a> fills the results body, names what was searched and offers a next action: "Reset filters". The field stays where it is.

### Let loading mirror empty.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

While results load, Skeleton bars take the results body, a Spinner takes the search icon's place and the count slot says what is happening. See <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-states-loading--docs">Loading</a>.

### Keep the bar still.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The field, the scope filter and the other bar controls hold their place in every state: typing, loading, no matches. Only the results area changes.

### Clear in two steps.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Esc empties the text and keeps the field. In focused, the first Esc closes the panel and keeps the text, and the next clears it. A collapsible field closes on the Esc after that and returns focus to the icon. "Clear search" empties the text and leaves the field open. Switching views keeps the search; "Reset filters" clears it.

### Place by context: compact in a bar, default in a page body.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The view bar holds controls of one height, so the field takes `spacing="compact"` there. A search in a page body takes the default spacing and stays open.

## Accessibility

- The hidden label still names the field: the collapsed icon and the field share the placeholder's words.
- The scope filter has its own name, "Search scope".
- In focused, the input is the combobox: focus stays in it, the highlighted row is its active descendant and the panel is a named listbox.
- The count in the results header is a polite live region, so "2 of 6 students" is announced as the rows narrow.
- Focus stays in the field when the results empty; it never jumps to the empty state.
- Loading is announced through the Spinner's status text.
