<FoundationSection title="Four principles">

Each principle as a moment in PLUS. Nearly everything a tutor does in a day is Productive. One moment in a term is Expressive.

Every Do and Don't below is the same scene with one rule broken, so the difference is the lesson. Each draws its paths from start to landing first, then plays; use the speed control to watch at ½× or ¼×.

<FoundationSubsection title="Always responsive">

Every press answers right away, and nothing waits for an animation to finish.

**Keep up, never hold up.**

The first visible answer to a press, hover or toggle is its color or press state. It starts in the same frame and is 90% done by about <SpringMs token="appearance.fast" />. A moving part, such as a Toggle's thumb, follows on `spatial.fast`. Any motion can be interrupted mid-way, switched to Reduced mode by the system setting, and paused if it runs longer than 5 s.

<FoundationSubsection title="Build understanding">

Motion shows how things work, so people learn a pattern once and know what to expect.

**Show how, not just what.**

Where it came from, where it went, and where you are now. Motion keeps the speed of the hand that started it, carries the weight of the thing that moves, and settles where it belongs.

<FoundationSubsection title="Lead with empathy">

Motion meets people where they are: it cheers a finished reflection and softens a failed save.

**Cheer the wins, cushion the misses.**

Expressive marks the end of something worth finishing, a few times a week at most. A blocked submit shakes once. A failed save never shakes: it eases in and stays until it is fixed.

<Canvas of={PrinciplesStories.LeadWithEmpathy} sourceState="none" />

<FoundationSubsection title="Cut the noise">

Only what explains the change moves, so the work stays in focus.

**One story at a time.**

How often something happens sets how loud its motion is, never to zero at page level. A change seen dozens of times a day moves quietly, usually a short fade or a flat slide. From the keyboard, controls and Popups open with no animation; layers and pages keep their motion. Reduced mode keeps one short fade, so a change is still noticed. Never use Expressive on a control, a field, or anything done dozens of times a day.

<FoundationSection title="Before you add motion">

1. What does it explain? Where something came from, where it went, or what is new. Can someone point to where the thing went?
2. How often will someone see it? Dozens a day means quiet. If it happens many times a day, make it quieter.
3. Does it answer at once, and can it be interrupted? Does anything wait for an animation to finish? Can someone stop it?
4. Does the weight fit the object and the input?
5. Does it fit how the person feels right now? Cheer a win once. Keep a failed save calm.
6. Does every moving part explain the change? Cut the rest, or move it as one group.

If the answer to the first question is "nothing", leave it out. A new kind of motion is a question for the design team.

</FoundationSection>
