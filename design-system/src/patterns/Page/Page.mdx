## Anatomy

A record page, one level down, with everything the header can carry. Each marker sits in the gutter beside its part.

<Story of={PageStories.AnatomyRecord} inline />

1. **Breadcrumb** (optional). The record's parents on their own row, above the title.
2. **Title.** The page's one h1, with its StatusTag after the text when the page is one record.
3. **Description** (optional). One line under the title.
4. **Page actions** (optional). At the end of the title row, about the page only.
5. **Page tabs** (optional). At the bottom of the header; they switch the whole page.
6. **Banner** (optional). Under the header, one at a time.
7. **Content.** Sections on the grid, under the header.

## Heading outline

Headings step down one size per nesting level, and their tags follow the nesting. The page's one h1 is Heading large (24), a section's h2 is Heading medium (20), and a subsection's h3 is Heading small (16).

The outline keeps a page's headings in one order, so a reader and assistive technology see the same structure the sizes show. It applies across the Page, Section, Card, Modal, Popup and Flag patterns.

### Page, Section and Subsection

<DocsCanvasShell attachSourceBelow description={<>The h1 is 24, the section's h2 is 20 and the subsection's h3 is 16.</>}>
    <DsCanvasQuiet of={HeadingOutlineStories.PageSectionSubsection} />
</DocsCanvasShell>

Use one h1 per page. Each Section opens with an h2, and a subsection inside it with an h3. Do not skip a level to get a different size: the size follows from the level.

### Container titles

Container titles are fixed whatever level they sit at.

<DocsCanvasShell description={<>A card's tag is one level below its section; its title and description sizes are on <DocsLink id="templates-section-card">Card</DocsLink>.</>}>
    <DsCanvasQuiet of={HeadingOutlineStories.CardTitles} />
</DocsCanvasShell>

<DocsCanvasShell description={<>A modal's title is 20. It is the dialog's own h1 and names it.</>}>
    <DsCanvasQuiet of={HeadingOutlineStories.ModalTitle} />
</DocsCanvasShell>

<DocsCanvasShell description={<>A popup's and a flag's titles are 14.</>}>
    <DsCanvasQuiet of={HeadingOutlineStories.PopupAndFlagTitles} />
</DocsCanvasShell>

### Group labels

<DocsCanvasShell description={<>Group labels are 12 semibold and are not headings.</>}>
    <DsCanvasQuiet of={HeadingOutlineStories.GroupLabels} />
</DocsCanvasShell>

A menu's label names a group, and a table's labels are column headers. Neither joins the outline.

### Heading outline accessibility

- **Tags follow nesting.** Heading levels are real `h1` to `h3` elements, not styled text, so screen reader users can move by heading.
- **A modal names itself.** Its title is the dialog's accessible name.

## Usage

Use Page for the content of every default Frame. It carries the page header, the one h1, and the Sections under it. The default type caps the grid at its content width; full width is for a page that fills it.

</div>

## Alternatives

- For one task that takes the whole window, use `Frame` in its focused type.
- For a region of the page with its own heading, use `PatternSection`.
- For one object with an agreed shape, use `PatternCard` inside a Section.

## Best practices

### Make the page header the page's one h1.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

A Section never stands in for it. Sections inside open at h2.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<HeaderIsTheH1 />}
    doCaption="The page header is the one h1; the Section under it opens at h2."
    dontExample={<SectionsAsTitles />}
    dontCaption="Sections standing in for the page title: two page-title-sized headings and no page header."
/>

### Show the breadcrumb by the Navigation pattern.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

When it shows and what it holds: the <DocsLink id="patterns-navigation">Navigation</DocsLink> pattern.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<AreaPageTitleAlone />}
    doCaption="An area page: the title alone."
    dontExample={<BreadcrumbOnAreaPage />}
    dontCaption="A breadcrumb on a top-level area page, repeating the Sidebar's place."
/>

### Place page actions and the add action by the Navigation pattern.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Page actions sit at the end of the title row and are about the page only. Where an add action goes: the <DocsLink id="patterns-navigation">Navigation</DocsLink> pattern.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<AddInViewBar />}
    doCaption={'"Add" ends the collection\'s view bar; the header carries only page actions.'}
    dontExample={<AddTwice />}
    dontCaption="The same Primary twice: in the page header and in the Section's view bar."
/>

### Keep the description to one line.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Anything longer moves into the first Section.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<OneLineDescription />}
    doCaption="One detail line under the title."
    dontExample={<ParagraphUnderTitle />}
    dontCaption="A paragraph under the title pushes the first Section down."
/>

### Keep one tab system per page.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The rule and its reasons: the <DocsLink id="patterns-navigation">Navigation</DocsLink> pattern. Page tabs name views of one subject; the Section's views become one labeled Select.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<OneTabSystem />}
    doCaption="Under page tabs the Section's views are one labeled Select at the start of its bar."
    dontExample={<TwoTabSystems />}
    dontCaption="Pill views under page tabs: two tab systems on one page."
/>

### Shrink the scrolled header to one sticky row.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The breadcrumb with the title at its end, then the page actions; page tabs stick under it. The Section view bar scrolls away. This state is planned.

### Show one banner at a time, scrolling with the content.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Which tier a message belongs to: the <DocsLink id="patterns-notifications">Notifications</DocsLink> pattern.

## Accessibility

- The Page is the `main` landmark. Its header's title is the one h1; Sections open at h2.
- The breadcrumb is a `nav` named "Breadcrumb".
- Page tabs are a tablist; the selected tab names its panel. Views swap in place and are announced.
- The StatusTag is text, never color alone.
- A title that truncates keeps its full text in a Tooltip on hover and focus.
- Text on a Page uses `--ds-text` at every level, in every color mode.
