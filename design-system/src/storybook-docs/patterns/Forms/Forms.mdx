## Usage

Use Forms when a page asks for input that is saved together: adding a student, logging a session, a reflection, profile settings. Ask only what is needed, group fields under a `Fieldset` legend of two or three words, keep one column except for pairs that belong together (first and last name, date and time), and reveal a conditional field under the answer that needs it rather than adding a separate one.

## Alternatives

- For one value on a record page, use `InlineEdit`; it saves on its own.
- For a setting that applies at once, use `Toggle`; it never waits for Save.
- For a filter, follow <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-filtering--docs">Filtering</a>: filters are not a form.
- For a decision with no fields, use <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/templates-modal-modal--docs">Modal</a> · Active.

## Best practices

### Validate a field when it loses focus and clear the error once the rule is met.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

The error shows on the field, says what to fix, and goes away as soon as the value passes. Nothing is checked while typing. A warning accepts the value; a field is invalid only when the form cannot go on.

### Keep the submit button enabled and let it show what is missing.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

A disabled submit gives no direction and leaves the tab order. Pressing it marks every missing field and moves focus to the first; the body scrolls to it. The button disables only while sending, when it swaps its label for a spinner in place and keeps its width.

### Keep a blocked submit in the same Modal.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

Mark the field, say what to fix inline, scroll the body to it, and shake once <PlannedTag />. Never a second dialog for the error. Reduced motion drops the shake; the color, icon, message and focus move carry it. A server failure: <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-states-errors--docs">Errors</a>.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<ErrorOnField />}
    doCaption="The message under the field, in the same Modal."
    dontExample={<ErrorInSecondDialog />}
    dontCaption="A second dialog over the first, with the field left unexplained."
/>

### Mark required fields with an asterisk and explain it once.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

The legend says what the asterisk means. Optional fields are marked "(optional)" only in a form where most fields are required, so one pattern holds across the product. `isRequired` sets the mark and `aria-required`.

### Put help in the caption, never in a tooltip.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

A caption stays after the person types and is read by a screen reader with the field. A tooltip never carries the only copy of anything. An explanation most people will never need opens from an `InlineMessage` beside the field.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<HelpInCaption />}
    doCaption="Help in the caption, where it stays after typing."
    dontExample={<HelpInTooltip />}
    dontCaption="Help only in a tooltip: gone on touch, gone after typing."
/>

### Ask before leaving with unsaved edits.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

Close, Cancel and navigating away raise a 4-col Modal: Back returns without losing anything typed; Discard is Danger. A form already in a Modal asks in that Modal's confirm view, so two layers never become three. A form with no edits leaves at once. In a focused task, Close and Cancel play the opening backwards once the person confirms.

### Prefer a bound input to a free one.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Up to five choices: `RadioGroup` or checkboxes, visible at once. Six or more: `Select`. A date: `DatePicker`. A number: min and max. A radio group starts unanswered and never falls back to its first option. A `Toggle` applies at once, so anything that waits for Save is a `Checkbox`.

### Give the form a title from its host and a legend for each group.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The form's title is the page, Panel or Modal heading. Groups take a `Fieldset` legend, two or three words, a noun phrase. Labels are sentence case, one to three words, naming the field rather than what to do.

### Set autofill on every field the browser can fill.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

`autoComplete` on name, email, phone and address fields. Prefill what is already known, such as the student picked from a row.

## Accessibility

- Every input has a visible label. A group is a `fieldset` with a `legend`; its one error is announced once.
- A field error is announced and sets `aria-invalid`; the submit moves focus to the first invalid field. A server message is announced when it appears.
- A required field sets `aria-required`; the asterisk is explained in the legend and never carries the meaning alone.
- A sending button keeps its name and sets `aria-busy`; Cancel stays reachable.
- A dialog opens only from a person's action. Focus goes to the first input and stays trapped until it closes.
