<FoundationSection title="The five layers">

<FoundationSubsection title="Frame">

<LayerDetailRow variant="frame">

The app shell behind the page: the ground and a docked Sidebar or Panel.

**Usage and styling**

- **Flat:** no shadow.
- **Docked chrome shares this ground;** floating chrome moves to Non-blocking overlay.

Stacking: base

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Page">

<LayerDetailRow variant="page">

Where work sits: Sections, Tables, Lists, Forms, Cards. Flat as a layer; three surfaces inside it.

**Usage and styling**

- **No shadow on the Page itself.**
- **Meets the Frame** at a 1px hairline (<C>--ds-border</C>).

Stacking: <C>--layer-card</C>

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Navigation">

<LayerDetailRow variant="navigation">

Bars and headers that stay put while the page scrolls under them.

**Usage and styling**

- **Flat at rest:** a hairline only before scroll.
- **Takes <C>--ds-shadow-overflow</C>** only while content passes under it.

Stacking: <C>--layer-navigation</C>

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Non-blocking overlay">

<LayerDetailRow variant="non-blocking-overlay">

Surfaces that float while the page stays live: Popups, Menus, Select lists, Flag, floating Sidebar and Panel, Spotlight, a dragged Card.

**Usage and styling**

- **No Blanket:** the page stays usable.
- **Dismisses** on outside click, Escape or timer.

Stacking: <C>--layer-popup</C> <C>--layer-flag</C> <C>--layer-spotlight</C>

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Blocking overlay">

<LayerDetailRow variant="blocking-overlay">

The Modal, over the Blanket. Blocks the page until you answer.

**Usage and styling**

- **Always on the Blanket;** traps focus and inert-locks the page.
- **One at a time;** a Flag may sit above it.

Stacking: <C>--layer-blanket</C> <C>--layer-modal</C>

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Not a layer">

A **Tooltip** labels a control: no shadow, <C>--layer-tooltip</C>.

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Within the page">

The Page has three surfaces: Default, Sunken and Raised.

<Canvas of={ElevationStories.WithinThePage} sourceState="none" />

<FoundationSubsection title="Default">

<LayerDetailRow variant="surface-default">

Most of the page: a Section and its side, tables, lists, forms; rows are hairlines.

**Usage and styling**

- **A Section takes no surface:** no fill, no padding, no border.

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Sunken">

<LayerDetailRow variant="surface-sunken">

A backdrop that groups static content, such as a subtle Section.

**Usage and styling**

- **Only on the Default surface;** one step only.
- **Flat:** no shadow, no border.

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Raised">

<LayerDetailRow variant="surface-raised">

The card surface on the page, and a grabbed control.

**Usage and styling**

- **One focal card per view at rest.**
- **Always the pair:** raised surface with raised shadow.
- **Clickable hover** lifts to <C>--ds-shadow-overlay</C>; **dragged,** it takes the overlay pair.

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="By template">

<Canvas of={ElevationStories.ByTemplate} sourceState="none" />

How a Section, its side and its detail sit on the page is in <DocsLink id="templates-section">Templates › Section</DocsLink>. Elevation sets only their surface: by default a Section takes none.

</FoundationSubsection>
