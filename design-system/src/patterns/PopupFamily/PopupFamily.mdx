<Title />
The Popups pattern is the set of floating layers that never block the page: Menu, Popup, Flag and Tooltip. They float with no blanket. A Menu and a Popup close on an outside click or Escape. A Flag closes on its timer or its Close button, and a Tooltip closes when hover or focus leaves its trigger. Each takes a fixed width tier. The examples on this page follow the toolbar's Color mode switch.

## Usage

The four layers are built from existing components, and each component's page holds its own props and states.

<DocsTable>
<thead>
<tr><th>Layer</th><th>Component</th><th>When to use</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Menu</td><td><code>DropdownMenu</code></td><td>To choose one action from a short list.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Popup</td><td><code>Popup</code></td><td>A small aside anchored to a control, such as a definition, a preview or a short list.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Flag</td><td><code>Flag</code>, placed by <code>FlagGroup</code></td><td>To report an outcome that does not need a decision.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Tooltip</td><td><code>Tooltip</code></td><td>One line of help on a control.</td></tr>
</tbody>

For a decision that must be made before going on, use a [Modal](?path=/docs/templates-overlays-modal--docs), which takes a Blanket. For a task that needs the page in view, use a [Drawer](?path=/docs/templates-overlays-panel--docs).

### Placement

Every layer stays at least 8 from the screen edge and flips when there is no room. It sits 4 from its trigger. Width hugs content inside its tier, and height hugs up to the room left, then scrolls. A Menu hugs its rows up to 320 wide and 320 tall, then scrolls.

## Parts

1. **Menu.** A floating box of Menu rows (`Section`, `ButtonItem`, `LinkItem`, `CustomItem`), built on Base UI Menu. Its anatomy lives on the [Dropdown menu](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-inputs-dropdown-menu--docs) page.
2. **Popup.** A floating box padded 16, with a title in Heading/xsmall and a body in Body/default.
3. **Flag.** A 16 icon in the appearance's icon color, a title in Heading/xsmall, a description in Body/default, optional actions and a 32 Close button.
4. **Tooltip.** One line of text, padded 4 above and below and 8 at the sides, rounded 6.

### States

Each layer is open or closed. Normal, information and success Flags close after 8 seconds; warning and error stay until dismissed. A Menu row has rest, hover and selected states, and selected uses neutral ink on the selected background; the full set is on the Dropdown menu page.

## Best practices

When a popup, a menu row and a tooltip help, and when they get in the way.

### A popup holds text or a short list.

Anything with inputs, steps or a long scroll belongs in a Modal or a Drawer. Never put content the person must read to finish the task in a popup; it belongs on the page.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<PopupShortList />}
    doCaption="Three short rows answer the question without leaving the page."
    dontExample={<PopupForm />}
    dontCaption="A form in a popup asks the person to work inside a floating box that closes on a stray click. Use a modal."
/>

### Menu rows: a short label, then a description.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<MenuShortLabels />}
    doCaption="Short labels, with a description only on the rows that need one."
    dontExample={<MenuSentenceLabel />}

/>

### A tooltip names a control in a few words.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<TooltipShort />}

    dontCaption="A tooltip that wraps into a paragraph hides what the person needed to read. Use a popup."

### Never bring a blanket.

A Popups-family layer never takes a blanket and never blocks the page.

## Accessibility

- A Popup's trigger announces it: it carries `aria-haspopup` and an `aria-expanded` that tracks the open state.
- A Popup is not a dialog. Focus does not move into it, and anything focusable inside it is reached by continuing to Tab.
- An outside click and Escape close a Popup and a Menu.
- A hover-only Tooltip leaves out the keyboard. Open it on hover and focus.
- A Flag is announced without taking focus. Normal, information and success are polite, and warning and error interrupt a screen reader.
