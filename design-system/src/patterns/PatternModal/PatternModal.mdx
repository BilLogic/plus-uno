<Title />
The Modal interrupts the page for one task. It is always centered on the screen inside an Overlay, and it carries a title, a body and a footer. Its width is a step of the screen grid, its height hugs or fills, and it sits on the overlay surface. The examples on this page follow the toolbar's Color mode switch.

### Purpose

A Modal asks for one decision or one short task before the person goes on. It blocks the page, so keep it brief and give it a clear way out.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

### When to use

- Use a Modal when one task blocks the page, such as moving a session or confirming a delete.
- For a task that needs the page in view, use a [Drawer](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-drawer--docs).
- For an aside that needs no decision, use a Popup from the [Popover family](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-popover-family--docs). A Modal blocks the page and a Popup does not.
- For content that belongs in the page flow, use a [Card](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-card--docs) or a [Section](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-section--docs).
- To tell someone something without interrupting them, use a Flag or an Alert.
- For one line of help on a control, use a Tooltip.

</div>

### Anatomy

<DocsCanvasShell attachSourceBelow description={<>A <code>sm</code>, <code>hug</code> Modal in its Overlay at the 1024 stand-in screen. <strong>1</strong> Header: the title and the Close button. <strong>2</strong> Body: a slot that scrolls past the cap. <strong>3</strong> Footer: a slot for actions, right-aligned, with the primary last. <strong>4</strong> Overlay: the scrim, pads and screen grid around it.</>}>

The Modal pads 16 on every side and rounds 12 at every width. The header, body and footer stack 16 apart, and footer buttons sit 8 apart. The title is Heading/medium 20/24 and the body is Body/default 14/20. The Close button is 32 with a 16 icon. The surface is the overlay pair: `--ds-surface-overlay` with `--ds-shadow-overlay`, the level every floating layer takes.

### Header with tabs

<DocsCanvasShell description={<><code>tabs</code> puts a tab group under the title, 8 below it. The tabs stay put in the header and only the body scrolls, so they never leave the screen. The selected tab keeps its full-width underline. Each tab panel is named by its tab.</>}>
    <DsCanvasQuiet of={PatternModalStories.Tabs} />
</DocsCanvasShell>

- Use tabs for two to five views of one thing that people open in any order.
- When the views must be done in order, or a later view depends on an earlier answer, split the flow into steps instead. Show one view at a time, put Back and Next in the footer, and write the step in the title or the body, such as "Step 2 of 3".
- Keep each label to one or two words on one line. At width <code>sm</code> that is about three tabs. Never wrap or scroll the strip, and never nest a second tab group in the body.
- One footer serves every tab. Save saves them all, and switching tabs keeps what was typed.
- Prefer height <code>fill</code> when the views differ in length, so switching tabs never resizes the Modal.

### Rules

- Always center the Modal on the screen, inside an [Overlay](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-overlay--docs). A Modal has no backdrop of its own.
- Use a width step: 384, 507 or 629 at 768, 512, 677 or 843 at 1024, and 640, 848 or 1056 at 1440.
- Pad 16 on every side, and round 12 for every size.
- Put the buttons in the footer, end-aligned, with the primary last. Never place them in the body, which scrolls while the footer stays put.
- Fill the slots. Do not detach the Modal to move things around.
- Space only inside the slot content, with the Modal stack tokens. Never add padding around the header or footer.
- A search or command dialog pins its top edge and grows downward.

</div>

### Accessibility

- The title is an h1 inside the dialog and names it through `aria-labelledby`, so the dialog reads as its own page. A section inside the Modal opens at h2.
- The Modal has the role dialog. A title-less Modal needs its own accessible name.
- A body that scrolls is a tab stop, so a keyboard can reach it.
- With tabs, the tab group is a tablist. Left and Right move between tabs, Home and End jump to the ends, and Tab moves from the strip into the panel. The panel is named by its tab.
- Opening a Modal closes a floating sidebar in a Surface container.
- At most two overlays stack. A confirm of width <code>sm</code> raised from a Modal is the second, and a third is not allowed.
- The Close button is 32 and its accessible name is "Close".
