<FoundationSection title="The five layers">

<FoundationSubsection title="Layer 0 · Frame">

<LayerDetailRow variant="frame">

The ground the app sits on, and the docked Sidebar on it.

#### Usage & styling

- **Flat.** Never a shadow, never floats.
- **Docked Sidebar** shares the Frame; floating, it moves to Layer 3.

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Layer 1 · Page">

<LayerDetailRow variant="page">

The page and everything in its flow. What sits on it is broken down below, in Within the page.

#### Usage & styling

- **Flat by default.** Set off from the Frame by tone alone.

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Layer 2 · Navigation">

<LayerDetailRow variant="navigation">

Edges that stay put while content scrolls under them: a sticky table header and column.

#### Usage & styling

- **Border first;** the overflow shadow appears only once content scrolls beneath.

#### Stacking

<C>--layer-card</C>

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Layer 3 · Non-blocking overlay">

<LayerDetailRow variant="non-blocking-overlay">

Floats while the page stays usable: the floating Sidebar and Panel, Popup types, Inline edit, Flag, Spotlight, a dragged Card.

#### Usage & styling

- **Overlay pair,** no border.
- **Dark mode:** steps lighter.

#### Stacking

<C>--layer-navigation</C> · <C>--layer-popup</C> · <C>--layer-flag</C> · <C>--layer-spotlight</C>

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Layer 4 · Blocking overlay">

<LayerDetailRow variant="blocking-overlay">

The Modal, and nothing else.

#### Usage & styling

- **Always on the Blanket;** nothing else dims the page.
- **One at a time;** a Flag may sit above it.

#### Stacking

<C>--layer-blanket</C> · <C>--layer-modal</C>

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Not a layer">

A **Tooltip** labels a control: no shadow, <C>--layer-tooltip</C>.

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Within the page">

Layer 1 has three surfaces: Default, Sunken and Raised.

<Canvas of={ElevationStories.WithinThePage} sourceState="none" />

<FoundationSubsection title="Default">

<LayerDetailRow variant="surface-default">

Most of the page: a Section and its side, tables, lists, forms; rows are hairlines. A Default card is this surface with a border.

#### Usage & styling

- **A Section takes no surface:** no fill, no padding, no border.
- **A Default card** takes <C>--ds-border</C>: on the Frame, on Sunken or colored areas, or when dense with its own rows.

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Sunken">

<LayerDetailRow variant="surface-sunken">

A backdrop that groups static content: a Card (the default surface), the empty box, a clickable card at rest.

#### Usage & styling

- **Only on the Default surface;** one step only, never a Card inside a Card.
- **Flat:** no shadow, no border.

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Raised">

<LayerDetailRow variant="surface-raised">

The one card a view is about, a clickable card on hover, a grabbed control.

#### Usage & styling

- **One per view at rest.**
- **Always the pair:** raised surface with raised shadow.
- **Dragged,** it takes the overlay pair.

</LayerDetailRow>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="By template">

<Canvas of={ElevationStories.ByTemplate} sourceState="none" />

How a Section, its side and its detail sit on the page is in <DocsLink id="templates-section">Templates › Section</DocsLink>. Elevation sets only their surface: a Section takes none.

</FoundationSubsection>
