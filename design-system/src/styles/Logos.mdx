<FoundationSection title="Overview">

Use it to mark PLUS itself in the tutor and student apps: the sidebar and navbar header, sign-in, email headers and the loading screen. Never use it as decoration. Its colors are brand artwork rather than theme tokens, so it looks the same on every surface.

```jsx
import { Logo } from '@/components';
```

The Logo component set is documented on the Logo page under Images and icons.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="One logo, two parts">

PLUS has one logo. In product, use the Logo component; do not redraw or export the mark by hand.

<RefTable
    head={['Part', 'What it is', 'When it shows']}
    rows={[
        ['Logomark', 'The PLUS plus-sign tile, drawn in the chosen color option.', 'Always.'],
        ['Wordmark', '"PLUS", set beside the logomark.', 'When the Logo’s wordmark is on.'],
    ]}
/>

<FoundationExample>
<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexWrap: 'wrap', alignItems: 'center', gap: 'var(--section-stack-lg)' }}>
    <Mark color="colored" size={132} />
    <Mark color="colored" size={132} text />
</div>
</FoundationExample>

Use the logomark on its own where the PLUS name already appears nearby or space is tight, and pair it with descriptive text where you can. Use the logomark with the wordmark where nothing else names PLUS.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Logomark shape and size">

The Logo comes in fixed sizes and the logomark has a pre-defined shape. Do not alter either.

- Sizes are fixed steps: 60, 96, 132, 192 and 224, the frame of the logomark. Pick a step; never scale an instance by hand.
- Use the default shape and corner radius of the logomark.
- Never nest the logomark inside an additional container, and leave breathing room around it.

<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexWrap: 'wrap', alignItems: 'flex-end', gap: 'var(--section-stack-lg)' }}>
    {[60, 96, 132, 192, 224].map((s) => <Mark key={s} size={s} />)}
</div>
</FoundationExample>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Color options">

Logos are available in three color options to suit different grounds. Choose by the ground the logo sits on.

<div style={{ display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: 'repeat(auto-fit, minmax(var(--card-min-width-sm), 1fr))', gap: 'var(--grid-gutter)' }}>
    <Ground token="--ds-surface" caption="Colored on a light surface."><Mark color="colored" size={132} text /></Ground>
    <Ground token="--ds-background-neutral-bold" caption="Filled on a bold or dark ground."><Mark color="filled" size={132} text /></Ground>
    <Ground token="--ds-background-accent-blue-bolder" caption="Outlined on a photo or busy ground."><Mark color="outlined" size={132} text /></Ground>
</div>

<RefTable
    head={['Color option', 'Ground', 'When to use it']}
    rows={[
        ['Colored', 'Light surfaces', 'The default. The brand artwork on a light surface.'],
        ['Filled', 'Any bold or dark ground', 'A white block logomark. Use where the logomark must read as a solid shape on a bold or dark ground.'],
        ['Outlined', 'Photos or busy grounds', 'A white outline logomark. Use where a solid block would hide or compete with the image.'],
    ]}
/>

Filled and outlined are drawn in white, so neither works on a light ground.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Logo clearance">

Surround the logo with clear space free of type, graphics and other elements that cause visual clutter.

<RefTable
    head={['Rule', 'Value', 'Note']}
    rows={[
        ['Clear space', 'Half the logomark’s height', 'On every side, measured from the edge of the logomark, not the edge of the Logo frame.'],
        ['Minimum size', '60', 'The lowest step. Do not use the logo below that, and never scale an instance by hand.'],
    ]}
/>

Every PLUS logo has the logomark, so clearance is measured from the logomark. Align a logo to its neighbors by the logomark's edge and center it on the same line as the text beside it.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Do and don't">

<FoundationSubsection title="Use approved colors">

Use the color options as drawn, on the ground each belongs on.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The colored logomark on a light ground."><Mark color="colored" size={132} /></Do>
<Dont caption="An unapproved color combination."><span style={{ display: 'inline-block', filter: 'hue-rotate(160deg) saturate(2)' }}><Mark color="colored" size={132} /></span></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Keep the logomark's shape and proportions">

Pick a size step. Do not stretch, crop or rotate the logomark.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="A size step, wordmark included."><Mark color="colored" size={132} text /></Do>
<Dont caption="A stretched logomark."><span style={{ display: 'inline-block', transform: 'scaleX(1.8)', transformOrigin: 'left center', marginInlineEnd: 'var(--card-pad-spacious)' }}><Mark color="colored" size={132} /></span></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Give the logomark breathing room">

Do not add unnecessary containers around the logomark.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The logomark on its own, with space around it."><Mark color="colored" size={132} /></Do>
<Dont caption="The logomark nested in an extra container."><span style={{ display: 'inline-block', padding: 'var(--pad-x-sm)', borderRadius: 'var(--radius-full)', boxShadow: 'inset 0 0 0 var(--border-default) var(--ds-border-bold)' }}><Mark color="colored" size={132} /></span></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Keep the artwork clean">

Do not add effects to the logo.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The logomark as drawn."><Mark color="colored" size={132} /></Do>
<Dont caption="A drop shadow added to the logomark."><span style={{ display: 'inline-block', filter: 'drop-shadow(0 4px 4px var(--ds-shadow-overlay))' }}><Mark color="colored" size={132} /></span></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

- Do not place a colored or filled logo on a complex background, and do not edit the artwork. Use the outlined option on photos and busy grounds.
- Do not rebuild the logomark from shapes or type; use the Logo component.
- One Logo per view. Partner and tool logos go in a Tile, never in the Logo set.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">

- Where the logo is the only thing naming PLUS, give it the alt text "PLUS".
- Beside a visible PLUS title the logomark is decorative: hide it so the name is not read twice.
- A logo that links home is a link: name it for where it goes, give it keyboard focus and the focus ring.
- Choose the color option by ground so the logomark keeps its contrast.

</FoundationSection>
