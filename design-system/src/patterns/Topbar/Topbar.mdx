### Anatomy

- **Row** — `--layout-height-topbar` 32. It has no fill of its own; the frame's `--ds-surface-sunken` shows through.
- **Start** — padded `--surface-container-pad` 8 on the left, so the toggle sits at the container pad. Docked, it is `--layout-width-sidebar` 240 wide; otherwise it hugs the toggle and pads `--element-inline-lg` 12 before the breadcrumb.
- **Toggle** — a primary tonal Button at the 32 size, icon only.
- **Middle** — a Breadcrumb that fills what is left. It stays on one line, and its crumbs end in an ellipsis rather than wrap.
- **End** — Help Center (a secondary text Button with `circle-question`) and the UserAvatar at `size="md"`, `--element-inline-lg` 12 apart and 12 after the breadcrumb.

### The toggle

- It shows `angles-left` while the sidebar is open, docked or floating, and `bars` while it is closed.
- Its name says what a press does: "Close sidebar" while docked, "Open sidebar" otherwise. `aria-expanded` says whether the sidebar is showing.
- Inside a Frame, the frame owns the sidebar state and hands it to the topbar, so there is one toggle. Standalone, pass `sidebar`, `docked` and `onToggle`; without `onToggle` there is no toggle.
