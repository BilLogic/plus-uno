### How the page is composed

Four flat Sections are the content grid's children, one per row, so the reading order is the visual order at every width.

- **Page header**: a page-level Section, the h1 with a one-line subtitle and the sign-up actions (under the title below 1024), and the badge as a slim callout row.
- **Next session**: a Section with a tabs header; the tab's content sits on the plain surface.
- **Your impact**: a Section with the pager in its header, a strip of three stats split by dividers (stacked below 1024), then the **Skills** subsection with its tabs and the chart on the surface.
- **Personalized recommendations**: a Section with the pager in its header and the page's only cards, the lessons: flat, outlined, and lifted on hover. The strip shows the most of 4 · 3 · 2 · 1 per view that keeps each card at `--card-min-width-md`: 2 at 768 and 1024, 3 at 1440.
