## Usage

Use `Footer` once, at the bottom of a Page, for the legal and version line. It is a quiet footnote, not navigation: do not put actions or a link list in it.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Parts

1. **Version.** The product version, first.
2. **Copyright.** The copyright line.
3. **Terms link.** The only control in the footer.

## Best practices

Use the footer once, at the bottom of a Page, for the legal and version line.

### One line of copyright.

The footer is a single quiet line; long legal text belongs on its own page.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Footer />}
    doCaption="Short copyright line."
    dontExample={<Footer copyright="© 2026 PLUS Tutoring Inc. All rights reserved. Unauthorized reproduction of any material on this site is prohibited without prior written consent." />}
    dontCaption="A legal paragraph that crowds the bar."
/>

### Name the link for where it goes.

The terms link is read out of context, so its label has to stand alone.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Footer />}
    doCaption="“Terms of Use” names where the link goes."
    dontExample={<Footer termsText="Click here" />}
    dontCaption="“Click here” says nothing out of context."
/>

### Version is a short tag.

The version reads like a build stamp, not a sentence.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Footer version="v5.2.0" />}
    doCaption="A short tag: v5.2.0."
    dontExample={<Footer version="Version two point four point zero, stable channel build 4812" />}
    dontCaption="A sentence about the build."
/>

### Set `version` and `termsUrl` from the app, not the defaults.

The defaults are placeholders. Pass the running version and the real terms address.

```jsx
<Footer version={appVersion} termsUrl="/terms" />
```

### Text and color come from tokens.

The line uses the small body style and `--ds-text`; the terms link uses `--ds-link`. Pass `style` only to change spacing.

## Accessibility

- **A landmark.** It renders a `<footer>`, which assistive technology announces as the page's content information.
- **A real link.** The terms text is an `<a>` with `termsUrl` as its `href`, so it is keyboard focusable and announced as a link.

</div>

## Related

- For the page's top edge, use `PageHeader`.
- For legal text inside a form or card, use body text, not `Footer`.
