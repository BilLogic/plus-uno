We write for many people. Who we are stays the same. How we say it fits who is reading.

<FoundationSection title="Voice">

Our voice is helpful, honest, warm and clear.

<RefTable
    head={['Word', 'The idea', 'What it asks of the writer']}
    rows={[
        [<VoiceTag word="helpful" />, 'The purpose: productivity', 'Point to the next step. Tell people what is coming, then show them how to get there.'],
        [<VoiceTag word="honest" />, 'The truth: learning', 'Say what happened. Give feedback straight, with the reason. Own our mistakes.'],
        [<VoiceTag word="warm" />, 'The feeling: uplift', 'Write the way a colleague talks. Notice the effort. Skip the jargon.'],
        [<VoiceTag word="clear" />, 'The words: simplicity', 'Keep it short. Use everyday words. Lead with the verb. One idea per sentence.'],
    ]}
/>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="When to dial">

The four words never change. How hard we lean on each one does.

<RefTable
    head={['Word', 'Lean in', 'Pull back']}
    rows={[
        [<VoiceTag word="helpful" />, 'A tutor is about to start a session.', 'The reader is browsing, not doing.'],
        [<VoiceTag word="honest" />, 'Something failed, or a student is stuck.', 'Never. Soften how you say it, not what you say.'],
        [<VoiceTag word="warm" />, 'A student did well. A tutor showed up.', 'Money, policy, or an error.'],
        [<VoiceTag word="clear" />, 'A live session, a phone, a hurry.', 'Never. Clear is always on.'],
    ]}

<FoundationSubsection title="Warm, pulled back">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="warm" /></RuleHead>

When money is late, warm means calm and specific. The date is the kindest part.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Kind, because it answers “when?”."><Say>Your October payout is delayed. It arrives by Nov 20.</Say></Do>
<Dont caption="Polite, but the tutor still has to ask when."><Say>We’re sorry, your October payout is delayed.</Say></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Tone">
