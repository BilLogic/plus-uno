<FoundationSection title="What AI UI is">

AI UI marks AI-generated work, such as session summaries, drafts and suggested focus areas, so people can distinguish it from human work. It extends PLUS tokens, type and components.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Guidelines">

- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-ai-interaction-guidelines">AI interaction guidelines</DocsLink>: the rules every AI surface follows, for tutors and students alike.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Foundations">

- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-foundations-color">Color</DocsLink>: the AI action ground and the generative border's colors.
- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-foundations-motion">Motion</DocsLink>: generating, streaming and reduced motion.
- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-foundations-elevation">Elevation</DocsLink> and <DocsLink id="ai-ui-foundations-voice-and-tone">Voice and tone</DocsLink>: not yet defined.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Components">

- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-button">Button</DocsLink>: the AI appearance, for the one primary AI action in a region.
- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-generative-border">Generative border</DocsLink>: shows where the AI is generating.
- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-icon">Icon</DocsLink>: the icons for AI actions.
- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-ai-message-ai-message">AI message</DocsLink>: one message in a conversation, with streaming and the typing indicator.
- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-prompt-input">Prompt input</DocsLink>: where a person writes to the AI.
- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-prompt-starters">Prompt starters</DocsLink>: suggested first prompts.
- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-suggestion">Suggestion</DocsLink>: a value the AI proposes for a field.
- <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-illustration">Illustration</DocsLink> and <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-skills-tag">Skills tag</DocsLink>: not yet defined.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Patterns">

- <DocsLink id="ai-ai-chat">AI chat</DocsLink>: a conversation in a popup, <DocsLink id="components-layout-and-structure-panel">Panel</DocsLink> or full screen.

</FoundationSection>
