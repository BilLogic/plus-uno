<Title>AI chat</Title>
AI chat sits beside the work or over it, whichever the task needs. One floating action button at the bottom right of the Page opens it as a popup. From there it moves to the Frame's Panel or to full screen, and the app remembers the mode for the tab.

The examples on this page follow the **Color mode** switch in the toolbar.

### Modes

1. **Popup** is the default. It opens anchored to the button, over the Page.
2. **Panel** docks beside the Page, or opens over it on a Blanket when there is no room. The Frame decides.
3. **Full screen** covers the window.

Under 768 the popup is shown full screen.

### Behavior

- **The button is the one way in.** It hides while chat is open in the Panel or full screen, and returns when chat closes.
- **Header controls** are clear chat, switch mode (popup and Panel trade places), full screen, more and close.
- **The conversation moves with the mode.** There is no history: clearing chat empties it.
- **The mode is remembered for the tab.** A Panel left open comes back open. A popup or full screen comes back closed, and the button opens it in the remembered mode.
