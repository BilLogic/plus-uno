## When to use

Use `DependencyWheel` for weighted, two-way connections among up to 10–15 peers
with no fixed start or end, such as skills that reinforce each other, SMART
competencies co-occurring in session notes or topics that cross-reference. Past
15 items, chords become too thin to trace.

For one-way flow through stages, use `SankeyDiagram`. For weighted connections
on a straight axis, use `ArcDiagram`. For unweighted connections, use
`NetworkGraph`.

## Data shape

`data` is an array of `[from, to, weight]` rows where `from` and `to` are item
names and `weight` sets chord width.

```jsx
<DependencyWheel
    data={[
        ['Social-emotional', 'Relationship', 18],
        ['Social-emotional', 'Advocacy', 9],
        ['Mastering content', 'Technology tools', 12],
        ['Mastering content', 'Relationship', 7],
        ['Advocacy', 'Relationship', 11],
    ]}
/>
```

## Color and accessibility

- **Per-segment color.** Segments cycle through the [categorical order](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-color--docs) from `--ds-chart-categorical-1` (repeating past eight), and each chord takes its origin segment's color.
- **Width and labels carry the reading.** Segment lengths, chord widths and outer labels let the chart read without hue. Because curved widths are hard to compare precisely, pair with a table, surrounding copy or `BarChart` when exact amounts matter.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** Renders a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation. Name the chart in its surrounding Section or Card header.
- **Contrast.** Colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens ([Foundations](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-foundations--docs)).

</div>

## States

`DependencyWheel` has no empty, loading or error state. Mount a `Skeleton` matching the chart's height, a compact `EmptyState` naming the missing data, or a `SectionMessage` (`appearance="danger"`) with a retry action:

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={500} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Competency links could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No competency links yet" description="Links appear once session notes are tagged." />
    : <DependencyWheel data={data} />}
```
