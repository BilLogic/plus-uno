## Usage

Use a date time picker when a date and a time are one answer: a session's start, a deadline. For a date with no time, use Date picker alone; for a time with no date, use Time picker alone.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Parts

1. **Label.** Names the whole group. `isLabelHidden` hides it visually and keeps the name.
2. **Section labels** (optional). "Date" and "Time" captions above each field.
3. **Date field.** The Date picker, on its own calendar.
4. **Time field.** The Time picker, with its AM/PM toggle.
5. **Caption.** One line under the pair: the validation message, or the viewer's time zone and the other person's time.

## Best practices

### `onChange` reports the date, the time and the combined moment.

Each field's change calls `onChange` with `{ date, time, utc }`. `date` and `time` are the two fields' own display values, and `utc` is the ISO moment once both halves are set, computed from `timeZone`. Store `utc`; show `date` and `time` back to the person who typed them.

```jsx
<DateTimePicker
    label="Session start"
    dateValue={date}
    timeValue={time}
    onChange={({ date, time, utc }) => {
        setDate(date);
        setTime(time);
        setStartUtc(utc);
    }}
/>
```

### `label` names the whole field, not either input.

The label's text is read as the group's accessible name, and the date and time inputs are additionally named off the "Date" and "Time" section headings. `isLabelHidden` only hides the label visually.

### A time-zone mismatch adds the other person's time, it does not replace yours.

`otherTimeZone` only changes the caption when it differs from the viewer's own zone, and then it adds the other person's time rather than converting the field. The fields always show the viewer's local time.

## Accessibility

- **One group, two named inputs.** The wrapper is `role="group"` named by `label`; the date and time inputs are each named by that label plus their own section heading, so a screen reader announces "Session start, Date" and "Session start, Time".
- **Validation and the time-zone caption are both wired through `aria-describedby`**, composed with anything already passed in rather than overwriting it.
- **`isLabelHidden` never removes the accessible name**, only its visible text.
