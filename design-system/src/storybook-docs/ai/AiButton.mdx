## One primary AI action per region

The AI appearance marks the single main AI action in a region, such as "Generate summary" on a session's notes. Use it only for actions that call the AI, and leave its look as shipped: the sparkle is always drawn and a label is required. Every other AI action in the region is a default or subtle button with an AI <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-for-ai-icon">Icon</DocsLink>.
