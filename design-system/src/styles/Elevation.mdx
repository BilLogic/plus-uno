<FoundationSection title="Overview">

**Elevation** is the order of layers: which surface sits above which, and whether it blocks the one below. **Shadow** is one way to show that order; surface color does the rest. Collapse the stack to see the screen as a person sees it.

<Canvas of={ElevationStories.Stack} sourceState="none" />

```css
background: var(--ds-surface-raised);
box-shadow: var(--ds-shadow-raised);
```

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="The levels">

Six levels, lowest first. A surface and its shadow always travel together; overflow is a shadow only.

<ModeCompare>{() => <LevelTiles />}</ModeCompare>

- **Sunken** holds the work: the frame, or a subtle Section. It never floats.
- **Default** is the page and everything in its flow.
- **Raised** marks one object a person can act on.
- **Overlay** floats while the page stays usable.
- **Blocking** is the Modal on its Backdrop, and nothing else.
- **Overflow** shows where content runs under a part that stays put.

Raised and overlay have `-hovered` and `-pressed` surfaces for a surface that can be clicked. `--ds-surface-container` is a translucent well inside a surface, and it is not a level.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Surface color and dark mode">

Higher is lighter. In light the lifted surfaces share the page color and the shadow lifts them; in dark the surface itself steps up.

<Canvas of={ElevationStories.Ladder} sourceState="none" />

Dark blocking is the same as dark overlay. On the dark Backdrop a shadow barely shows, so the lighter overlay surface is what lifts the Modal.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Layering">

Each layer is a `--layer-*` z-index token, set in code only. Components name the token, never a number.

<Canvas of={ElevationStories.Layers} sourceState="none" />

- **One Modal at a time.** A follow-up step opens inside it, never as a second Modal.
- **Popups and Tooltips open over a Modal.** A Popup opened inside a Modal portals into it.
- **A Flag sits above all of it**, so a message is never hidden.
- **`--layer-dialog`** holds a view that replaces the page: Frame · focused and AiChat full screen.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">

- **A shadow is never the boundary.** The edge slot is: under increased contrast it becomes a 3:1 line. Turn it on below.
- **Forced colors keep an edge.** Shadows disappear there, so a lifted surface carries a transparent border that the system paints.
- **Text clears 4.5:1 on every level**, in light and dark. Check a new pairing on the overlay surface in dark, where it is lightest.
