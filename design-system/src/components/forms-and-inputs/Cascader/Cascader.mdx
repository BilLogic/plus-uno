## Usage

Use `Cascader` when a choice is naturally made one level at a time, in
sequence — a district, then a school, then a grade. For a hierarchy where
someone might pick or see several nodes at once rather than one path, use
`TreeSelect` instead. For a flat list, use `Select`.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Parts

1. **Field.** The box that shows the chosen path, its levels joined with a slash.
2. **Label.** Names the field. A required marker follows it.
3. **Columns.** One column per level, opening to the right of the last.
4. **Caption.** Helper text, or the error that replaces it.

## Best practices

### Show the path while choosing.

An open cascader shows each level beside the last, so the path stays visible. Reopening a field with a path reveals every column up to its level, so any step can be changed without starting from the top.

### Disable a field that has nothing to offer yet.

A dependent field stays disabled until its earlier answer exists.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Cascader label="Location" value={[]} options={[]} onChange={() => {}} isDisabled />}
    doCaption="Disabled until the earlier answer is set."
    dontExample={<Cascader label="Location" value={[]} options={[]} onChange={() => {}} />}
    dontCaption="An enabled field with no options to show."
/>

### Show the chosen path in the field.

A filled field shows the choice; an empty one cannot confirm it. `value` is the whole path, outermost value first, and `onChange` fires with the path as each column is chosen. Pass and read the whole array, since the levels between are part of the answer.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Cascader label="Location" value={['north', 'lincoln', 'grade-6']} options={locationOptions} onChange={() => {}} />}
    doCaption="The field shows the whole chosen path."
    dontExample={<Cascader label="Location" value={[]} options={locationOptions} onChange={() => {}} />}
    dontCaption="An empty field after a choice cannot confirm it."
/>

### Mark a failed pick on the field.

With `isInvalid`, the field draws the danger border and `errorMessage` replaces the caption.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Cascader label="Location" value={[]} options={locationOptions} onChange={() => {}} isInvalid errorMessage="Pick a location." />}
    doCaption="A failed pick draws the danger border, so the error is seen at the field."
    dontExample={<Cascader label="Location" value={[]} options={locationOptions} onChange={() => {}} caption="Pick a location." />}
    dontCaption="A failed pick still draws the normal border, so nothing marks the error."
/>

## Content guidelines

<DocsTable>

<tr><th>Do</th><th>Don't</th></tr>

## Accessibility

- **The field states the full path**, not just the deepest choice, so its
  accessible value matches what's visibly shown.
- **Without a visible label, name the field explicitly** with `aria-label`
  or `aria-labelledby`.
- **Disabled nodes are skipped, not just dimmed** — they take no focus and
  can't be chosen at any column.
