<FoundationSection title="Overview">

Use the logo to mark PLUS in the tutor and student apps: the sidebar and navbar header, sign-in, email headers and the loading screen. Never use it as decoration. Its colors are brand artwork rather than theme tokens, so it looks the same on every surface.

The wordmark is Google Sans Flex, the product's type family, with four brand tweaks: rounded ends, wider letter gaps, a slightly narrower U and a weight tuned to each lockup size. It is drawn as outlines, so it never waits on a web font.

```jsx
import { Logo } from '@/components';
```

See <DocsLink id="components-images-and-icons-logo">Logo</DocsLink> for props and code.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="One logo, two parts">

The logo has two parts: the logomark and the wordmark. Together they form the lockup. In product, always render the `Logo` component rather than redrawing or exporting the mark by hand.

<LogoExpressions />

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Logomark shape and size">

The logomark ships in fixed heights: 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 96. At 24 and below it uses a heavier small-size drawing so the plus stays open. Pick a step; never scale an instance by hand.

<FoundationSection title="Construction">

The logomark is drawn on a 12 by 12 unit square (1 unit = 8 of the 96 drawing coordinates). Two circles of 6 and 4.5 units share its center, two 2-unit corner circles sit at the top right and bottom left, and the 3-unit plus is centered 8.5 units from the left and 3.5 from the top. Use the `Logo` component; the drawing explains the shape and is not a template to redraw from.

<FoundationExample>

</FoundationExample>

<FoundationSubsection title="Lockup">

The wordmark's cap height is half the logomark's height. The gap between them is a quarter of the logomark's height, and the wordmark is centered on the logomark. These proportions hold at every size.

<FoundationExample caption="Cap height is ½ the logomark, the gap is ¼, and the wordmark is centered.">

</FoundationExample>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSection title="Do and don't">

<FoundationSubsection title="Use approved colors">

Use the color options as drawn, on the ground each belongs on.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The colored logomark on a light ground."><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></Do>
<Dont caption="An unapproved color combination."><AnUnapprovedColorDontFigure /></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Keep the logomark's shape and proportions">

Pick a size step. Do not stretch, crop or rotate the logomark.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="A size step, wordmark included."><Mark color="colored" size={64} wordmark /></Do>
<Dont caption="A stretched logomark."><AStretchedLogomarkDontFigure /></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Give the logomark breathing room">

Do not add unnecessary containers around the logomark. Never rebuild it from shapes or type; use the Logo component.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The logomark on its own, with space around it."><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></Do>
<Dont caption="The logomark nested in an extra container."><TheLogomarkNestedDontFigure /></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Keep the artwork clean">

Do not add effects to the logo or edit the artwork. Use the outlined option on photos and busy grounds, and keep to one Logo per view: partner and tool logos go in a Tile, never in the Logo set.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The logomark as drawn."><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></Do>
<Dont caption="A drop shadow added to the logomark."><ADropShadowDontFigure /></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Use the Logo component's wordmark">

The wordmark is outlined artwork inside the component. Do not set PLUS in a font beside the mark, even Google Sans Flex.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The Logo component's wordmark."><Mark color="colored" size={64} wordmark /></Do>
<Dont caption="PLUS typed in a font beside the mark."><TypedWordmarkFigure /></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Keep the letter spacing">

The letter gaps are tuned for the logo. Do not track the wordmark out or in.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The wordmark's own letter spacing."><Mark color="colored" size={64} wordmark /></Do>

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>
