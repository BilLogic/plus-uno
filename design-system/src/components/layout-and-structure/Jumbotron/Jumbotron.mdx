## When to use

Use `Jumbotron` when a landing or overview page opens with one message and one
next step, such as a tutor home or a program overview. One per page, at the top.
For one item among many, use `Card`. For a message the reader has to act on, use
`Alert`.

It is a composition, not a new surface: the page-level section header (`title`
as the page's `h1` at Heading/large, one `description` line at Body/default), an
`actions` slot and an optional `media` slot, inside a `container`.

```jsx
<Jumbotron
  title="Plan your next session"
  description="See who is on your roster, review their progress and join the call from one place."
  actions={<>
    <Button text="Get started" style="primary" fill="filled" />
    <Button text="Learn more" style="secondary" fill="outline" />
  </>}
/>
```

</div>

## Usage notes

### Lead with one filled button.

The filled button is the next step. The outlined one is an optional alternative,
such as reading more first.

```jsx
// Incorrect — two filled buttons compete for the main action.
<Jumbotron
  title="Plan your next session"
  actions={<>
    <Button text="Get started" style="primary" fill="filled" />
    <Button text="Learn more" style="primary" fill="filled" />
  </>}
/>

// Correct — one filled primary, then an outlined alternative.
<Jumbotron
  title="Plan your next session"
  actions={<>
    <Button text="Get started" style="primary" fill="filled" />
    <Button text="Learn more" style="secondary" fill="outline" />
  </>}
/>
```

### One per page, at the top.

The title is the page's `h1`, so a second Jumbotron, or one under another `h1`,
breaks the outline.

```jsx
// Incorrect — two level-one headings.
<h1>Program</h1>
<Jumbotron title="Autumn 2026" description="12 weeks, 48 sessions" />

// Correct — the Jumbotron is the page heading.
<Jumbotron title="Autumn 2026" description="12 weeks, 48 sessions" />
```

### Keep it to a headline and one line.

Longer copy belongs in the page below. The title is the page title at
Heading/large; do not swap in a Display style to make it louder.

### Pick the container by where it sits.

`container="band"` runs edge to edge at the top of the page. `container="card"`
sits inside the content column, next to other cards and sections. A band between
sections reads as a card with no edge.

### Side media needs 944 or wider.

480 for the text, 16 of gutter and 400 for the image make 896 inside the padding;
with 24 of padding on each side that is 944. Below that the media moves on top on
its own, inside the padding. Center alignment goes with no media or top media
only.

```jsx
// Incorrect — side media keeps the text start-aligned, so `center` is ignored.
<Jumbotron title="Plan your next session" align="center" media={image} mediaPosition="side" />

// Correct.
<Jumbotron title="Plan your next session" align="center" media={image} />
```

Images fill the slot at its ratio, 16:9 on top and 4:3 at the side, and are
cropped rather than stretched.

## Accessibility

- **The title is the page's `h1`.** A page with a Jumbotron has no other `h1`,
  and its sections are `h2`s.
- **The description is a paragraph,** not a heading, so the outline has one entry
  for the hero.
- **Media is content.** Give an image its `alt`, or an empty `alt` when it only
  decorates.
- **The block is a plain `div`.** It sits inside whatever `main` the page already
  has.
