<FoundationSection title="Words">

Use the word a tutor would say out loud.

<FoundationSubsection title="Pick everyday words">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="clear" /></RuleHead>

Verbs over nouns. Short words over long ones.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="A verb the reader can act on."><Say>Pick a time that works for you.</Say></Do>
<Dont caption="Correct, but “availability” is an abstract noun to decode."><Say>Choose your availability.</Say></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Spell out team terms">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="helpful" /></RuleHead>

New tutors don’t know our shorthand yet. Write the full name.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Clear on day one."><Say>Your tutor improvement plan is ready.</Say></Do>
<Dont caption="Fine for a lead tutor. A new tutor has to ask."><Say>Your TIP is ready.</Say></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSection title="People">

PLUS reaches students, families and schools who never open the app. Write so every one of them is included.

<FoundationSubsection title="Describe the work">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="honest" /></RuleHead>

Say what a student is learning. Leave out labels about who they are.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Specific, and it points forward."><Say>Leo is working on two-step equations.</Say></Do>
<Dont caption="True, but it labels Leo and doesn’t say which skill."><Say>Leo is struggling with equations.</Say></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Don’t assume a household">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="warm" /></RuleHead>

Families look different. So do devices and quiet rooms.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Works for every family."><Say>Ask someone at home to sign the form.</Say></Do>
<Dont caption="Close, but leaves out a grandparent or older sibling."><Say>Ask a parent or guardian to sign the form.</Say></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Show the least you need">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="honest" /></RuleHead>

On shared screens, first name and last initial. No names in research exports.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Enough to find her."><Say>Ana P. · Grade 7</Say></Do>
<Dont caption="Fine in a private record. Too much on a projected screen."><Say>Ana Pérez · Grade 7</Say></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Sentences">

<FoundationSubsection title="Say who acted">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="honest" /></RuleHead>

Name the person behind a change. Passive voice hides them.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The tutor knows who to thank or ask."><Say>Your lead tutor approved the swap.</Say></Do>
<Dont caption="Fine in a tight flag. In a notice, it hides who decided."><Say>Swap approved.</Say></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Write as “we” and “you”">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="warm" /></RuleHead>

PLUS is “we”. The reader is “you”. Use contractions.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="A promise from someone."><Say>We’ll remind you 30 min before.</Say></Do>
<Dont caption="Accurate, but no one is making the promise."><Say>A reminder will be sent 30 min before.</Say></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Always add the time zone">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="clear" /></RuleHead>

Tutors and schools sit in different zones.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Means the same thing everywhere."><Say>Wed, Nov 13 · 3:30 PM ET</Say></Do>
<Dont caption="Reads fine, until a tutor in another zone joins an hour late."><Say>Wed, Nov 13 at 3:30 PM</Say></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>
