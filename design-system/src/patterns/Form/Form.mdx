## Anatomy

### In a page

Settings saved as a group, in a Section with the subtle fill.

<Story of={FormStories.AnatomyPage} inline />

1. **Message slot.** At the top, for a server failure.
2. **Group.** One Fieldset per group of fields that describe one thing, with its legend.
3. **Field.** Label, control, help text, and the error under it when there is one. Required fields are marked.
4. **Footer.** Left-aligned, `--space-300` below the last field, Primary first. Cancel is Subtle.

### In a Panel

The Details Panel with the form open. The footer shows only while editing.

<Story of={FormStories.AnatomyPanel} inline />

1. **Header.** The record's name and its fixed actions.
2. **Body.** The fields, with `--space-200` of pad from every edge.
3. **Footer.** Cancel (Default) and Save (Primary), full bleed, half each.

### In a Modal

A short form at 8-col: the Modal's header, the fields in its body, its full-bleed footer.

<Story of={FormStories.AnatomyModal} inline />

1. **Header.** The title and Close.
2. **Body.** The fields, scrolling past the cap.
3. **Footer.** Cancel (Default) and the action (Primary), full bleed.

### In steps

A stepped form on its own page is Frame · focused with the form in the body. Back keeps what was typed.

<Story of={FormStories.AnatomySteps} inline />

1. **Steps.** The rail at the start, with done, current and later steps; four or fewer run across the top.
2. **Body.** One group per step, at reading width.
3. **Bar.** Cancel (Subtle) at the start; Back and Next at the end. On the last step Next takes the outcome's name.

## Slots

```jsx
import { Fieldset, TextField, Select, DatePicker, Button, SectionMessage } from '@/components';
```

A Form is a composition: the fields in a Fieldset, inside a Page, a Panel, a Modal or a focused Frame. Its footer is the container's.

## Usage

Use a Form wherever a person enters or changes values that are saved together. The place sets the footer: in a page the buttons sit left under the fields; in a Panel, a Modal or a card they go full bleed; in steps the bar carries Cancel, Back and Next. A stepped form on its own page is Frame · focused with the form in the body.

Pick the host from the task:

<DocsTable>
<thead>
<tr><th>The form…</th><th>Host</th><th>Buttons</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>is saved as a group on its own page: settings, a profile</td><td>In a page</td><td>Left-aligned under the fields, Primary first, Cancel Subtle</td></tr>
<tr><td>is a short edit of a row, with the page still in view</td><td>In a Panel · Details</td><td>Cancel and Save full bleed at the bottom</td></tr>
<tr><td>is rare and critical, with fewer than five fields</td><td>In a Modal, 8-col</td><td>Cancel (Default) and the action, full bleed</td></tr>
<tr><td>is long, or has sections someone works through</td><td>In steps on Frame · focused</td><td>Cancel, Back and Next in the bar</td></tr>
</tbody>
</DocsTable>

When to validate, what a blocked submit does, required and optional marks, and leaving with edits are the <DocsLink id="patterns-forms">Forms</DocsLink> pattern; where the buttons sit is the <DocsLink id="patterns-common-actions">Common actions</DocsLink> pattern. This page shows how a form sits in each container.

## Alternatives

- For a single value changed in place, use `InlineEdit`.
- For a yes-or-no that applies at once, use `Toggle`.
- For a confirm with no fields, use `Modal` · Active, at the confirm width.

## Best practices

The rules for a form live in the pattern pages. This page keeps only how a form sits in each container.

- Where the buttons sit and in which look, full bleed and 48 high, and the footer that stays put: <DocsLink id="patterns-common-actions">Common actions</DocsLink>.
- When to validate, the enabled submit, a blocked submit and a rejected one, bound inputs and leaving with edits: <DocsLink id="patterns-forms">Forms</DocsLink>.
- The sending button: <DocsLink id="patterns-states-loading">Loading</DocsLink>.
- A field that waits on another, and where its reason goes: <DocsLink id="patterns-states-disabled">Disabled</DocsLink>.

### Keep a short edit beside the page.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Editing a row opens a Panel so the person keeps the page in view. A Modal is for a rare, critical task with fewer than five fields.

### Keep a form to one column.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Pair two fields side by side only when they belong together, such as first and last name, or date and time. Anything else reads down one column.

## Accessibility

- Group related fields in a `Fieldset`, so a screen reader announces the legend with each field.
- A Modal form's title is the dialog's one h1. Focus goes to the first field when it opens.
- A server message is announced when it appears. A field's error is tied to its field, and focus moves to the first field that needs fixing.
- A sending button keeps its name while its label is replaced by a spinner, and Cancel stays reachable.
