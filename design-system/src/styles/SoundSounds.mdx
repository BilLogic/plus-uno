<FoundationSection title="Material">

Every sound is struck on one material, Detent: a dry abacus bead, a short tick, then a soft knock. Before it lands it clicks three notches, the pickup, which takes <Ms s={SOUND_SPEC.pickup} />. A token sets its notes and its level, nothing else.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Key">

Every sound is in C major, tuned to whole-number ratios on <Hz of="tonic" />. Only Milestone starts lower, on <Hz of="milestone" />. Any two sounds can play back to back without clashing.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="The sound sheet">

<SoundSwitch />

<SoundSheetTable />

Length runs from the first notch to the last note falling silent. Tokens drop their `sound.` prefix elsewhere in the docs.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Levels">

Each token sits on one of three levels, measured offline as its loudest 50 ms.

<SoundLevelTable />

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="AI feedback">

An AI sound counts in first: six rising ticks over <Ms s={SOUND_SPEC.aiCount.lead} />, then the result.

<SoundSheetTable ai />

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="In code">

```js
import { playSound } from '@/components';

playSound('done.step');
```

`playSound` plays nothing until the first tap or key press on the page, and nothing while the switch is off. Mount `SoundProvider` once at the app root to unlock audio on that first gesture.
