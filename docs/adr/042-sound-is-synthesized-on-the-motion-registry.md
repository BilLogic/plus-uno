# ADR-042: Sound is synthesized on the motion registry (2026-11-12)

**Decision.** PLUS sound is synthesized in the browser with Web Audio from a plan
this repo owns, and it plays where motion lands. There are no audio files and no
audio packages. The plan is `scripts/lib/tokens/sound-plan.mjs`, generated by
`npm run generate:sound-spec` into `design-system/src/sound/spec.generated.js`;
`check:sound-spec` fails on drift.

**Tiers.**
- **Primitives:** the material (Detent, a dry abacus bead), the key (C major in just
  intonation on C5; Milestone on G4), three level tiers (soft −26, standard −24,
  fuller −21.5 dBFS, measured as the loudest 50 ms rendered offline), the pickup
  (60 ms of ratchet ahead of the first note) and the AI count (six ticks over 150 ms).
- **Semantics:** twelve tokens, `sound.notify` … `sound.ai.refine`, read by name without
  the prefix. A token sets its notes and its level, nothing else.
- **Registry:** a `sound` cell on a motion registry event:
  `sound: { token, at?: 'land' | 'start', when? }`. `token` has no prefix, `at` defaults
  to `land`, `when` is a condition in words. Sound owns and validates the cell; the
  motion registry check ignores it.

**Land.** A sound lands when its motion lands: it starts at the motion token's 90% time
(from the generated spring configs) minus its pickup, never below 0
(`soundStartFor(springToken, soundToken)`).

**Expressive.** Sound follows motion's Expressive moments and nowhere else: Step on its
Finish moments, Milestone on its Milestone moments, plus the rows the registry names.

**One switch.** One setting turns all sound off. It is on by default and kept across
visits. Nothing is opt-in. Reduced motion does not mute sound; the device volume applies.

**Always silent.** These never play, whatever a registry row says: field errors and a
blocked submit's shake; a load failure in the empty-state slot; Banners; AI generating
and AI messages streaming; "Revise your response"; Goal updated; saves, live counts and
Copied; navigation, search and filtering; strikes, Tutor Coach flags, TIP and PIP;
escalations; any concern while a student can hear.

**Runtime.** `design-system/src/sound/`, exported through the components barrel:
`playSound(token, { at })`, `SoundProvider` (unlocks audio on the first gesture) and
`useSound()` (the switch and the player), `soundStartFor`. A token's score is one pure
function that both the Web Audio runtime and an offline render read, so tests measure
the same notes the browser plays.

**Why.** Synthesis keeps every sound in one key and one material, editable as numbers in
a plan, at no download cost. Hanging sound on the motion registry means a sound cannot
drift from the motion it pairs with: the registry row is the one place both are named.
