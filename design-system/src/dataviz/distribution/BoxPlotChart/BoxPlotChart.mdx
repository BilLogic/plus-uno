## When to use

Use `BoxPlotChart` to compare medians and spreads across groups, such as test
scores by class, session length by subject or tutor ratings by school. For a
general audience, add a short note explaining what the box and whiskers mean.

For the full shape of one distribution, use `HistogramChart`. For a plain
min-to-max span without quartiles, use `ColumnRangeChart`. For one value per
group, use `BarChart`; for change over time, use `LineChart`.

## Data shape

`categories` is an array of strings. `data` holds one precomputed
`[low, q1, median, q3, high]` array per category in `categories` order. There is
one series and no outlier points; if low and high are trimmed (such as to the
5th and 95th percentiles), state that in the description. `height` defaults to
400.

```jsx
<BoxPlotChart
    yAxisLabel="Session length (min)"
    categories={['Math', 'Reading', 'Science']}
    data={[
        [20, 32, 41, 50, 62],
        [18, 28, 35, 44, 55],
        [25, 34, 40, 48, 58],
    ]}
/>
```

## Color and accessibility

- **Single series color.** Box, stem and whiskers use `--ds-chart-categorical-1`; position and the contrasting `--ds-border-inverse` median line carry the reading without hue.
- **Limit group count.** Keep to roughly ten or fewer groups so category labels do not crowd.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** Renders a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type and axes, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation. Name the chart in its surrounding Section or Card header.
- **Contrast.** Colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens ([Foundations](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-foundations--docs)).

## States

`BoxPlotChart` has no empty, loading or error state. Mount a `Skeleton` matching the chart's height, a compact `EmptyState` naming the missing data, or a `SectionMessage` (`appearance="danger"`) with a retry action:

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={400} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Scores could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No scores yet" description="Scores appear after a session is logged." />
    : <BoxPlotChart categories={categories} data={data} yAxisLabel="Test score" />}
```
