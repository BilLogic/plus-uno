## For developers

- **Name what closes when more than one thing can.** Three dismissible messages expose three buttons all called "Close". Pass <code>what</code> and the name becomes "Dismiss" followed by it, such as "Dismiss session reminder".
- **Size it with the component, not with text.** A typography class on the button does nothing to the ×. Place it in the parent and let the parent align it to the line it belongs to. Section message centers it on its first line, and Flag places it at the end of its row.
- **The inverse tone is declared for eight grounds:** <code>--color-inverse-surface</code>, <code>--color-primary</code>, <code>--color-secondary</code>, <code>--color-success</code>, <code>--color-danger</code>, <code>--color-tertiary</code>, <code>--color-info</code> and <code>--color-warning</code>. Paint the ground in the parent.
- **Contrast is held per ground.** The ring is at least 3:1 on each ground, and the × is held to the 3:1 non-text minimum. Warning is the tightest at 3.52:1.
- **It is a real button of type button**, so it never submits a surrounding form.

## When to use

Use a Close button for a surface that closes as a whole: Section message, Flag and Modal. To take one item out of a control, such as a Tag, use a Remove button instead.

- **Use the inverse tone only on colored or dark grounds.** The inverse × is light. On a neutral surface it disappears, so keep the default tone there.
- **Name what closes.** A bare "Close" is ambiguous when two surfaces are open.
- **Keep its size fixed.** The control stays 32×32 whatever the text beside it. Align it in the parent rather than scaling it.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Accessibility

- **The name is "Close" by default**, or "Dismiss" followed by <code>what</code> when you pass it. An explicit <code>aria-label</code> overrides both, for places with an established label. Section message keeps "Close alert".
- **The × is hidden from assistive technology.** The name is the whole of what is announced.
- **The focus ring is 2 wide and 2 outside the target**, with a larger corner than the target and no fill under it. It is at least 3:1 on every declared ground, from 3.52:1 on warning to 12.46:1 on inverse surface.
- **Enter and Space press it.** It is a real button, so the keyboard needs no extra work.
- **It is never disabled.** A close control is always available.

</div>
