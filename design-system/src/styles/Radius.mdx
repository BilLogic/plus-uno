<FoundationSection title="Overview">

A corner's radius comes from where the element sits and what it is. Each part rounds by its own token.

Nine rules decide a corner. Take the first one that matches.

1. **Window edge:** 0. A Page in the Frame, and a Modal footer that runs to the Modal's sides.
2. **Pill:** `--radius-full`. A pill keeps its shape wherever it sits.
3. **Joined group:** the outer corners round; the seams inside stay square.
4. **Tight grid:** `--radius-md` (4).
5. **Nested:** inner radius = outer radius − padding. Fix an uneven corner with padding, not a new radius.
6. **Button in a field:** the field's radius − 4, so 6 default and 4 compact.
7. **Other controls:** the control's height ÷ 4 (`Radius / Control`).
8. **Templates:** Section, Modal and Card 16; Popup, compact Card, Table and boxes inside a page 12; the smallest nested visual 8.
9. **Focus ring:** the element's radius + the ring's offset.

A radius comes only from a token.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Scale">

Eight steps. Each tile is drawn at its token; its value and role sit beneath it.

<Canvas of={RadiusStories.Scale} sourceState="none" />

A primitive's name is its rem value × 100 (`--radius-0625` is 0.625rem, 10), and it aliases the Space step of the same name. `--radius-full` is the only literal. Components read the semantic tokens below, never a primitive.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Controls">

A control rounds at a quarter of its height, so the corner reads the same at every size. The three heights each have a token in the `Radius / Control` group.

<RefTable
    head={['Control height (px)', 'Radius (px)', 'Token', 'For example']}
    rows={[
        ['24', '6', <C>--radius-control-sm</C>, 'A Tooltip, a Sidebar row, a compact tab'],
        ['32', '8', <C>--radius-control-md</C>, 'A compact field, a tab, an option row'],
        ['40', '10', <C>--radius-control-lg</C>, 'A field at the default density'],
    ]}
/>

A Tile follows the same quarter: 20 rounds at 5 and 40 at 10. A square Avatar rounds at a quarter of its size, up to 16.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Buttons">

A button is built to sit inside a field, so it rounds at the field's radius minus the 4 it sits in from the field's edge. A 32 button in a 40 field takes 6; a 24 compact button in a 32 field takes 4.

<Canvas of={RadiusStories.ButtonsInFields} sourceState="none" />

The same two values hold wherever the button sits, in a field or not: `--button-radius-default` and `--button-radius-compact`. A button inside a field takes the field's spacing, so a compact field holds a compact button. This covers Button, Icon button, Split button and every button built on them.

A button group joins its buttons: the group's outer corners round, and the seams between the buttons stay square.

<Canvas of={RadiusStories.Joined} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Templates">

Each template owns a token. A Page sits square in the Frame; a Section with a fill and a Card round at 16; a Card inside a filled Section takes the compact 12.

<Canvas of={RadiusStories.Templates} sourceState="none" />

<RefTable

A box inside a page, such as a Comment, a Code block, a Section message or a File upload, rounds at 12 (`--radius-xl`). The smallest nested visual, such as a choice card or a spacious Status tag, rounds at 8 (`--radius-lg`).

<FoundationSubsection title="Window edge">

Where a box meets the window or its parent's side, it takes 0. The Page runs to the Frame's edge, and a Modal footer with a fill runs to the Modal's sides, so its seam is square and the Modal's own corner clips it.

<Canvas of={RadiusStories.Window} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Nesting">

A shape inset in a rounded box rounds at the box's radius minus the inset, so the gap between the two curves stays even. Rows inset 4 in a 12 Popup take 8.

<Canvas of={RadiusStories.Nesting} sourceState="none" />

If the corners still look uneven, change the padding, not the radius: the inner radius has to land on a step. A part flush with its parent's edge, such as a Table's head row or a Card's image, takes no radius of its own; the parent clips it.

<FoundationSubsection title="Pills">

A pill is exempt from nesting. It keeps `--radius-full` inside a Card, a field or a row.

<Canvas of={RadiusStories.Pill} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Small details">

Small parts take the two smallest steps, and keep them wherever they sit.

<RefTable
    head={['Radius (px)', 'Token', 'For example']}
    rows={[
        ['2', <C>--radius-sm</C>, 'A logo in a Tile, a team avatar in a Tag'],
        ['4', <C>--radius-md</C>, 'A Count tag, a Status tag, a Checkbox, a Link, a day in the Calendar, a Table loading bar'],
    ]}
/>

A tight grid, such as the days of a Calendar, rounds at 4. A larger radius pinches the gap between neighbors.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Focus ring">

The focus ring sits 2 px outside the element (`--focus-ring-width` for both its width and its offset), so its radius is the element's radius + 2. Interactive PLUS components draw the ring for you, and the browser rounds the outline to match.

<RefTable
    head={['Element', 'Radius (px)', 'Focus ring radius (px)']}
    rows={[
        ['Default button (32)', '6', '8'],
        ['Compact button (24)', '4', '6'],
        ['24 high control', '6', '8'],
        ['32 high control', '8', '10'],
        ['40 high field', '10', '12'],
        ['Checkbox', '4', '6'],
    ]}
/>

[Border](?path=/docs/foundations-border--docs#foundation-focus-ring) owns the ring's color and width. Radius is decoration: never let a corner alone signal state or meaning.

</FoundationSection>
