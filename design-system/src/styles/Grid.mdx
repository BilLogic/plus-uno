<FoundationSection title="Overview">

PLUS is desktop only, from 768 up. The screen grid spans the screen on the Backdrop layer: modals and floating Panels size from it. The content grid sits inside the Page: cards, tables, forms and text blocks align to it. The grid decides where containers sit; spacing tokens decide everything inside them.

<Canvas of={GridStories.GridAnatomy} sourceState="none" />

```css
display: grid;
grid-template-columns: repeat(var(--grid-columns), minmax(0, 1fr));
gap: var(--grid-gutter);
```

Figma holds the breakpoint modes (768, 1024 and 1440) on `spacing / semantics` and `spacing / patterns`. The content grid sits on the Page's content frame; the screen grid sits on the Backdrop only.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Breakpoints">

Each breakpoint is one minimum width and one mode. The column count stays 12 and the gutter 16 at every width. The sidebar docks from 1024.

<RefTable

Nothing switches above 1440 or below 768: below 768 the content holds its 720 minimum and the page scrolls sideways. Design at Medium and X-Large at least, by switching the frame's variable mode rather than resizing by hand.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Two grids">

Both grids are 12 columns with a 16 gutter.

- **The screen grid** spans the screen inside the screen pad, centered and capped at 1232: `clamp(720, screen − 2 × 24, 1232)`, which is 720 · 976 · 1232 at the three modes. Past a 1280 screen it stops growing and centers. It ignores the sidebar, so a modal sits in the same place whether the sidebar is docked or closed.
- **The content grid** sits inside the Page, after the sidebar and the Page pad take their share (`Frame width − Sidebar (240 if docked) − Panel (if docked) − 48`, never under 720). A Section with a detail region keeps its 7 + 5 split from 960 of content.

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">

- Cap long-form text at the reading width (`--content-max-width-reading`, 864) so lines stay a readable length on wide screens.
- Content holds a 720 minimum (`--content-min-width`); below it the page scrolls sideways instead of squeezing columns, so text never overlaps or clips when zoomed.
- Keep DOM order the same as visual order on the grid so keyboard focus and screen readers follow what a reader sees.
- Every control is at least 24 tall, and 32 or more outside a table row or inline toolbar.

</FoundationSection>
