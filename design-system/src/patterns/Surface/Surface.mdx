The surface is the base background layer of the application shell. It is a bare tokenized container: pick an elevation level, padding scale, radius scale and border — everything else is a content slot you fill. A level is a fill and its shadow together, so a surface never wears one without the other. It mirrors the Surface slot component in Figma.
