## When to use

Use `DependencyWheel` for weighted connections among a set of peers where no
item comes first: how skills support each other, how often SMART competencies
come up together in session notes, or how topics reference one another.

For an amount moving one way through stages, use `SankeyDiagram`. For the same
connections on a straight line, use `ArcDiagram`. For connections with no
weight, use `NetworkGraph`.

Keep to 10 to 15 items. Past that, the chords get too thin to follow.

## Data shape

`data` is an array of `[from, to, weight]` rows. `from` and `to` are item
names; `weight` sets the chord's width.

```jsx
<DependencyWheel
    data={[
        ['Social-emotional', 'Relationship', 18],
        ['Social-emotional', 'Advocacy', 9],
        ['Mastering content', 'Technology tools', 12],
        ['Mastering content', 'Relationship', 7],
        ['Advocacy', 'Relationship', 11],
    ]}
/>
```

## Color and accessibility

- **Color tells items apart.** Segments take the categorical colors in order, starting at `--ds-chart-categorical-1`, and a chord takes the color of the item it leaves. There are eight, so past eight items the colors repeat; the labels keep them distinct.
- **Width and labels carry the reading.** Segment length and chord width are the values, and every segment is named, so the wheel reads without hue.
- **Angles are hard to compare.** Readers judge curved widths poorly. When exact amounts matter, give them in a table or the surrounding text, or use `BarChart`.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** The chart is a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation through the data. The chart takes no title of its own, so name it in the Section or Card header around it.
- **Contrast.** Chart colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens.

## States

`DependencyWheel` draws what it is given and has no empty, loading or error state of its own. The caller chooses what to mount in its place:

- **Loading:** a `Skeleton` the height of the chart.
- **Empty:** a compact `EmptyState` saying what data belongs here.
- **Error:** a `SectionMessage` with `appearance="danger"` and a way to retry.

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={500} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Competency links could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No competency links yet" description="Links appear once session notes are tagged." />
    : <DependencyWheel data={data} />}
```
