<FoundationSection title="Overview">

A sound plays where a moment matters and a tutor may be looking elsewhere: a Flag arriving, a step done, a sign-up confirmed. Every sound is synthesized in the browser from one material, a dry abacus bead, so there are no audio files.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Principles">

<FoundationSubsection title="Rare">

Most moments are silent. A sound plays only where its registry row names one: <DocsLink id="foundations-expression-sound-when-to-play">When to play</DocsLink>.

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Paired">

Every sound has an on-screen change that says the same thing, so turning sound off loses nothing: <DocsLink id="foundations-expression-sound-settings-and-accessibility">Settings and accessibility</DocsLink>.

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Landed">

A sound lands when its motion lands: <DocsLink id="foundations-expression-sound-with-motion">With motion</DocsLink>.

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="In key">

Every sound is in one key, so any two can play back to back: <DocsLink id="foundations-expression-sound-sounds">Sounds</DocsLink>.

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="The nine sounds">

Press one to hear it. The switch is the same setting the app reads; <DocsLink id="foundations-expression-sound-sounds">Sounds</DocsLink> adds the three AI feedback sounds.
