## Usage

Use Loading whenever the product is fetching or saving and the person is waiting. Decide by two inputs: what is loading, and how long it has taken. The rule is the same whatever the page.

<DocsTable>
<thead>
<tr><th>What is loading</th><th>Under 1 s</th><th>1 to 10 s</th><th>Over 10 s</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>A list or cards</td><td>Keep what was there; blank on first load</td><td>A skeleton in the real shape, `aria-busy`, "Loading students…" announced</td><td>The skeleton stays; a Flag explains the wait</td></tr>
<tr><td>A button action</td><td>Nothing yet; the button blocks a second press</td><td>A spinner inside the button; fields stay as they are</td><td>A Flag says it is still working and the person can leave; a Flag confirms when done</td></tr>
<tr><td>A whole page</td><td>Keep the previous page; blank screens wait</td><td>Frame and page header now, a skeleton in each Section</td><td>A labeled progress bar for a long job</td></tr>
<tr><td>A menu, Popup, Flag or Modal</td><td>Open complete; fetch before the click</td><td>One "Loading options" row; no skeleton</td><td>"Couldn't load options" with Retry, still one row</td></tr>
</tbody>
</DocsTable>

## Alternatives

- For a wait whose length is known, use `ProgressBar`; it answers "how much longer".
- For a view that turns out to have nothing, follow <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-states-empty--docs">Empty</a>.
- For a load that failed, follow the Errors pattern: the error goes in the empty-state slot with Try again.

## Best practices

### Show nothing new under 1 s.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Keep the old content, or leave the area blank on a first load. A skeleton or spinner that appears and vanishes reads as a glitch rather than work. A button blocks a second press at once.

### Draw a skeleton in the real shape from 1 s.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The same rows, columns and cards the content will have, as plain text bars, so nothing jumps when it arrives. The container owns the skeleton; labels become text bars rather than label shapes. The shimmer stays on while loading.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<SkeletonInShape />}
    doCaption="A skeleton in the table's own shape, text bars throughout."
    dontExample={<SpinnerInBlank />}
    dontCaption="One spinner in a blank area: the layout jumps when the rows arrive."
/>

### Put the spinner inside the button that started the action.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

The label swaps for a spinner in place and the button keeps its width. Only that button disables; the fields and Cancel stay live. One spinner per wait.

### Draw the Frame and page header first.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

On a quick move keep the previous page until the next is ready. Past 1 s the Frame, Sidebar and page header draw at once and each Section fills as its data arrives. A full-screen blocking overlay has no place in a wait.

### Say so after 10 s.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

A Flag explains the wait and that the person can keep working or leave; a Flag confirms when the work finishes. A long job with a known length, such as importing a roster, gets a labeled progress bar instead.

### Keep a menu, Popup, Flag or Modal free of skeletons.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

They open in answer to a click and open complete. A menu whose items must load shows one "Loading options" row, then "Couldn't load options" with Retry. A Modal's body may hold its content's own loading state.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<MenuOneRow />}
    doCaption="A menu shows one &quot;Loading options&quot; row."
    dontExample={<MenuSkeletonRows />}
    dontCaption="A menu full of skeleton rows, promising items it may not have."
/>

### Mark the loading region busy and announce it.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

`aria-busy` on the container that is loading, and a visually hidden status beside it whose text is set after mount: "Loading students…", then "Students loaded". Skeletons themselves are hidden from assistive technology.

</div>

## Accessibility

- Every skeleton is `aria-hidden`, never takes focus and ignores the pointer.
- The busy region's live label sits beside the region, not inside it, because content inside a busy region may be held back.
- A spinner announces as a status with a label that says what is loading when it is not obvious.
- Reduced motion: the shimmer stops and the spinner becomes a static ring; the shape still reads as working.
