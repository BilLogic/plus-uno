Review Modal Redesign

uno-bot's Review pop-up, before and after, in the order to check it. Each button opens the exact payload in Slack's Block Kit Builder on the modal surface, so sign in to Slack first.

Decided by Bill on 2026-10-15. The audit and the reasoning behind each variant are in docs/plans/2026-10-15-review-modal-redesign.md.

Check these first

  1. 1

    Share-out

    Bad

    Raw tool inputs become a header, a channel and reviewer line, a warning alert, a card with Open prototype, and prefilled inputs for the summary, questions and “not looking for”.

    BeforeAfter
  2. 2

    Archive a Notion card

    Bad

    A destructive write gets a header, the card with Open in Notion, and an alert that it can be restored.

    BeforeAfter
  3. 3

    Issue follow-up

    Bad

    “Comment on and close #688” as the header, a public-repo warning alert, state and labels as fields, the comment as a quote.

    BeforeAfter
  4. 4

    Workflow run

    Needs help

    No “The draft” or ⚠️ preamble: a linked repo, and workflow, branch and inputs as fields.

    BeforeAfter
  5. 5

    Roadmap card (PRD / intake)

    Needs help

    The no-open-questions warning moves from the bottom to an alert at the top; pillar and status become fields beside Edit fields; section headers become bold lines and lists.

    BeforeAfter
  6. 6

    New issue

    Needs help

    The repo joins the destination line as a link, and the public-repo warning is an alert above the inputs.

    BeforeAfter
  7. 7

    Decided card, opened from View

    Bad
