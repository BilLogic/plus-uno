<FoundationSection title="Chart container and tooltip">

A chart sits in a Card: <C>--card-pad-compact</C> sets Highcharts' `chart.spacing`, and the chart adds no outer padding of its own. The tooltip uses the Popup pattern's surface, shadow, radius, padding and layer tokens rather than Highcharts' built-in `shadow` or `borderRadius` options.

<Canvas of={FoundationsStories.TooltipSurface} sourceState="none" />

<RefTable
    head={['Role', 'Token']}
    rows={[
        ['Chart padding', <C>--card-pad-compact</C>],
        ['Tooltip surface', <C>--ds-surface-overlay</C>],
        ['Tooltip shadow', <C>--ds-shadow-overlay</C>],
        ['Tooltip radius', <C>--popup-radius</C>],
        ['Tooltip padding', <C>--popover-pad</C>],
        ['Tooltip layer', <C>--layer-popup</C>],
    ]}
/>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Mark radius">

Bars and columns round only the value end so the baseline stays flat and length comparisons stay accurate. Pies, donuts and areas take no radius.

<Canvas of={FoundationsStories.BarRadius} sourceState="none" />

<RefTable
    head={['Role', 'Token']}
    rows={[
        ['Bar / column value-end radius', <C>--chart-bar-radius</C>],
    ]}
/>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Stroke">

Gridlines, axes, series lines and chart separators (such as gaps between stacked bar segments) use two component-tier tokens declared in `chartTheme.css`:

<RefTable
    head={['Role', 'Token']}
    rows={[
        ['Gridline / axis line', <C>--chart-grid-width</C>],
        ['Series line, whisker, marker ring, segment separator', <C>--chart-line-width</C>],
    ]}
/>

Removing a border or line with `0` needs no token.

</FoundationSection>
