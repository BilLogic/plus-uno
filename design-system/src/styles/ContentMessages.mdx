<FoundationSection title="Writing each type">

<FoundationSubsection title="Errors: reassure, then fix">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="honest" /></RuleHead>

Say what broke, what’s safe, and the next step.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Answers the first worry: did I lose my work?"><Sample icon="circle-exclamation" title="Notes didn’t save" action="Try again">Your connection dropped. Your draft is safe on this device.</Sample></Do>
<Dont caption="Polite and short, but the tutor still wonders if the notes are gone."><Sample icon="circle-exclamation" title="Notes didn’t save" action="Try again">Something went wrong. Try again.</Sample></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Warnings: name who it affects">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="helpful" /></RuleHead>

Say what happens to other people, then name both buttons.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The tutor sees the ripple before deciding."><Sample icon="triangle-exclamation" title="Call off Thursday’s session?" action="Keep session" secondary="Call off">Jordan’s teacher gets a note, and we look for a fill-in.</Sample></Do>
<Dont caption="Clear action, but it hides the people it touches."><Sample icon="triangle-exclamation" title="Call off this session?" action="Cancel" secondary="Call off">You can’t undo this.</Sample></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Success: say what’s next">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="warm" /></RuleHead>

Name what finished, then what happens with it.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The tutor knows their work lands somewhere."><Sample icon="circle-check" title="Reflection submitted">Your lead tutor reads it before Friday’s check-in.</Sample></Do>
<Dont caption="Warm, but the tutor doesn’t know who reads it."><Sample icon="circle-check" title="Reflection submitted">Thanks for sharing.</Sample></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Empty states: say how it fills">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="helpful" /></RuleHead>

Say why it’s empty and what will fill it.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Sets the expectation and names who acts."><Sample icon="calendar" title="No sessions this week">Sessions show up here once your lead tutor schedules them.</Sample></Do>
<Dont caption="Calm, but later when, and why?"><Sample icon="calendar" title="No sessions this week">Check back later.</Sample></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Field errors: write the fix">

<RuleHead><VoiceTag word="clear" /></RuleHead>

One line, under the field, phrased as what to do.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="An instruction, with the unit."><Sample icon="circle-exclamation" title="Session length: 95">Enter 30 to 90 minutes.</Sample></Do>
<Dont caption="Correct, but reads like a rule, and drops the unit."><Sample icon="circle-exclamation" title="Session length: 95">Must be between 30 and 90.</Sample></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>
