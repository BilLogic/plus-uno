<FoundationSection title="Rules">

- **Rare:** frequent actions, field errors and anything once per keystroke, row or field stay silent. Repeats inside a burst play once.
- **Severity:** Error follows only a person's own submit or a job they started. Most confirmations stay silent; the registry names the ones that play.
- **Session:** inside a session, only Roster, Goal, Prompt, Sent and Milestone play. Other notices wait until the session ends.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Rows that play">

Each row is a motion registry row with a `sound` cell: the token, when it plays and any condition. The same cell shows in the row's Motion section.

<SoundRegistryTable />

The app decides the conditions, such as whether a session is running. To add a sound, add the cell to the row; `check:sound-registry` fails on a token that does not exist or a row the list below covers.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Always silent">

<SilentTable />

These moments are silent too, and have no registry row of their own: "Revise your response", Goal updated, Copied, strikes, Tutor Coach flags, TIP and PIP, escalations, and any concern while a student can hear.

</FoundationSection>
