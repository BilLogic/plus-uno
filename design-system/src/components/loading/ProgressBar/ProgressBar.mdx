## Usage

Use `ProgressBar` for progress through a task: an upload, a course, a
multi-step form. When the task's length is unknown, set `isIndeterminate`. For
a short wait with no task to track, use a `Spinner`.

### Appearance guidance

## Parts

1. **Track.** The full-width bar the fill runs along, a pill 8 tall.
2. **Fill.** The done part, a percent of the track. Indeterminate, it is a moving segment.

## Best practices

### Indeterminate when the length is unknown.

A bar that fills implies a known total; use the moving bar when the work has
no measurable end. `isIndeterminate` drops `value` and the value attributes.

### Use inverse on bold surfaces.

The default track disappears on a bold ground; inverse keeps the bar readable.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<div style={{ padding: 'var(--pad-y-lg) var(--pad-x-md)', borderRadius: 'var(--radius-md)', backgroundColor: 'var(--ds-background-brand-bold)' }}><ProgressBar value={60} appearance="inverse" ariaLabel="Upload" /></div>}
    doCaption="Inverse appearance on a bold surface."
    dontExample={<div style={{ padding: 'var(--pad-y-lg) var(--pad-x-md)', borderRadius: 'var(--radius-md)', backgroundColor: 'var(--ds-background-brand-bold)' }}><ProgressBar value={60} ariaLabel="Upload" /></div>}
    dontCaption="Default appearance on a bold surface."
/>

### One bar per task.

Stacked bars for one task read as separate jobs; show a single bar for the whole.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', gap: 'var(--stack-md)' }}><ProgressBar value={30} ariaLabel="Upload" /></div>}
    doCaption="A single bar."
    dontExample={<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', gap: 'var(--stack-md)' }}><ProgressBar value={30} ariaLabel="Upload, part 1" /><ProgressBar value={30} ariaLabel="Upload, part 2" /><ProgressBar value={30} ariaLabel="Upload, part 3" /></div>}
    dontCaption="Three bars for one task."
/>

### Put the number in text beside the bar.

The bar is 8 tall and carries no text of its own.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', gap: 'var(--stack-sm)' }}><ProgressBar value={68} ariaLabel="68% of goals met" /><span>68% of goals met</span></div>}
    doCaption="A caption beside the bar says what the fill means."
    dontExample={<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', gap: 'var(--stack-sm)' }}><ProgressBar value={68} ariaLabel="68% of goals met" /></div>}
    dontCaption="A bare bar leaves the reader to guess the number."
/>

### Show a finished task as success.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<ProgressBar value={100} appearance="success" ariaLabel="Course complete" />}
    doCaption="At 100, the success appearance marks the task done."
    dontExample={<ProgressBar value={100} ariaLabel="Course complete" />}
    dontCaption="A finished task left as a brand bar."
/>

## Accessibility

- **The bar is a real progressbar.** `role="progressbar"` with
  `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` when determinate, and
  an `aria-label` that defaults to "Progress".
- **Reduced motion is handled.** The indeterminate sweep stops under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **Color is not the message.** `appearance` colors the bar and says nothing
  to a screen reader; the `ariaLabel` and a caption carry the meaning.

</div>

## Related

- For an indeterminate wait with no track at all, use `Spinner`.
- For the outcome when it finishes, use `Flag` or `SectionMessage`.
- For position in a hierarchy rather than progress through a task, use
