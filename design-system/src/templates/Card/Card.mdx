## Image placement

`imagePlacement` is `top` (default), `start` or `end`. Side placement applies when the card itself is at least the 480 minimum width, independently of the viewport. Below that width the image returns to the top and takes its `imageRatio` height. Use one placement per row of cards.

`imageSplit` is `1/3` (default) or `1/2`, for side placement only. A side image fills the card height, crops to cover, has no padding and takes the card's corners on its side only. The half split steps the title up to Heading md; top placement and narrow cards keep the regular card title.

## Checklist

A checklist tracks setup or compliance steps in three layouts: a dismissible Card on Home, a persistent Card in a page, and a compact Card at the foot of the Sidebar.

Finish every step to shrink it to one line." />

<ComponentExample of={ChecklistStories.ChecklistCardInPage} title="Checklist: card in a page" description="Required training or compliance steps in a persistent Card inside a page Section: the Progress bar fills the full 128 action column, and completing every step keeps the card on screen with all five steps ticked." />

<ComponentExample of={ChecklistStories.ChecklistCompactInSidebar} title="Checklist: compact in the sidebar" description="At the foot of the Sidebar, a compact Card shows the count, the Progress bar and the next open step from any page; pressing it opens the full checklist." />

Build the checklist from `Card`, `Section` and `ProgressBar`:

- Compose each row from an `Icon`, step text with one fact, and an action column of one width (`--checklist-action-width`, with `--checklist-action-min-width` as its floor). Buttons fill the column and align down the list.
- Put the title at the header's start and the count of steps done with a `ProgressBar` at the end. Align the bar's start edge with the action column. On a persistent card the bar fills the column; where a dismiss `IconButton` occupies the end, the bar is shorter by its width.
- Keep open steps in order above completed steps. Center a check icon and the word "Completed" (Body small, subtle) in each completed row's action column.

Choose the layout by where the checklist appears:

## Usage

Use a card when one object needs its own container on the page. Pick `surface` by the card's role: `sunken` (default) groups static content on `--ds-surface-sunken` without a shadow; `default` pairs `--ds-surface` with a 1px `--ds-border` on the Frame, on Sunken or colored areas, and for dense rows; `raised` pairs `--ds-surface-raised` with `--ds-shadow-raised` for the one primary card per view, first in reading order. A card holds a heading, content and an optional footer for actions. Fill its slots with your own parts.

1. Pick the size by what the card holds.
2. Choose a header, or omit it when the body carries its own title.
3. Fill the body slot. Add spacing only inside slot content, using Card stack tokens.
4. Add a footer only for the card's own actions: a link for one that goes somewhere, or a compact pair with no Primary.

### Color

To give a card a semantic or accent fill, swap its surface token for a solid brand (`--ds-background-brand-subtlest`, or `--ds-background-brand-bold` with `--ds-text-inverse`), status (`--ds-background-information`, `--ds-background-success`), accent-subtlest (`--ds-background-accent-teal-subtlest`), or selected (`--ds-background-selected`) background token. `surface="raised"` keeps `--ds-shadow-raised`. On a clickable colored card, hovered and pressed states use that same token's `-hovered` and `-pressed` steps.

### Width guidance

Content picks the minimum width. Choose the narrowest card that holds the content without clipping, then let the row decide how many fit. Height always hugs the content, and a card in a row stretches to the tallest card.

### Cards in a row

In a row, place the most cards that keep each card at its minimum width. Each row is the content width of its mode, with the `--grid-gutter` between cards. The count switches between viewport widths of 1224 and 1256 with the sidebar docked.

## Parts

1. **Image** (optional). A slot above everything else, or beside the content in a side layout. It runs edge to edge, outside the pad, and the card radius clips its corners. On top, `imageRatio` (16:9 default, 4:3 or 1:1) sets its height from the card width, rounded to a multiple of 4, and the picture crops to cover; beside the content it fills the full height at one third or one half of the width and crops to cover.
2. **Header.** The title, an optional description and an optional actions slot. A custom `header` replaces all three. The title uses Heading xs at every heading level, stepping up to Heading md for a half side image. The description uses Body default. In Figma the title truncates to one line and the description's `text` variant truncates at three lines or wraps; code leaves wrapping to CSS.
3. **Body.** A slot for text, a list or media.
4. **Footer** (optional). A start-aligned slot for the card's actions: one link, or a compact pair with no Primary.

The image sits above (or, in a side layout, beside) a padded frame, and the header, body and footer sit inside it.

## Best practices

### Fit the width to what the card holds.

Visible empty space inside the card means a narrower minimum width. A clipped or crowded body means a wider one.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<MetricAtMin224 />}
    doCode={CODE.MetricAtMin224}
    doCaption="One number fits the `--card-min-width-sm` minimum; the card is only as wide as it needs, so a row of them stays tidy."
    dontExample={<MetricAtMin480 />}
    dontCode={CODE.MetricAtMin480}
    dontCaption="The same number at `--card-min-width-lg` leaves most of the card empty; narrow it instead of filling the space."
/>

### Let a title longer than the card wrap.

A title takes more lines and never ends in an ellipsis; the actions stay at the end of the row. See [Overflow](?path=/docs/patterns-states-overflow--docs).

### Never put a card inside a card.

A card's fill and edge are its own object boundary. Group related cards in a Section instead.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<MetricsInOneCard />}
    doCode={CODE.MetricsInOneCard}
    doCaption="One card; three metrics stacked in its body, spaced by the card's own rhythm."
    dontExample={<MetricsAsNestedCards />}
    dontCode={CODE.MetricsAsNestedCards}
    dontCaption="Three mini cards inside the card; borders and shadows pile up and the hierarchy flattens."
/>

### Keep the card in place while it loads.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<LoadingInPlace />}
    doCode={CODE.LoadingInPlace}
    doCaption="Loading keeps the card in place: a skeleton fills the body, so the layout does not jump when data arrives."
    dontExample={<LoadingLine />}
    dontCode={CODE.LoadingLine}
    dontCaption="A bare loading line takes the card away; the row collapses and everything below it moves."
/>

### Let the footer's job pick its buttons.

A card's footer actions hug the start, under the content. One action that goes somewhere, such as View session, is a link. Two actions are a compact Default pair with no Primary. A card that is all one action is clickable and has no buttons. Only a Modal or Panel footer, on <DocsLink id="components-forms-and-inputs-button-footer-actions">FooterActions</DocsLink>, fills tracks and ends in a Primary. The whole rule: <DocsLink id="patterns-common-actions">Common actions</DocsLink>.

## Accessibility

- The title is a heading one level below the section the card sits in: an h3 in a section, an h2 straight on a page. Nest the card in a Section rather than choosing a tag.
- A card that is clickable as a whole needs a role and a tab stop, and it must answer Enter and Space. When the card has one action, put a Button in the footer instead.
- Mark the card `aria-busy` while it loads. The skeleton bars are hidden from assistive technology.
- The hover lift is never the only cue that a card is clickable. Pair it with a visible action or a link in the card.
- Every card keeps a 1px border (transparent on sunken and raised) so forced colors draw its edge.
- Text sits on each surface in `--ds-text` and `--ds-text-subtle`, which meet contrast in every color mode.
