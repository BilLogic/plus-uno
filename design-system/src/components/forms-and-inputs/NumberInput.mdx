## For developers

- **Controlled or uncontrolled.** Pass <code>value</code> and <code>onChange</code> to control it, or leave <code>value</code> off and it keeps its own.
- **Stepper events.** The stepper buttons call <code>onChange</code> with a synthetic event carrying the name, the value and the type "number". <code>onIncrement</code> and <code>onDecrement</code> receive the new number.
- **Empty reports as an empty string.** Stepping from empty starts at 0 and clamps, so an empty field with a minimum of 5 steps to 5. Give a field with a floor a starting value anyway.
- **The input is a text input with a decimal input mode**, which is why the wheel does nothing and phones show a number pad.
- **Extra props reach the input.** Your <code>aria-describedby</code> is composed with the validation message's, not replaced by it.

## When to use

Use Number input for a quantity a person adjusts as much as types: a session count, a duration, a score out of a known total. Typing or stepping to a value is precise.

- **Set a minimum and a maximum, and say what they are.** Values clamp to the limits when the field loses focus, so name the limits in the caption, such as "0 to 40 hours".
- **Keep it for quantities.** Phone numbers, IDs and ZIP codes are not amounts and should not step. Use [Text field](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-inputs-text-field--docs) for them.
- **For a rough position on a span**, use [Range](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-inputs-range--docs).
- **For a quick judgment**, use [Rating](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-inputs-rating--docs).

Three behaviors to design around:

- **Empty is not 0.** An empty field stays empty, and 0 is a value of its own.
- **Typed values clamp on blur.** "1" on the way to "12" is left alone while it is typed, and "12" becomes "10" when the field loses focus and the maximum is 10.
- **The scroll wheel does not change the value.**

</div>

## Accessibility

- **The steppers are named for the field.** They read as "Decrease" and "Increase" followed by the field's label, minus before plus, so a form with three number fields does not read out three identical "Increase" buttons. They turn off at the bounds, and read-only hides them.
- **Hiding the label keeps the name.** <code>showLabel=false</code> clips the label out of sight and keeps it for assistive technology. Prefer a visible label anyway: a field whose name exists only in the accessibility tree is one sighted people have to guess at.
- **Required is marked once.** An asterisk shows beside the label and the field is announced as required. Explain the asterisk once, in the form's legend.
- **Errors are announced.** The validation message is read when it appears and again with the field's name, and an invalid field is announced as invalid.
- **The value is text to a screen reader.** There is no spinbutton role, so the label and the unit carry the meaning. Put the unit in the label or caption.
