## Usage

Use `VerticalButton` for a small set of large, equally weighted choices
picked at a glance — a role at sign-up, a mode at the start of a flow. For a
longer list or for actions that read better in a row, use `Button` or
`ButtonGroup` instead; the fixed 60×60 size only suits a few options.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Parts

1. **Container.** One size, 64 by 64.
2. **Icon.** A Font Awesome Free glyph, stacked above the label.
3. **Label.** One or two words in Body/small at the medium weight.

## Best practices

### Use it to pick among two to four big options.

A role at sign-in is the case it is made for. Toolbars and ordinary actions take `Button` or `IconButton`.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<div style={{ display: 'flex', gap: 'var(--inline-md)' }}><VerticalButton icon="chalkboard-user" label="Tutor" /><VerticalButton icon="user-graduate" label="Student" /><VerticalButton icon="house-user" label="Parent" /></div>}
    doCaption="Picking a role at sign-in."
    dontExample={<div style={{ display: 'flex', gap: 'var(--inline-md)' }}><VerticalButton icon="note-sticky" label="Notes" /><VerticalButton icon="pen" label="Edit" /><VerticalButton icon="share" label="Share" /><VerticalButton icon="ellipsis" label="More" /></div>}
    dontCaption="Ordinary actions lined up as a toolbar."
/>

### Use one or two words in sentence case.

The label sits under the icon and must fit on one or two lines. The button is a fixed size, so a longer label wraps awkwardly or clips.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<VerticalButton icon="user-gear" label="Admin" />}
    doCaption="A short label under a familiar glyph."
    dontExample={<VerticalButton icon="user-gear" label="Manage account settings" />}
    dontCaption="A long label wraps and breaks the fixed size."
/>

```jsx
<VerticalButton icon="user-gear" label="Admin" onClick={selectRole} />
```

### Pair it with a clear icon.

The icon carries the meaning at a glance; the label confirms it.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<VerticalButton icon="chalkboard-user" label="Tutor" />}
    doCaption="An icon that matches its label."
    dontExample={<VerticalButton icon="star" label="Tutor" />}
    dontCaption="A decorative icon unrelated to the label."
/>

### `icon` is required — there is no text-only variant.

`VerticalButton` always stacks an icon over the label; a text-only choice
reads better as a plain `Button`.

## Accessibility

- **A real button, unchanged.** `VerticalButton` renders a `Button`
  underneath, so focus, activation and disabled/loading states all follow
  `Button`'s own contract.
- **The label is the accessible name.** The icon is decorative; the visible
  label beneath it is what a screen reader announces.

</div>

## Related

- For a single action in a row, use `Button`.
- For a connected row of related actions, use `ButtonGroup`.
