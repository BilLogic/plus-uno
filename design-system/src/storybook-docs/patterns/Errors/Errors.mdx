## Usage

Use Errors whenever something failed and the person needs to know. Put the message where the cause is, say what happened and what to do, and leave the rest of the page working. Severity comes from the component that carries the message, not from louder words.

<DocsTable>
<thead>
<tr><th>What failed</th><th>Where it shows</th><th>Stays until</th><th>Offers</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>One field's value</td><td>On the field, after blur</td><td>The rule is met</td><td>What to fix</td></tr>
<tr><td>A submit with fields missing</td><td>On each field; focus moves to the first; a Modal stays open</td><td>The fields are fixed</td><td>What to fix</td></tr>
<tr><td>A submit the server rejected</td><td>Danger `SectionMessage` at the top of the form, plus field errors</td><td>Dismissed or fixed</td><td>What happened, what to do</td></tr>

## Alternatives

- For a caution before something goes wrong, use a warning `SectionMessage`; see <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-states-disabled--docs">Disabled</a>.
- For a view that is simply empty, follow the Empty pattern; it holds the slot a load failure takes.
- For a decision the person must make before going on, use <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/templates-modal-modal--docs">Modal</a>, not an error.

## Best practices

### Validate a field when it loses focus and clear the error once the rule is met.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

The error sits on the field and says what to fix. <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-actions-and-input-forms--docs">Forms</a> holds when a field is checked.

### Say what to fix on the field, in words.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

The message sits right under the invalid field and names the fix. The red border supports the words and never replaces them.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<MessageOnField />}
    doCaption="Say what to fix, on the field, in words."
    dontExample={<BorderAndTooltip />}
    dontCaption="A red border alone, with its words in a tooltip."
/>

### Keep a blocked submit in the same Modal.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

Mark the field and move focus to it; never a second dialog for the error. A single shake is Planned <PlannedTag />. <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-actions-and-input-forms--docs">Forms</a> holds the whole rule.

### Show a rejected submit as a danger SectionMessage at the top of the form.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

It says what happened and what to do, and the field errors it names sit on their fields. It is announced when it appears. Scrolling it into view is Planned <PlannedTag />.

### Put a load failure in the empty-state slot with Try again.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

Content that fails to load takes the place an empty view would, in the Section's body, so the view bar and the rest of the page keep working. No access uses the same slot and names who can help.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<FailureInSlot />}
    doCaption="The failure in the Section's slot; the rest of the page works."
    dontExample={<FailureInModal />}
    dontCaption="A Modal over the whole page to report one Section's failure."
/>

### Make a failed background job a danger Flag that stays.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

The person has moved on, so the Flag finds them at the top right. A danger Flag does not hide on its own; it offers Try again and stays until Try again resolves it. `Flag` names the appearance `error`.

### Use a Banner only for a condition on the whole page.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

An outage, a lost connection, an account-wide block: one Banner at its tier, danger only when action is needed now, mounted only while the condition holds. A Banner has no dismiss.

### Say what happened and what to do, with no codes.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Own the problem: "Couldn't add the student. Check the fields below and try again." The words carry the severity, because the icon is hidden from screen readers and color never carries the meaning alone.

### Keep every error in visible text where the cause is.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

A tooltip is absent on touch and gone on blur. An error is text on the page, next to what failed.

## Accessibility

- A field error is announced and sets `aria-invalid`; a group's one error is announced once.
- A `SectionMessage` announces assertively when it appears; a danger `Flag` is an alert. A `Banner` is a status.
- Move focus to the first field that needs fixing when a submit is blocked.
- The error icon is decorative; the words say "couldn't", "missing" or "failed".
