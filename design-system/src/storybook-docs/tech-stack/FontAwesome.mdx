<FoundationSection title="What it is for">

Every icon is a Font Awesome Free glyph in `fa-solid`, `fa-regular` or `fa-brands`. Sizing and usage rules live in <DocsLink id="foundations-visual-iconography">Iconography</DocsLink>.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="What wraps it">

The <DocsLink id="components-images-and-icons-icon">Icon</DocsLink> component renders a glyph from its name. Components that take an icon prop, such as <DocsLink id="components-forms-and-inputs-button-button">Button</DocsLink> and <DocsLink id="components-labels-tag-tag">Tag</DocsLink>, render it through `Icon`. The stylesheet loads once in `.storybook/preview-head.html`.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="What not to do">

- Do not use a Pro family or Pro-only icon (`fa-light`, `fa-thin`, `fa-sharp`, `fa-duotone`). Unlicensed styles render blank.
- Do not write `<i className="fa-solid ...">` in product code. Use `Icon`.
- Do not import a custom SVG or another icon library as an icon.

</FoundationSection>
