## For developers

- **Radio is one option, not the group.** The group is whatever shares a <code>name</code>. Nothing here renders the set, names it or manages which member is chosen. Choice group and Choice grid do that.
- **checked makes it controlled.** Pass <code>checked</code> with an <code>onChange</code> that sets it, or pass <code>defaultChecked</code> and let the browser hold the state. Passing both renders a radio that cannot be selected.
- **Each radio derives its own id**, so a group written without <code>id</code>s is wired correctly. Pass <code>id</code> when something outside the component needs to point at the input.
- **Extra props land on the input**, so <code>aria-label</code>, <code>required</code> and <code>data-*</code> reach the thing that has the role.

## When to use

Use radios for one choice from a short, visible set. Two to about seven options, all on screen, let a person compare them without opening anything.

- **Give the group its question.** A radio on its own says nothing about what it answers. [Choice group](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-inputs-choice-group--docs) wraps the set in a fieldset with a legend, so a screen reader announces the question with the options.
- **Use the card appearance when each option needs a description.** Cards give each option room for a line of explanation and a larger target. Keep every option in a group the same appearance.
- **With more than about seven options**, use [Select](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-inputs-select--docs), which saves space.
- **For a lone yes or no**, use [Checkbox](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-inputs-checkbox--docs) or [Toggle](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-inputs-toggle--docs). A radio cannot be unchecked once chosen.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Accessibility

- **A radio needs a label.** Nothing substitutes for it. Where the design has no room for visible text, pass an <code>aria-label</code>.
- **Name the set with a question.** The group's name is not announced by a screen reader. Wrap related radios in a fieldset with a legend, or hand the whole set to [Choice group](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-inputs-choice-group--docs).
- **The label is part of the target.** The mark is 12 wide, well under a 24 target, so the label's hit area does the work. A radio with no label is hard to point at as well as unnamed.
- **Read-only keeps the choice readable.** The radio stays in the tab order at full contrast and ignores clicks and arrow keys. A group can announce itself as read-only and a lone radio cannot, so put read-only radios in a Choice group.
- **Description describes; the label names.** The description sits under the label and is read after the name.
