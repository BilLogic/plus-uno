<FoundationSection title="Chart container and tooltip">

The chart container is a Card: <C>--card-pad-compact</C> sets the padding Highcharts' `chart.spacing` draws with, and the chart itself adds none of its own. The tooltip is a floating layer — the Popup pattern's surface, overlay shadow, radius and padding, not Highcharts' own `shadow`/`borderRadius` options.

<FoundationSection title="Mark radius">

Bars and columns round only the value end — the one end a bar can round without distorting the length comparison it exists to support. Pies, donuts and areas take no radius at all.

<FoundationSection title="Stroke">

Gridlines, axes, series lines and chart-only separators (the gap between stacked bar segments, for instance) take one of two component-tier tokens — there is no shared stroke-primitive tier yet for them to alias, so they're declared directly in `chartTheme.css`.

<RefTable
    head={['Role', 'Token']}
    rows={[
        ['Gridline / axis line', <C>--chart-grid-width</C>],
        ['Series line, whisker, marker ring, segment separator', <C>--chart-line-width</C>],
    ]}
/>

A `0` (removing a border or line entirely) needs no token.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Type and motion">

Tick labels, legends, tooltips and data labels take the current text tokens — never a `'12px'` literal, and never a token `var()` paired with a literal fallback value. A chart's title comes from its surrounding Section or Card header, not Highcharts' own `title` option.

Series entrance and hover transitions take the motion tokens in `design-system/src/tokens/ladder/_motion.scss`. `prefers-reduced-motion` is handled once, globally — every duration collapses to `0ms` under it — so a chart needs no reduced-motion branch of its own.

</FoundationSection>
