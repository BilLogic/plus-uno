## Usage

Use `Logo` to mark PLUS in the sidebar header, sign-in, email headers and the loading screen. Brand rules, color options, clear space and construction live on <a href="?path=/docs/foundations-brand-logos--docs">Logos</a>.

## Alternatives

- For partner or tool logos or school crests or lesson thumbnails, use `Tile`.
- For a person or team or agent, use `Avatar`.
- For an action or object glyph, use `Icon`.

</div>

## Parts

1. **Logomark.** The PLUS plus-sign tile at height `size` (`16` to `96`). At `SMALL_MAX_SIZE` (`24`) and below, it uses the small-size drawing.
2. **Wordmark** (optional). Shown beside the logomark when `wordmark` is true and `size` is at least `LOCKUP_MIN_SIZE` (`24`).

### Tokens

The logomark's glows and fills use fixed brand palette colors (`Logo.scss`); in the colored appearance the wordmark follows `--ds-text`, so it tracks the color mode.

## Best practices

### Pick a `LOGO_SIZES` step rather than scaling by hand.

`size` takes a step from `LOGO_SIZES`; never stretch or resize the SVG with custom CSS.

### Use `wordmark` from `LOCKUP_MIN_SIZE` up.

Below `LOCKUP_MIN_SIZE` (`24`), `Logo` renders the mark alone even when `wordmark` is set.

</div>

## Accessibility

- **Labeled SVG.** `Logo` renders an SVG with `role="img"` and `aria-label="PLUS"`.
- **Alt-text and link rules.** When the logo sits beside a visible PLUS title or inside a home link, follow the alt-text table on <a href="?path=/docs/foundations-brand-logos--docs">Logos</a>.
