<Title />

AI in PLUS is a register of the design system, not a separate kit. It follows
Atlassian's Rovo UI, which treats AI as a layer of the design language (color,
elevation, motion, voice) and then adds only the components that layer needs.
People should always be able to tell when they are working with AI, and the
AI should still feel like part of the product.

### What already exists elsewhere

Two AI pieces shipped before this section and stay where they are, under
Components, Labels, AI label: **AI label** (this came from the agent, and how
much to trust it) and **Suggestion** (a value or prompt the system proposes).
The Prompt starters page builds on Suggestion.

The chat surfaces built in prototypes (a floating launcher, a compact chat bar,
streaming message text, the reflection assistant) are not in the design system
yet. They are tracked as follow-ups; Atlassian itself leaves message lists and
streaming to the product.
</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">
### What Atlassian does not cover

Atlassian's Rovo UI pages do not specify a prompt input, streaming text,
sources or citations, thumbs feedback, or disclaimers. Where PLUS defines
those, the page says it is PLUS's own rule.
