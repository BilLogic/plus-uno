<FoundationSection title="Overview">

Foundations are the tokens, guidelines and visual styles that every pattern and component builds on: color, spacing, typography and more.

- Repo: `design-system/guidelines/foundations/` holds the written guidance for each foundation.
- Figma: one page per foundation under Foundations.
- Storybook: one page per foundation under Foundations.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Page anatomy">

Every foundation page follows the same shape:

- A landing opens the page: the foundation's name, one-line purpose, quick links, and hero.
- The right rail lists every section, Overview and Related included.
- The foundation's sections come next, then Accessibility, then Related last.
- Examples read live tokens and follow the toolbar's Color mode and Contrast switches.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">
