## Usage

Use `GenerativeBorder` only while the AI is generating into a piece of content, one ring per piece. It is never decoration, and two must not nest. For other waits, use `Spinner` or `Skeleton`.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Alternatives

- For a general wait, use `Spinner`.
- For a wait where the layout is known, use `Skeleton`.
- For a wait with measurable progress, use `ProgressBar`.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Parts

1. **Content.** The content being generated, unchanged by the border.
2. **Ring.** A stroke over the edge while `isActive`, so layout never moves. Its 2-unit width comes from `--border-width-selected`.
3. **Segments.** Four hard-edged, flat segments alternating brand and discovery colors.

## Best practices

Wrap the content and toggle `isActive` while the AI generates into it.

### Animate one GenerativeBorder at a time.

One moving border keeps attention on the content being generated.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<FigureRow><GenerativeBorder><FigureBox tone="raised">Summary</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder><GenerativeBorder isActive><FigureBox tone="raised">Goals</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder><GenerativeBorder><FigureBox tone="raised">Next steps</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder></FigureRow>}
    doCaption="One active border."
    dontExample={<FigureRow><GenerativeBorder isActive><FigureBox tone="raised">Summary</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder><GenerativeBorder isActive><FigureBox tone="raised">Goals</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder><GenerativeBorder isActive><FigureBox tone="raised">Next steps</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder></FigureRow>}
    dontCaption="Every border active."
/>

### Match the GenerativeBorder radius to the pattern.

The border traces the content's corners; a pill around a rectangular card looks detached.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive radius="radius-xxl"><FigureBox tone="raised">Drafting the session summary…</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder>}
    doCaption="Radius that matches the content."
    dontExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive radius="radius-full"><FigureBox tone="raised">Drafting the session summary…</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder>}
    dontCaption="A pill around a rectangle."
/>

### Stop animating the GenerativeBorder when the work ends.

Set `isActive` to false when generation ends.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<GenerativeBorder><FigureBox tone="raised">Here is a first draft of the session summary.</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder>}
    doCaption="Inactive once done."
    dontExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive><FigureBox tone="raised">Here is a first draft of the session summary.</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder>}
    dontCaption="Still animating after completion."
/>

### Use one GenerativeBorder ring per piece of content.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive><FigureBox tone="raised">Drafting the session summary…</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder>}
    doCaption="One ring around the content being generated."
    dontExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive><FigureStack><GenerativeBorder isActive><FigureBox tone="raised">Drafting the session summary…</FigureBox></GenerativeBorder></FigureStack></GenerativeBorder>}
    dontCaption="Two rings nested around the same content."
/>

## Accessibility

- **`aria-busy` says content is changing.** The wrapper sets it from `isActive`; the ring itself is decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
- **Reduced motion.** The ring keeps its colors without moving; see States above.

</div>
