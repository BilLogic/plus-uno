<Title />
The drawer pattern is a full-height panel at the edge of the screen, for a task that needs the page in view: the details of a row, a set of filters, a short edit. It places itself in an `Overlay` at the `end` edge (the right) or the `start` edge (the left), over the same scrim a modal takes. Its width is a Modal width step, and its title, description and close button follow the Modal rules. It draws the overlay pair, `--ds-surface-overlay` with `--ds-shadow-overlay`, with Drawer/pad 16 and Drawer/stack-md 16 (the Modal steps, under their own names), and no radius. It is a real dialog: it moves focus inside when it opens, keeps Tab there, closes on Escape, the close button or a click on the scrim, and returns focus to what opened it.
