Radius is the roundness of a box's corners. Each component sets its own, and
it does not change with where the component sits. An **Item**, a control with
a fixed height such as a button or a field, takes its height ÷ 4. A container
takes its pattern's radius. A part inset inside a parent takes the parent's
radius minus the inset.

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">
### Accessibility

- The focus ring follows the control's shape at its radius + 2, so it reads
  as belonging to the control. Never clip it with the parent's radius or
  overflow.
- Radius never carries meaning on its own. A pill and a square tag are the
  same component at two shapes, not two states.
</div>
