<FoundationSection title="Chart color">

A chart color does one of four jobs, and each job has one token family. Name a color by its job, never by its hue.

<RefTable
    head={['Job', 'What it encodes', 'Tokens']}
    rows={[
        ['Identity', 'Which series', <><C>--ds-chart-categorical-1</C> through <C>-6</C> in order, or <C>--ds-chart-smart-*</C> for the five SMART subjects</>],
        ['Magnitude', 'How much', <>One hue's sequential ramp: <C>--ds-background-accent-&lt;hue&gt;-subtler</C>, then <C>--ds-chart-&lt;hue&gt;-bold</C>, <C>-bolder</C> and <C>-boldest</C></>],
        ['Polarity', 'Which side of a baseline', <>Two hues' ramps meeting at <C>--ds-background-accent-gray-subtler</C>, orange and blue by default. Never pair red with green.</>],
        ['State', 'On track, at risk, off track', <><C>--ds-chart-success</C>, <C>-warning</C>, <C>-danger</C>, <C>-information</C>, <C>-discovery</C>, each with a <C>-bold</C> step, plus a neutral for to-do</>],
    ]}

A single-series chart takes <C>--ds-chart-brand</C>, and a de-emphasized comparison or reference line takes <C>--ds-chart-neutral</C>.

<FoundationSection title="Color do and don't">

    <Do caption="Stay within five or six series. Each keeps its slot in the categorical order, and the legend can name every one.">

<DoDont>
    <Do caption="Use categorical colors to tell categories apart, and keep state colors for values that mean a state.">
        <Story of={OverviewStories.CategoriesOnly} />
    </Do>
    <Dont caption="Mixing a categorical color with a state color reads wrong: one series now reads as success and the other as nothing.">
        <Story of={OverviewStories.CategoryAndStateMixed} />
    </Dont>
</DoDont>

More rules that hold for every chart:

- **Never color alone.** Pair every series or state with a label, a shape, a pattern or a table the reader can check. Some chart pages name where the chart itself leans on color, so add the label there.
- **No text on a chart color.** A label sits beside its mark. Where it must sit on a fill, such as a heatmap cell, it takes <C>--ds-text</C> or <C>--ds-text-inverse</C>, whichever reads.
- **Sort categories from most to least.** An ordered set such as grades or stages takes one hue's ramp, with the strongest tone on the biggest value.
