## Usage

Use `Banner` for a condition that applies to the whole page, for as long as it
holds: scheduled maintenance, an outage, an account-wide notice. It has no
timeout and no dismiss, so only mount it while the condition is true.

For something that belongs to one part of the page and stays until resolved
or dismissed, use `SectionMessage`. For a confirmation that something already
happened and needs no reply, use `Flag`.

## Parts

1. **Icon.** Leading glyph for <code>warning</code> and <code>danger</code>; <code>announcement</code> has none.
2. **Message.** Short text that fits on one line. A link inside inherits the banner's text color and is underlined, so it stays visible on every fill.

## Best practices

### Use the danger banner only when action is needed now.

Routine news belongs in an announcement.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Banner appearance="danger">Your 4:00 PM session was canceled. Pick a new time.</Banner>}
    doCaption="Danger for a canceled session."
    dontExample={<Banner appearance="danger">New: the schedule view is here.</Banner>}
    dontCaption="Danger for a feature announcement."
/>

### Show one banner at a time.

Stacked banners compete and push the page down.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Banner appearance="announcement">New: the schedule view is here.</Banner>}
    doCaption="A single announcement."
    dontExample={<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', gap: 'var(--stack-sm)' }}><Banner appearance="warning">Finish setting up your account.</Banner><Banner appearance="danger">We could not save your changes. Try again.</Banner></div>}
    dontCaption="A warning and a danger banner stacked."
/>

### Keep it to one line.

A banner is read at a glance; long copy belongs on the page.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Banner appearance="warning">Add a payment method to keep your plan active.</Banner>}
    doCaption="One short sentence."
    dontExample={<Banner appearance="warning">Add a payment method to keep your plan active. Your plan renews at the start of each term, and without a payment method on file we cannot renew it, so your tutors will lose access to scheduled sessions until one is added.</Banner>}
    dontCaption="A paragraph in the banner."
/>

## Accessibility

- **Announced as a status.** The banner's role is `status`, so assistive
  technology reports its content without interrupting the current task.
- **Severity needs more than color.** `warning` and `danger` pair their fill
  with a leading icon, but the icon is decorative, so say the severity in the
  words of the message. Only `announcement` relies on fill alone, which suits
  a notice with no severity.
- **No focus trap.** A banner never captures focus or keyboard navigation; it
  sits in the page's reading order like any other content.
- **A banner link takes focus like any link.** It shows the 2px focus ring and
  its text names the destination.
