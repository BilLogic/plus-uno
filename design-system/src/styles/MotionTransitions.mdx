<FoundationSection title="Reading this page">

To tell the Between places transitions apart, look at the Breadcrumb:

- It gets longer or shorter: **Drill into and climb back out**.
- It stays the same length while your place in an ordered set changes: **Step to the next or previous**.
- Tabs change: **Switch views**.
- It is replaced by an unrelated one: **Go to another area**.

In running text, Drill, Step and Go to stand for the full names. Tokens drop the `motion.` prefix (`spatial.fast`); their values live on [Physics](?path=/docs/foundations-expression-motion-physics--docs). Per-component motion lives in each component's Motion section.

</FoundationSection>

    rows={[
        ['Tapped row', 'grows into the record (position, size, corner radius together)', <C>spatial.default</C>, '0'],
        ['The row\'s own content', 'opacity out', <C>appearance.fast</C>, '0'],
        ['List', 'fades out in place; it doesn\'t move, and nothing dims', <C>appearance.fast</C>, '0'],

The row becomes the page: its own bars fade as it grows, and the record's content fades in once the container shows where it is going. Because people drill many times a day, the grow runs on `spatial.default`, and Back reverses it one speed faster on `spatial.fast`, returning the list at the same scroll position with that row briefly lit.

<Canvas of={FrameStories.MotionDrillAndClimb} sourceState="none" />

PLUS is at most three levels deep (area, record, one record inside it), so the same container transform holds at deeper levels. On a desktop with a fixed Sidebar a page never slides in: a slide reads as a floating Panel opening.

<Canvas of={FrameStories.MotionDrillDeeper} sourceState="none" />

Climbing back one level shrinks the page into the row it came from. Jumping back two or more levels, past a page no longer on screen, fades in place with the Breadcrumb already shorter.

<Canvas of={FrameStories.MotionClimbTwoLevels} sourceState="none" />

**Focused task.** Opening a focused task (Log a session) replaces the frame itself: the page and Sidebar fade out together on `appearance.fast`, then Frame · focused fades in on `appearance.default`. Moving between steps is Step; leaving with unsaved work confirms in a Modal, then reverses one speed faster.

<Canvas of={FrameStories.MotionFocusedTask} sourceState="none" />

**Used in:** Record page, Table rows, PatternCard, Breadcrumb, focused tasks.
