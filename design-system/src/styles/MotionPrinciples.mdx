Our motion is responsive, clear about how things work, kind in the moments that matter, and quiet everywhere else.

<FoundationSection title="Four principles">

Nearly everything a tutor does in a day is Productive; one moment in a term is Expressive. Every Do and Don't below plays the same scene with one rule broken, drawing its path from start to landing before it moves.

<FoundationSubsection title="Always responsive">

**Keep up, never hold up.**

Every press answers in the same frame through its color or press state (90% done by about <SpringMs token="appearance.fast" />). A moving part, such as a Toggle thumb, follows on `spatial.fast`. Any motion can be interrupted mid-way, switched to Reduced mode by the system setting, and paused if it runs longer than 5 s.

<PrincipleReel id="responsive" />

<Canvas of={PrinciplesStories.AlwaysResponsive} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Build understanding">

**Show how, not just what.**

Motion shows where something came from, where it went, and where you are now. It keeps the speed of the hand that started it, carries the weight of the thing that moves, and settles where it belongs.

<PrincipleReel id="understanding" />

<Canvas of={PrinciplesStories.BuildUnderstanding} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Lead with empathy">

**Cheer the wins, cushion the misses.**

Expressive marks the end of something worth finishing, a few times a week at most. A blocked submit shakes once; a failed save eases in and stays until it is fixed.

<PrincipleReel id="empathy" />

<Canvas of={PrinciplesStories.LeadWithEmpathy} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Cut the noise">

**One story at a time.**

Only what explains the change moves. How often something happens sets how loud its motion is, never to zero at page level. From the keyboard, controls and Popups open with no animation; layers and pages keep their motion. Never use Expressive on a control, a field, or anything done dozens of times a day.

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSection title="Before you add motion">

1. **What does it explain?** Where something came from, where it went, or what is new. If the answer is "nothing", leave it out.
2. **How often will someone see it?** Dozens a day means quiet.
3. **Does it answer at once, and can it be interrupted?** Nothing waits for an animation to finish.
4. **Does the weight fit the object and the input?**
5. **Does it fit the moment?** Cheer a win once; keep a failed save calm.
6. **Does every moving part explain the change?** Cut the rest, or move it as one group.

A new kind of motion is a question for the design team.

</FoundationSection>
