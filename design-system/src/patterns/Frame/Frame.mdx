## Multi-step task

A task with several steps runs in the Sidebar's inner mode on a default Frame. A one-screen task stays a <DocsLink id="templates-modal">Modal</DocsLink>. When a long task takes over the Sidebar is the <DocsLink id="patterns-navigation">Navigation</DocsLink> pattern.

<ComponentExample of={FrameStories.LayoutMultiStepTask} title="Five steps, in the inner sidebar" description="The steps are the rows. Done steps tick and stay pressable; steps past your answers are off. Save and exit sits at the start of the footer, Back and Next at the end, on one 32 line." />

1. **Back.** The inner sidebar's first row names the area the task came from and returns to the main Sidebar.
2. **Steps.** Text-only rows, one noun each. The current step is marked, answered steps carry a tick, and steps past your answers are disabled with the reason in a tooltip.
3. **Page.** The task's name as the page's one h1, then the step's body at reading width, 864, centered.
4. **Footer**, fixed along the bottom and 48 high. Save and exit at the start; Back, then Next (Finish on the last step) at the end, each 32 high on one line. Back on the first step is disabled and needs no reason.

## Width behavior

The Frame decides what docks from its own width, so the content never drops under 720. Drag the width: the Sidebar and an open AI panel both dock from 1408 with a 400 panel; from 1168 the panel docks alone and the Sidebar collapses; below that, and under 1024, the panel floats over the Page with no Blanket. Under 768 the panel is full width. The Sidebar docks from 1024 (in Figma, the `Sidebar docked` variable); in code the Frame measures its own width through the layout resolver and has no token for it.

## Usage

Use Frame for every page of the app. It owns the frame sizes and the Sidebar's behavior, so a page only supplies its Sidebar, its breadcrumb and its content.

The Frame decides what docks from its own width, not the window's, through one layout resolver, so the content never drops under 720:

- **Sidebar.** Below its docking width, opened by hand, it floats over the Page and closes on an outside press or Escape. Hovering the toggle or the left edge peeks a collapsed Sidebar over the Page with no Blanket. Clicking the toggle, or pressing Ctrl+[, closes a docked Sidebar and opens a collapsed one.
- **AI panel.** It opens from anything inside the Frame through `usePanelControl()`. Both dock when the Sidebar, the panel and the content minimum fit. Otherwise a Sidebar left open by default collapses and comes back when the panel closes. Below that, the panel floats over the Page and the Sidebar on the overlay fill and shadow, with no Blanket.
- **Narrow Frames** hold one layer open at a time: opening the Sidebar closes the panel, and opening the panel closes the Sidebar.
- **Modals.** Opening a Modal closes a peeking or floating Sidebar, and a docked Sidebar stays. While a Modal is open the Sidebar does not peek, and Escape closes the Modal only. A Modal leaves the panel open under its Blanket.

Docked, the panel meets the Page at a hairline. Its splitter resizes it from 320 to 640, but never past what keeps 720 beside a docked Sidebar. The width and whether the panel is open are kept for the tab.

</div>

- For a bare fill and elevation, use `Page`.
- For a region inside the content, use `PatternSection`.
- For a short task that keeps the page in view behind it, use `Modal`. A multi-step task runs in the Sidebar's inner mode.

## Best practices

### Give a multi-step task its steps in the inner sidebar.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The steps are the Sidebar's rows, so the Page holds only the task. Done steps tick and stay pressable; steps past your answers are off.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<MultiStepInnerSidebar />}
    doCaption="The steps as rows in the inner sidebar; the Page holds only the task."
    dontExample={<MultiStepStepsInPage />}
    dontCaption="The steps drawn again inside the Page: two lists for one task."
/>

### Put Save and exit, Back and Next in the footer, with no Cancel.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The footer is fixed along the bottom, one row high, with its buttons on one line. Save and exit leaves the task and keeps the answers, so the footer holds only it, Back and the primary Next. Back on the first step is disabled and needs no reason.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<BarInOrder />}
    doCaption="Save and exit at the start; Back and the primary Next at the end, on one line."
    dontExample={<BarScrambled />}
    dontCaption="Cancel repeated in the footer beside Save and exit: two ways out of one task."
/>

### Keep the AI panel docked as its own region.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The AI panel is a region of the Frame, not a floating Panel, so it stays put as a person moves between pages. The Page makes room for it; below `FRAME_LAYOUT.dockMin` it floats over the Page, with no Blanket.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<AiPanelDocked />}
    doCaption="The panel docked at the right edge, the Page narrowing beside it."
    dontExample={<AiPanelFloating />}
    dontCaption="The panel floating over the Page, hiding the rows beneath it."
/>

### Keep a bare Frame to one column.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Sign-in, errors and welcome have one job and no account yet, so the Frame carries no Sidebar, header row or Panel.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<BareColumn />}
    doCaption="One centered column: the form and its button."
    dontExample={<BareWithSidebar />}
    dontCaption="The Sidebar beside a sign-in: navigation for a person who has not signed in."
/>

### Put the place in the header as a breadcrumb.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

The header row is the breadcrumb, shown only after drilling into a record; area pages and multi-step tasks have none. A page's title belongs to its content, in a Section with `level="page"`.

Elevation and fills follow <DocsLink id="foundations-visual-elevation-overview">Elevation</DocsLink>.

## Accessibility

- The Page is the `main` landmark, and the `nav` inside the Sidebar is the navigation landmark. Pass `contentAs="div"` when the page supplies its own `main`.
- The toggle is a button. Open, it ends the Sidebar's logo row as "Close sidebar"; closed, it sits at the Page's top left as "Open sidebar". It reports whether the Sidebar is showing and announces its shortcut, Ctrl+[ (Cmd+[ on a Mac).
- A peeking or floating Sidebar closes on Escape, and focus returns to the toggle.
- Docked, the AI panel is a complementary landmark named by `panelLabel`, after the Page in reading order. Below `FRAME_LAYOUT.dockMin` it is a Modal-style layer: focus moves to its first control, Tab stays inside it, the Page and the Sidebar under it are inert, and closing returns focus to what opened it.
- The splitter is a vertical separator named "Resize panel" that reports its width. The left arrow widens the panel by 16 and the right arrow narrows it, 64 with Shift; Home and End go to the narrowest and the widest.
- A multi-step task's steps are a list named "Steps" in the Sidebar's navigation; the current step is marked, a done step is named "Done", and an off step is not pressable. The footer's buttons follow the body in the tab order.
