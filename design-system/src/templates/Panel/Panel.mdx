## Anatomy

### Details

Opened from a session row. The page behind stays usable.

<ComponentExample of={PanelStories.AnatomyDetails} />

1. **Splitter.** On the Page-facing edge; it resizes from 320 to 640.
2. **Header.** The title names the record, with its status in the Subheader row under it; then Edit and the fixed actions: Up, Down, More, Close.
3. **Body.** The record's Sections. It scrolls between the header and the footer.

The picked row stays highlighted on the page while the Panel is open.

### Notifications

Picking an item opens its page behind the Panel and marks it read. The Inbox stays open.

<ComponentExample of={PanelStories.AnatomyNotifications} />

1. **Header.** The title, Mark all as read and Close.
2. **Tabs.** Unread, with its count, and All.
3. **Items.** One `NotificationItem` per update, grouped by day. An unread item says "Unread" to a screen reader.

The bell at the end of the Sidebar's account row, with its unread count, opens the Inbox. The Inbox opens beside the Sidebar at `--inbox-width` (360) over the page, never covers the Sidebar, and closes on Escape, with focus back on the bell, on an outside click or on Close.

## Usage

Use a Panel when content is read beside the page and outlives a click: a quick look at a row, a short edit with the table in view, the Inbox. It never has a Blanket, so the page behind stays usable. Details sits on the right, docked beside the Page from 1168 and floating over it under that. The Inbox opens from the Sidebar's bell, beside the Sidebar at the start, 360 wide, over the page. One floating Panel is open at a time; opening another replaces it.

The AI panel is not a floating Panel. It is a region of the Frame, docked where there is room, so it persists across pages.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Alternatives

- For a decision that must happen before the page continues, use `Modal`.
- For a record that opens in place and pushes the content below it down, use `Section` with a detail.
- For a short aside off a control, use `Popup`.
- For complex or multistep work, use `Frame` with a multi-step task.

## Parts

1. **Container** (`PanelContainer`). The outer surface. Its `width` falls back to the nearest `PanelProvider`'s width, then to `--panel-width-default`; inside the Frame's Panel area it fills the area instead.
2. **Header** (`PanelHeader`). An optional back action, an optional title icon, the title, and an action group (`PanelCloseAction`, `PanelMoreAction`).
3. **Subheader** (`PanelSubheader`). An optional row of secondary content under the header: tabs, a filter summary.
4. **Body** (`PanelBody`). The main content, which scrolls.
5. **Footer** (`PanelFooter`). Actions that commit a change, with an optional `PanelDisclaimer` for small print beside them.
6. **Overlay** (`PanelOverlay`). A Panel that floats over the Page with no Blanket, opened from a trigger. `placement="start"` opens it on the Sidebar's end edge at `--inbox-width`; `"end"` opens at the far edge. Escape and a press outside close it, a press on the trigger is its own toggle, and focus returns to the trigger. It fades on `appearance.fast`, like a menu.

## Best practices

### Float a Panel with no Blanket.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

The page keeps scrolling and stays clickable. A press on the page outside the Panel, or Escape, closes it. Narrow, it is capped and then full width, still with no Blanket. A confirm Modal over a Panel brings its own Blanket.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

<RuleTag kind="review" />

### Move a Panel that needs full attention into a Modal.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

If the person must not look at anything outside it, it is a Modal.

### Open the Inbox beside the Sidebar.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Its left edge sits against the Sidebar, over the left of the Page. Picking an item opens its page behind the Inbox, which stays open. Flags still show at the top right.

### Give the Panel room to read.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Below its default width the title wraps and the body stops being scannable.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<ReadingNoFooter />}
    doCode={CODE.ReadingNoFooter}
    doCaption="Default width."
    dontExample={<ReadingNarrow />}
    dontCode={CODE.ReadingNarrow}
    dontCaption="Squeezed to a narrow strip."
/>

### Keep the header to one cue.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

An icon, a back action and a subheader together bury the title; pick the one the task needs.

### Share one width across a page's Panels with `PanelProvider`.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Wrap the region that can open Panels in a `PanelProvider`, and read or change the shared width with `usePanelManager`, so Panels opened over time stay the same size instead of each picking its own.

```jsx
<PanelProvider>
    <PanelContainer>
        <PanelHeader title="Student detail" actions={<PanelCloseAction onClick={close} />} />
        <PanelBody>...</PanelBody>
    </PanelContainer>
</PanelProvider>
```

### Reach for the fixed action components before building your own.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

`PanelBackAction`, `PanelCloseAction` and the rest already carry the right icon, label and compact spacing; a custom `IconButton` for the same job risks a different icon or label than the rest of the system uses.

### Title the Panel with the object.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

"Session · Ana", not "Details", so someone who opened it from elsewhere still knows.

### Offer "Open as page" in More.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

For the step up when the quick look is not enough.

## Accessibility

- Floating, the Panel is a complementary landmark named by its title, after the Page in reading order. Focus moves to its title on open and returns to the row, or to the bell for the start-side Inbox, on close.
- Floating at a narrow width it is a dialog: focus is trapped, and Escape, a press outside or Close return it.
- The Frame closes the Panel on Escape unless an inner control handles it, such as an open menu.
- Up and Down are named "Previous" and "Next" and announce the record that opens.
- **`usePanelManager` outside a provider fails loudly.** Calling it without a `PanelProvider` ancestor throws, rather than silently falling back, so a missing provider is caught during development.
- An unread dot is paired with the text "unread" for a screen reader.
