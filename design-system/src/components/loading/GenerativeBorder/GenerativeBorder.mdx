## Usage

Use `GenerativeBorder` only while the AI is generating into a piece of content, one ring per piece. It is never decoration, and two must not nest. For other waits, use `Spinner` or `Skeleton`.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Parts

1. **Content.** What is being generated into, unchanged by the border.
2. **Ring.** A 2px stroke over the edge while `isActive`, so layout never moves.
3. **Segments.** Four flat segments, brand and discovery alternating, turning clockwise.

## Best practices

Wrap the content and toggle `isActive` while the AI generates into it.

### Animate one thing at a time.

The moving border marks where the AI is working; three at once means none of them is the focus.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<div style={{ display: 'flex', gap: 'var(--inline-md)' }}><GenerativeBorder><div style={card}>Summary</div></GenerativeBorder><GenerativeBorder isActive><div style={card}>Goals</div></GenerativeBorder><GenerativeBorder><div style={card}>Next steps</div></GenerativeBorder></div>}
    doCaption="One active border."
    dontExample={<div style={{ display: 'flex', gap: 'var(--inline-md)' }}><GenerativeBorder isActive><div style={card}>Summary</div></GenerativeBorder><GenerativeBorder isActive><div style={card}>Goals</div></GenerativeBorder><GenerativeBorder isActive><div style={card}>Next steps</div></GenerativeBorder></div>}
    dontCaption="Every border active."
/>

### Match the radius to the pattern.

The border traces the content's corners; a pill around a rectangular card looks detached.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive radius="radius-100"><div style={card}>Drafting the session summary…</div></GenerativeBorder>}
    doCaption="Radius that matches the content."
    dontExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive radius="radius-full"><div style={card}>Drafting the session summary…</div></GenerativeBorder>}
    dontCaption="A pill around a rectangle."
/>

### Stop animating when the work ends.

An idle border should read as finished, not as still thinking. Set `isActive` back to false.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<GenerativeBorder><div style={card}>Here is a first draft of the session summary.</div></GenerativeBorder>}
    doCaption="Inactive once done."
    dontExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive><div style={card}>Here is a first draft of the session summary.</div></GenerativeBorder>}
    dontCaption="Still animating after completion."
/>

### Use one ring per piece of content.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive><div style={card}>Drafting the session summary…</div></GenerativeBorder>}
    doCaption="One ring around the content being generated."
    dontExample={<GenerativeBorder isActive><div style={{ padding: 'var(--pad-x-sm)' }}><GenerativeBorder isActive><div style={card}>Drafting the session summary…</div></GenerativeBorder></div></GenerativeBorder>}
    dontCaption="Two rings nested around the same content."
/>

</div>

## Accessibility

- **`aria-busy` says content is changing.** The wrapper sets it from `isActive`; the ring itself is decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
- **Motion respects the user.** The segments turn slowly while active and hold still under reduced motion.
