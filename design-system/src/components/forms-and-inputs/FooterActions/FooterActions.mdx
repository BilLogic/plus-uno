## Usage

Use `FooterActions` inside <DocsLink id="templates-modal">Modal</DocsLink>, <DocsLink id="templates-panel">Panel</DocsLink> (`PanelFooter`) and the <DocsLink id="templates-frame">multi-step task</DocsLink> footer to place one to three filled `Button` actions on a four-track grid with an optional leading action. Secondary buttons draw the `default` soft neutral fill (`--ds-background-neutral`) with no outline so pairs balance without heavy borders.

1. **Four equal tracks.** `FooterActions` divides the width inside container padding into four equal tracks separated by `--inline-md`; every filled track button is cloned with `spacing="spacious"` (`48` high) and `shouldFitContainer`.
2. **One action.** Sits in the right half (tracks `3` and `4`). A single action fills the full width (all four tracks) only when the footer itself is `400` px wide or less (`@container (max-width: 400px)`) and there is no leading action.
3. **Two actions.** Split `50/50` across tracks `1`–`2` and `3`–`4`: secondary on the left in the `default` soft neutral fill (`--ds-background-neutral`), `primary` on the right.
4. **Three actions.** Take one quarter each in tracks `2`, `3` and `4`; only the rightmost is `primary`.
5. **Leading action.** A leading text, `link` or `subtle` action (or a link with `href`) hugs the left content edge only when it is the first child (of two or more) or is passed as `leadingAction`. A `danger` first child of two or more is also treated as leading, rendered in danger text ink on a subtle background. Filled actions then sit in tracks `3`–`4` (one filled), `3` and `4` (two filled), or `2`, `3` and `4` (three filled, clamping the leading action to track `1`).
6. **Destructive confirmations.** Place the safer alternative (`default` secondary, such as "Archive instead") on the left and the `danger` action as the rightmost child, splitting `50/50` across tracks `1`–`2` and `3`–`4`.
7. **No Cancel in footers.** `Modal` and `Panel` already carry a corner close button, so footers name real outcomes ("Discard", "Save", never Cancel). Cancel appears only in inline popups (which have no close button): `compact`, `subtle`, right-aligned beside the action.
8. **Steps.** In a multi-step flow, "Back" (`default`) and "Next" (`primary`) split `50/50` across tracks `1`–`2` and `3`–`4`.

### Hug or fill

Buttons fill in only two places: filled tracks inside `FooterActions`, and a single action when the footer itself is `400` px wide or less with no leading action. Everywhere else they hug their label.

## Parts

1. **Grid container.** Four equal tracks separated by `--inline-md`, with `container-type: inline-size` so footers `400` px wide or narrower adapt automatically.
2. **Leading action** (`leadingAction`, or the first child of two or more when `subtle`, `link`, a link with `href`, or `danger`). Hugs the left content edge and never stretches across a filled track.
3. **Filled actions.** One to three `Button` children cloned at `spacing="spacious"` (`48`) with `shouldFitContainer`.

## Best practices

### Keep the primary action on the right.

Only the rightmost filled button carries the `primary` (or `danger`) appearance; secondary actions sit to its left in the `default` soft neutral fill.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={
        <div>
            <FooterActions>
                <Button text="Discard" />
                <Button text="Save" appearance="primary" />
            </FooterActions>
        </div>
    }
    doCaption="Secondary on the left, primary on the right."
    dontExample={
        <div>
            <FooterActions>
                <Button text="Save" appearance="primary" />
                <Button text="Discard" appearance="primary" />
            </FooterActions>
        </div>
    }
    dontCaption="Two primary buttons competing in the same footer."
/>

### Place a leading text or escape action first or in `leadingAction`.

A `subtle`, `link` or `danger` button hugs the left edge only when it is the first child of two or more or passed via `leadingAction`.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={
        <div>
            <FooterActions>
                <Button appearance="subtle" text="Not now" />
                <Button text="Keep draft" />
                <Button appearance="primary" text="Publish" />
            </FooterActions>
        </div>
    }
    doCaption="A subtle first child hugs the left edge while the filled pair sits in the rightmost two tracks."
    dontExample={
        <div>
            <FooterActions>
                <Button text="Keep draft" />
                <Button appearance="subtle" text="Not now" />
                <Button appearance="primary" text="Publish" />
            </FooterActions>
        </div>
    }
    dontCaption="A subtle child placed second takes a filled track instead of hugging the left edge."
/>

### Pair a destructive confirmation with a safer alternative.

Put the safer alternative in the left half and the `danger` action rightmost; a `danger` button placed first of two is treated as a leading text action instead.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={
        <div>
            <FooterActions>
                <Button text="Archive instead" />
                <Button appearance="danger" text="Delete session" />
            </FooterActions>
        </div>
    }
    doCaption="Safer alternative on the left and danger rightmost, splitting equally across both halves."
    dontExample={
        <div>
            <FooterActions>
                <Button appearance="danger" text="Delete session" />
                <Button text="Archive instead" />
            </FooterActions>
        </div>
    }
    dontCaption="Danger placed first of two becomes a leading text action instead of the primary confirmation."
/>

### Omit Cancel when the corner close button dismisses.

`Modal` and `Panel` already provide a corner close control, so the footer names the real secondary outcome (`Discard`, `Keep editing`, `Archive instead`) rather than a generic Cancel.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={
        <div>
            <FooterActions>
                <Button text="Discard" />
                <Button appearance="primary" text="Save changes" />
            </FooterActions>
        </div>
    }
    doCaption="Real verbs split equally across both halves of the footer."
    dontExample={
        <FigureRow justify="end">
            <Button text="Cancel" />
            <Button appearance="primary" text="Save changes" />
        </FigureRow>
    }
    dontCaption="Default-height hugging buttons and a Cancel label that duplicates the corner close button."
/>

### Split Back and Next across the footer in multi-step flows.

Keep `Back` in the `default` appearance so the step pair splits `50/50` across both halves rather than collapsing `Back` into a leading text button.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={
        <div>
            <FooterActions>
                <Button text="Back" />
                <Button text="Next" appearance="primary" />
            </FooterActions>
        </div>
    }
    doCaption="Back in the left half and Next in the right half, splitting the footer equally."
    dontExample={
        <div>
            <FooterActions>
                <Button appearance="subtle" text="Back" />
                <Button text="Next" appearance="primary" />
            </FooterActions>
        </div>
    }
    dontCaption="Making Back subtle turns it into a leading text action instead of an equal step pair."
/>

## Accessibility

- **Focus order follows reading order.** The leading action receives focus first, followed by the secondary actions and the rightmost primary action.
- **Full-track hit targets.** Every filled action uses `spacing="spacious"` (`48`) and fills its track.
