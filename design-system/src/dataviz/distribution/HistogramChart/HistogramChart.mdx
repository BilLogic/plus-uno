## When to use

Use `HistogramChart` for the spread of one continuous measure, such as class
test scores, weekly session lengths or minutes to first message.

For counts of named categories, use `BarChart`. To compare distributions across
groups, use `BoxPlotChart`. For a distribution across two dimensions, use
`HeatmapChart`.

## Data shape

`data` is a flat array of raw numbers; the chart bins and counts them. Pick a
`binWidth` that matches how readers think about the unit (such as 5 points or
10 minutes) so the shape is neither hidden nor noisy. `height` defaults to 400.

```jsx
<HistogramChart
    xAxisLabel="Session length (min)"
    yAxisLabel="Session length (min)"
    binWidth={10}
    data={[22, 28, 30, 31, 35, 38, 40, 41, 44, 45, 45, 48, 52, 55, 58, 60, 62, 75]}
/>
```

## Color and accessibility

- **Single series color.** Bins use `--ds-chart-brand` with no legend; height and position carry the count from a zero baseline without hue.
- **State sample size.** Small samples produce jagged shapes that look meaningful; note how many records are behind the chart.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** Renders a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type and axes, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation. Name the chart in its surrounding Section or Card header.
- **Contrast.** Colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens ([Foundations](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-foundations--docs)).

</div>

## States

`HistogramChart` has no empty, loading or error state. Mount a `Skeleton` matching the chart's height, a compact `EmptyState` naming the missing data, or a `SectionMessage` (`appearance="danger"`) with a retry action:

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={400} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Scores could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No scores yet" description="Scores appear after a session is logged." />
    : <HistogramChart data={data} xAxisLabel="Score" yAxisLabel="Score" />}
```
