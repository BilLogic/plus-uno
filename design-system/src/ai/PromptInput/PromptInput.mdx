## When to use

Use `PromptInput` where a person asks the agent for something, at the foot of a
chat or in a panel that talks to it. Use `Textarea` for text that is a field in a
form.

## Usage notes

### One AI action per region.

The send button is the one AI action here, filled with <code>--ds-ai-background-bold</code>.
Every other control in the region is quiet, so the eye finds one place to act.

    <Do caption="One filled AI action. The attach control beside it is a ghost button.">

### Starters suggest; they never send.

`PromptStarters` puts words in the field for a person to read, change and send.
Do not send a starter on press.

```jsx
// Incorrect: pressing a starter sends it before the person has read it.
<PromptStarters label="Suggested prompts" starters={starters} onSelect={(s) => send(s.text)} />

// Correct: the starter fills the field and the person sends.
<PromptStarters label="Suggested prompts" starters={starters} onSelect={(s) => setText(s.text)} />
```

### Show generation once.

While `isGenerating`, the field wears a GenerativeBorder and the action is Stop.
Do not add a spinner beside it.

## Accessibility

- **A named field.** `label` is the textarea's accessible name. A placeholder is
  not a name.
- **Named actions.** The send and Stop buttons carry their names, &ldquo;Send&rdquo; and
  &ldquo;Stop generating&rdquo;, and are real buttons.
- **Keys.** Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a line, and Enter during input method
  composition does nothing.
- **Busy.** While generating, the wrapper is `aria-busy` and the ring is hidden
  from assistive technology; under reduced motion the ring does not turn.
- **Focus.** The box draws the Text area's focus ring.
