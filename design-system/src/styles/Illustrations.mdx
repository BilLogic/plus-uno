<FoundationSection title="Overview">

PLUS illustrations are 12 hand-drawn spot characters: five for the competency areas and seven math symbols. Each character's body takes its area or accent token, so it adapts to Light and Dark. See <DocsLink id="components-images-and-icons-illustration">Illustration</DocsLink> for props, sizes and code. A low-fidelity illustration of the UI uses basic shapes in mostly gray, never a real screenshot; see the <DocsLink id="get-started-illustration-kit">Illustration kit</DocsLink>. Decide first that an illustration is needed.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Color and competency area mapping">

All 12 bodies sit at one mid strength so the set reads equally light in Light and Dark. Competency area characters take `color/background/competency/<area>/subtle`. Math characters take `color/background/accent/<hue>/subtle` (red, blue, green or purple) and never a competency area hue. Line work takes `color/icon/subtle`.

<FoundationSection title="Usage">

<RefTable
    head={['Where', 'Illustration', 'Guidance']}
    rows={[
        ['Empty state', 'Yes', 'One character above the message: 96 on a card, 160 on a page.'],
        ['Onboarding and tours', 'Yes', 'One character per step: 160 or 240.'],
        ['Completion and encouragement', 'Yes', 'A finished session or met goal: 160 or 240.'],
        ['Competency area header', 'Yes', 'The area’s own character, at 48 or 96.'],
        ['Tables, forms and toolbars', 'No', 'Working surfaces. Use icons or copy instead.'],
        ['Inline errors and buttons', 'No', 'These need words, not mood. Use a Banner.'],
    ]}
/>

- Use the `Illustration` component; never detach or recolor its parts.
- Pick a size from the scale and scale uniformly.
- Reuse existing characters first. Adding a new character requires design lead approval.
- An illustration works with the copy and never replaces it.
- Other visuals have their own components: `Avatar` for people, `Icon` for actions, `Logo` for brand, `Image` for photos.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">

- Keep an illustration decorative, hidden from assistive technology, wherever the copy beside it says the same thing.
- Name an illustration that carries meaning on its own, using the component's `title`, and say what it shows in a few words.
- Never put the only copy of information in an illustration. The words carry it.

</FoundationSection>
