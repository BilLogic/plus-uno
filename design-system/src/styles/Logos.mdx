<FoundationSection title="Overview">

Use the logo to mark PLUS itself in the tutor and student apps: the sidebar and navbar header, sign-in, email headers and the loading screen. Never use it as decoration. Its colors are brand artwork rather than theme tokens, so it looks the same on every surface.

```jsx
import { Logo } from '@/components';
```

The Logo component set is documented on the Logo page under Images and icons.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="One logo, two parts">

PLUS has one logo, drawn from two parts: the logomark and the wordmark. The lockup is the logomark with the wordmark set beside it. Logo is the umbrella word for all of it, in either expression. In product, use the Logo component; do not redraw or export the mark by hand.

<FoundationSection title="Logomark shape and size">

The Logo comes in fixed sizes and the logomark has a pre-defined shape. Do not alter either.

- Sizes are fixed steps: 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 96, the height of the logomark. At 24 and below the logomark uses a heavier small-size drawing so the plus stays open. Pick a step; never scale an instance by hand.
- Use the default shape and corner radius of the logomark.
- Never nest the logomark inside an additional container, and leave breathing room around it.

<FoundationSection title="Construction">

The logomark is drawn on a 12 by 12 unit square, where 1 unit is 8 of the 96 drawing coordinates. Two circles of 6 and 4.5 units share its center, two corner circles of 2 units sit at the top right and bottom left, and the plus is 3 units across, centered 8.5 units from the left and 3.5 from the top. Use the Logo component; the drawing explains the shape and is not a template to redraw from.

<FoundationSection title="Logo clearance">

Surround the logo with clear space free of type, graphics and other elements that cause visual clutter.

<RefTable
    head={['Rule', 'Value', 'Note']}
    rows={[
        ['Clear space', 'Half the logomark’s height', 'On every side, measured from the edge of the logomark, not the edge of the Logo frame.'],
        ['Minimum size', '24 (lockup)', 'The wordmark needs a 24 logomark; below that the logo is the logomark alone, down to 16. Never scale an instance by hand.'],
    ]}
/>

<FoundationExample caption="Clear space is half the logomark's height on every side of the lockup.">
<LogoClearSpace />
</FoundationExample>

Every PLUS logo has the logomark, so clearance is measured from the logomark. Align a logo to its neighbors by the logomark's edge and center it on the same line as the text beside it.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Do and don't">

<FoundationSubsection title="Use approved colors">

Use the color options as drawn, on the ground each belongs on.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The colored logomark on a light ground."><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></Do>
<Dont caption="An unapproved color combination."><span style={{ display: 'inline-block', filter: 'hue-rotate(160deg) saturate(2)' }}><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></span></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Keep the logomark's shape and proportions">

Pick a size step. Do not stretch, crop or rotate the logomark.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="A size step, wordmark included."><Mark color="colored" size={64} wordmark /></Do>
<Dont caption="A stretched logomark."><span style={{ display: 'inline-block', transform: 'scaleX(1.8)', transformOrigin: 'left center', marginInlineEnd: 'var(--card-pad-spacious)' }}><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></span></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Give the logomark breathing room">

Do not add unnecessary containers around the logomark. Never rebuild it from shapes or type; use the Logo component.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The logomark on its own, with space around it."><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></Do>
<Dont caption="The logomark nested in an extra container."><span style={{ display: 'inline-block', padding: 'var(--pad-x-sm)', borderRadius: 'var(--radius-full)', boxShadow: 'inset 0 0 0 var(--border-default) var(--ds-border-bold)' }}><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></span></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Keep the artwork clean">

Do not add effects to the logo or edit the artwork. Use the outlined option on photos and busy grounds, and keep to one Logo per view: partner and tool logos go in a Tile, never in the Logo set.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="The logomark as drawn."><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></Do>
<Dont caption="A drop shadow added to the logomark."><span style={{ display: 'inline-block', filter: 'drop-shadow(0 4px 4px var(--ds-shadow-overlay))' }}><Mark color="colored" size={64} /></span></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>
