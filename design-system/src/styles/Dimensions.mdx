<FoundationSection title="Overview">

Dimension is the ruler. Spacing, sizing, radius and stroke are what you measure with it, the way color roles read from the palette. Every length on a page is a dimension primitive, or is computed from them (a column width, a fill).

<FoundationSection title="Primitives">

Primitives are named in basis points of a rem: `100` is 1 rem, 16 px at the default root; `025` is 0.25 rem, 4 px. The quarter primitives below step by 4 px (`025`). Above 80, the size ladder continues for widths: 120 · 160 · 224 · 240 · 304 · 320 · 400 · 480 · 640 · 720 · 864 · 1232.

<FoundationSubsection title="Fine primitives">

Below `100`, four values are not a multiple of 4 px (`025`): 1, 2, 6 and 10. They live inside components only: a stroke, a radius, a focus offset, an icon gap, or the pad-y that lands a control’s height on the grid. Templates read quarter primitives only.

<FoundationSection title="Heights snap to 4 px (025)">

Every line-height, control, row, label and fixed-height box is a multiple of 4 px (025 rem). A box that hugs its content stays on the grid because its lines and pads are on it. The line box snaps, not the glyph: 14 px text sits in a 20 line. Sizes are rem, so a reader who sets a larger default font gets a larger grid, and everything still lands on it.

<FoundationSection title="Core sizes">

Five box sizes, every `050` from `100` to `300`: 16 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 48. Reach for these first; any other multiple of 025 is still allowed. An empty cell is a rule: no control below the 24 target, no row under 32.

<FoundationSection title="Building a control">

Height first, then pad. A Button is a 20 line plus 6 above and below, with 12 at each side. A Tag is a 16 line plus 2 above and below, with 4 at each side: 20. A fine primitive exists so the height lands on the grid.

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">

- A control is never smaller than 24 × 24, the minimum pointer target; 32 or more outside a table row or inline toolbar.
- Sizes are rem, so they follow the reader’s font setting.
- A tag is never the only way to act: a removable tag’s remove button has a name.

</FoundationSection>
