Each row is a template's, pattern's or component's entry in the motion registry, and the same text sits in that page's Motion section. Rows are grouped by tier: templates first, then patterns, then components. A page absent from the table does not move: it is static, or its motion is the Update a value or state change on its own page. The Sound column names the sound an event plays, when it plays and its condition; an empty cell is silent (Foundations · Sound · When to play).
