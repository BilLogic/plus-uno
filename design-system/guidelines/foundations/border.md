# Border

Border widths and their semantic tokens.

## Rules

- **State and emphasis use the whole shape.** Show a selected, active or
  flagged row, card, alert, quote or nav item with its fill, text, icon or
  weight, or with a border or ring on every side. A colored stripe on one edge
  (a left or leading bar) is not part of this system.

Further border-width rules go here — file them through `uno-maintain`.
