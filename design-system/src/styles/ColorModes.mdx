### Modes

A mode is an attribute on `<html>`: `data-color-mode` is `light`, `dark` or `auto`, and `data-contrast-mode` is `no-preference`, `more` or `auto`. No attribute means light at standard contrast, and `auto` follows the OS's `prefers-color-scheme` and `prefers-contrast`. The toolbar also sets Bootstrap's `data-bs-theme` to match.
