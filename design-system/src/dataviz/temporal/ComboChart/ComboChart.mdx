## When to use

Use `ComboChart` when a count and a rate belong together: sessions held each
week as columns with the attendance rate as a line, or tutors active per month
with the average session rating. The columns carry the volume, the line the
ratio.

When every series shares one unit, use `LineChart` or `BarChart`: a second axis
tells the reader the two scales cannot be compared directly, and invites them
to read a crossing as meaningful when it is only a choice of scale. For a
ranked breakdown with a cumulative line, use `ParetoChart`. For a value against
a target, use `BulletChart`.

## Data shape

`categories` is an array of period labels. `barData` and `lineData` are each an
array of `{ name, data }`, with one number per category. All three are
required; pass an empty array for a side you do not need. Give each line series
`yAxis: 1` to put it on the secondary axis; leave it out and the line shares
the columns' scale.

```jsx
<ComboChart
    primaryAxisLabel="Sessions held"
    secondaryAxisLabel="Attendance (%)"
    categories={['Wk 1', 'Wk 2', 'Wk 3', 'Wk 4', 'Wk 5']}
    barData={[{ name: 'Sessions', data: [120, 134, 128, 142, 150] }]}
    lineData={[{ name: 'Attendance', data: [86, 88, 84, 90, 92], yAxis: 1 }]}
/>
```

A series may carry its own `color`; leave it out and the chart assigns one.
`height` defaults to 300.

## Color and accessibility

- **Color follows series order across both kinds.** Column series take the categorical colors from slot 1; line series continue from the next free slot. A single column series takes slot 1, not the brand chart color.
- **Shape separates the kinds.** Columns encode by length, lines by position, so a column series and a line series stay distinct without color. Two lines, or two column series, are told apart by color alone.
- **Make the axis pairing explicit.** Nothing on the chart ties a series to its axis except the axis titles. Name the unit in both `primaryAxisLabel` and `secondaryAxisLabel`, and in the series names.
- **Known gap.** The line markers' ring and the secondary axis title take Highcharts' built-in default colors, and the marker fill is white, rather than chart tokens, so they do not follow the color mode.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** The chart is a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type and axes, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation through the data. The chart takes no title of its own, so name it in the Section or Card header around it.
- **Contrast.** Series colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens.

## States

`ComboChart` draws what it is given and has no empty, loading or error state of its own. The caller chooses what to mount in its place:

- **Loading:** a `Skeleton` the height of the chart.
- **Empty:** a compact `EmptyState` saying what data belongs here.
- **Error:** a `SectionMessage` with `appearance="danger"` and a way to retry.

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={300} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Attendance could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : categories.length === 0 ? <EmptyState size="compact" header="No sessions yet" description="Sessions and attendance appear once a week is logged." />
    : <ComboChart categories={categories} barData={barData} lineData={lineData} />}
```
