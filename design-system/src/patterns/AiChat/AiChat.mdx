### Modes

1. **Popup** is the default. It opens anchored to the button, over the Page.
2. **Panel** docks beside the Page, or opens over it on a Blanket when there is no room. The Frame decides.
3. **Full screen** covers the window.

Under 768 the popup is shown full screen.

### Behavior

- **The button is the one way in.** It hides while chat is open in the Panel or full screen, and returns when chat closes.
- **Header controls** are clear chat, switch mode (popup and Panel trade places), full screen, more and close.
- **The conversation moves with the mode.** There is no history: clearing chat empties it.
- **The mode is remembered for the tab.** A Panel left open comes back open. A popup or full screen comes back closed, and the button opens it in the remembered mode.

### Identity and trust

AI chat applies the six AI rules from the AI overview.

1. **One AI identity, unnamed.** The chat is called AI chat and carries the AI icon. It has no persona name or avatar.
2. **Every answer is marked.** Each reply is an AI message, which leads with the AI icon.
3. **Fallibility, once.** "AI can make mistakes. Check important info." sits under the prompt input, never on each message.
4. **Sources when it draws on data.** A reply that used records lists them under the message: `respond` returns `{ text, sources }` instead of text.
5. **People confirm actions.** The chat proposes changes; anything that changes data waits for the person's confirm.
6. **Feedback on every answer.** Each reply offers thumbs up and thumbs down, and `onFeedback` receives the reply with its rating. The rating stays on the reply as chat moves between modes.

### Storybook variant

`variant="storybook"` is the chat in the Storybook docs. Its header shows clear chat, move to Panel and close, with no full screen and no more menu. The docs' floating button opens it through its AI agent action, so the chat's own button is left out with `launcher={false}`: the popup floats above the bottom right corner instead. `respond` may return `{ text, links }`, and the links are drawn under the text. The docs' answerer calls a server-side function that answers from the design-system index and holds the credentials, so none reaches the browser; when it errors or is rate limited, the chat searches the site's pages and the component index instead and says so.
