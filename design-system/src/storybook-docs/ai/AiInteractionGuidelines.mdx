<FoundationSection title="Identity and trust">

1. **One unnamed AI identity.** Mark AI with the <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-icon">AI icon</DocsLink>. Give it no persona name, face or avatar; its voice is a plain "I".
2. **Mark every AI answer.** Use an <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-ai-message-ai-message">AI message</DocsLink> for replies. Mark generated content, including drafts and summaries, with the AI icon until a person edits or accepts it.
3. **Say fallibility once.** Show "AI can make mistakes. Check important info." under the <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-prompt-input">Prompt input</DocsLink>, never on each message.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Working with people">

4. **List sources.** An answer that uses records lists what it read with <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-ai-message-ai-sources">AI sources</DocsLink>.
5. **Confirm changes.** AI proposes; wait for a person's confirmation before changing data.
6. **Offer feedback on every answer.** Use <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-ai-message-ai-feedback">AI feedback</DocsLink> for thumbs up and down.

</FoundationSection>
