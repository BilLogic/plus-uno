## When to use

Use the Logo to mark PLUS itself: the sidebar and navbar header, sign-in, email
headers and the loading screen.

- For a partner or tool logo, use **Tile** with <code>content="logo"</code>.
- For the loading state of a page, use **Spinner**.
- Never use the Logo as decoration.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Anatomy

1. **Mark.** The PLUS plus-sign tile, drawn in the chosen style: colored, filled or outlined.
2. **Wordmark.** The word PLUS, set beside the mark when <code>text</code> is on.

Logo colors are brand artwork, not theme tokens. Pick the style by the ground it sits on.

## Rules

- **Use a size step.** Never stretch, crop, recolor or rotate the mark.
- **Pick the style by the ground.** Use colored on light grounds, filled where the mark must read as a block and outlined on busy or tinted grounds. Filled and outlined need a colored or dark ground.
- **Leave the brand colors alone.** Do not rebind them to theme tokens.
- **One brand mark per view.** A second Logo competes with the first and reads as two products.
- **Partner and tool logos go in a Tile.** Never in the Logo.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Accessibility

- **Name the Logo where it stands alone.** On the sign-in screen, where nothing else says PLUS, give the wrapper <code>role="img"</code> and <code>aria-label="PLUS"</code>. The Logo draws an SVG that has no name of its own.
- **Hide it beside a visible title.** Next to the word PLUS the mark is decorative, so hide it from assistive technology and the name is not read twice.
- **Name a link for where it goes.** A Logo that links home is a link named "PLUS home". It takes keyboard focus and shows the focus ring.
- **Choose the style by ground.** The mark keeps its contrast only on the ground its style is made for.

</div>
