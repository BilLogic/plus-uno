## Anatomy

### Details

Opened from a session row. The page behind stays usable.

<Story of={PanelStories.AnatomyDetails} inline />

1. **Splitter.** On the Page-facing edge; it resizes from 320 to 640.
2. **Header.** The title names the record, with its status in the Subheader row under it; then the fixed actions: Up, Down, More, Close.
3. **Body.** The record's Sections. It scrolls between the header and the footer.

The picked row stays highlighted on the page while the Panel is open.

### Notifications

Picking an item opens its page behind the Panel and marks it read. The Inbox stays open.

<Story of={PanelStories.AnatomyNotifications} inline />

1. **Header.** The title, a filter (Unread, All, Mentions), Archive all and Close.
2. **Items.** Avatar, who did what and where, a one-line excerpt and the time. An unread item ends in a dot, paired with the word "unread" for a screen reader.

The Bell in the Sidebar, with its unread count, opens the Inbox. It sits with the Sidebar and is Planned with the Inbox's placement.

## Usage

Use a Panel when content is read beside the page and outlives a click: a quick look at a row, a short edit with the table in view, the Inbox. It never has a Backdrop, so the page behind stays usable. Details floats over the right of the Page; the Inbox floats out of the Sidebar on the left. One floating Panel is open at a time; opening another replaces it.

The AI panel is not a floating Panel. It is a region of the Frame, docked where there is room, so it persists across pages.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Alternatives

- For a decision that must happen before the page continues, use `Modal`.
- For a queue page, use a `Section` detail: the record in place beside its list.
- For a short aside off a control, use `Popup`.
- For complex or multistep work, give the record its own page with `Frame` · focused.

</div>

## Best practices

### Float a Panel with no Backdrop.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

The page keeps scrolling and stays clickable. A press on the page outside the Panel, or Escape, closes it. Under 1024 it is capped at 320, and under 768 it takes the whole width, still with no Backdrop. A confirm Modal over a Panel brings its own Backdrop.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<FloatsNoBackdrop />}
    doCaption="The Panel floats on its shadow and the page beside it stays undimmed."
    dontExample={<PanelOnBackdrop />}
    dontCaption="The same Panel with a Backdrop dimming the whole page."
/>

### Open one floating Panel at a time.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

Another row swaps the content; the Inbox replaces Details. The overlay module keeps a second overlay closed.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<OneFloatingPanel />}
    doCaption="One Panel; another row swaps what it shows."
    dontExample={<TwoFloatingPanels />}
    dontCaption="Two Panels open at once, the second covering the first."
/>

### Keep the picked row highlighted while Details is open.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Picking the same row again closes it; Up and Down step through the rows.

### Show a footer only while editing.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Cancel (Default) and Save (Primary), full bleed. Reading needs no footer.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<ReadingNoFooter />}
    doCaption="Reading: a header and a body, nothing to save."
    dontExample={<ReadingWithFooter />}
    dontCaption="A footer of buttons on a Panel that is only read."
/>

### Leave the AI panel uncovered.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

A floating Panel and the docked AI panel sit side by side. The AI panel is never covered. This pair is Planned.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<BesideAiPanel />}
    doCaption="The floating Panel beside the docked AI panel, both fully visible."
    dontExample={<CoversAiPanel />}
    dontCaption="The floating Panel drawn over the AI panel, hiding part of it."
/>

### Move a Panel that needs full attention into a Modal.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

If the person must not look at anything outside it, it is a Modal.

### Grow the Inbox out of the Sidebar.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Its left edge sits against the Sidebar, over the left of the Page. Picking an item opens its page behind the Inbox, which stays open. Flags still show at the top right. This placement is Planned.

### Title the Panel with the object.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

"Session · Mar 4", not "Details", so someone who opened it from elsewhere still knows.

### Offer "Open as page" in More.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

For the step up when the quick look is not enough.

## Accessibility

- Floating, the Panel is a complementary landmark named by its title, after the Page in reading order. Focus moves to its title on open and returns to the row, or the Bell, on close.
- Under 1024 it is a dialog: focus is trapped, and Escape, a press outside or Close return it.
- Escape closes the Panel unless an inner control handles it, such as an open menu.
- Up and Down are named "Previous" and "Next" and announce the record that opens.
- An unread dot is paired with the text "unread" for a screen reader.
