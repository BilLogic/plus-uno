The card pattern is a componentized shell with baked-in tokens — the `--surface-raised` fill on a 1px outline-variant border, card radius, and no shadow at rest. A card with an `onClick` lifts to `--surface-raised-shadow` (Light/2) on hover, and `dragging` lifts any card while it moves. Consumers fill the header (`title`, `description` and `actions`, or a custom `header`), the body, an optional `image` and a left-aligned footer; the shell owns the frame and the Card spacing tokens. It mirrors `Pattern/Card` and `_Card header` in Figma. For the fully-featured content card (title, media, links, actions), use the core Card component instead.

## Related

- For a REGION of a page rather than one object, use `PatternSection` — a card is a thing, a section is a place.
- For the raw fill and elevation with no slots, use `Surface`. `Card` is a `Surface` with a shape agreed.
- For something that must be decided before the page can continue, use `Modal`.
- For many objects of the same shape, use `Table` — a column of cards is a table that lost its columns.
