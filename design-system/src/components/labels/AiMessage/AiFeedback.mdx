## Usage

Place `AiFeedback` under a finished AI message. Leave `value` unset to manage
the rating internally, starting from `defaultValue`, or pass `value` to control it.

<DocsTable>
<thead>
<tr><th>Action</th><th>Result</th><th><code>onFeedback</code> receives</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Press a thumb</td><td>Choose one rating; thumbs down opens an optional comment.</td><td><code>{'{ rating: \'up\' }'}</code> or <code>{'{ rating: \'down\' }'}</code></td></tr>
<tr><td>Press the selected thumb</td><td>Clear the rating.</td><td><code>{'{ rating: null }'}</code></td></tr>
<tr><td>Send a comment</td><td>Submit the comment and close the popup.</td><td><code>{'{ rating: \'down\', comment }'}</code></td></tr>
<tr><td>Skip the comment</td><td>Close the popup and keep the rating.</td><td>No additional callback.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</DocsTable>

</div>

## Accessibility

- The thumbs form a group named "Rate this answer". Its toggle buttons, "Good answer" and "Bad answer", report `aria-pressed`, true on the chosen one.
- Thumbs down never reports `aria-expanded`: the popup opens after rating, anchored to the thumb. Focus enters it and returns to the thumb on close.
- A polite status announces each rating and change of rating, such as "Thanks. Marked as a good answer." No animation.
