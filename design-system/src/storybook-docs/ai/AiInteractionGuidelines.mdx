<FoundationSection title="Identity and trust">

1. **One AI identity, unnamed.** The AI shows as AI, marked by the <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-icon">AI icon</DocsLink>. It has no persona name, no face or avatar, and no brand voice beyond a plain "I".
2. **Every AI answer is marked.** An <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-ai-message">AI message</DocsLink> carries the AI icon, and so does generated content placed into the product, such as a draft or a summary, until a person edits or accepts it.
3. **Fallibility, once.** One line under the <DocsLink id="ai-ui-components-prompt-input">Prompt input</DocsLink> says "AI can make mistakes. Check important info." It is not repeated on each message.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Working with people">

4. **Sources when it draws on data.** An answer that uses records lists the sources it read.
5. **People confirm actions.** The AI proposes. Anything that changes data waits for the person to confirm it.
6. **Feedback on every answer.** Each AI answer offers thumbs up and thumbs down.

</FoundationSection>
