<FoundationSection title="Overview">

Radius tokens keep corners consistent across the tutor and student apps. A control's radius is a quarter of its height, so a 24 high control takes 6, a 32 high one takes 8 and a 40 high one takes 10. Buttons are the exception: 6 default and 4 compact. Containers take the step their pattern names, and pair every radius with a focus ring that is 2 larger.

Code reads `--radius-0125` to `--radius-100` and `--radius-full` as primitives, and `--radius-sm` to `--radius-xxl`, the `--radius-control-*` group, `--card-radius`, `--modal-radius`, `--popup-radius` and the other pattern tokens as semantic tokens. Figma holds the primitives in `spacing / primitives`, the semantic tokens in `spacing / semantics` (the control group is `Radius/Control`) and `spacing / patterns`.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="About radius tokens">

Radius tokens standardize how round a corner is, so a session card, a menu and a status tag read as one family. The primitive steps are building blocks; features use the semantic tokens below.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Radius tokens and usage">

Use the token for the role, not the one that matches a pixel value. Values are read from the page.

<Banner appearance="announcement">
<strong>How PLUS rounds</strong><br />

</Banner>

<FoundationSection title="Radius token for focus state">

A component's focus state is critical for accessibility. The corner radius of the focus ring must align visually with the component it surrounds.

- Offset: the focus ring sits 2 away from the component's bounding box, so its corner radius is the component's own + 2.
- Stroke: `Border/focused` (2) in `color/border/focused`; use `color/border/focused/inverse` on a colored ground.
- In code, interactive PLUS components draw the ring for you, and the browser rounds an outline to the control's own radius.
- Containers draw no ring of their own.

<FoundationSection title="Tiles and placement">

A Tile rounds at 25% of its size. Every part keeps the radius of its own component, wherever it is placed.

<FoundationExample>

        <span key={label} style={{ display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', gap: 'var(--stack-sm)', alignItems: 'center', font: 'var(--body-small)', color: 'var(--ds-text-subtle)' }}>

        </span>

- Tile size 20 rounds at 5, 40 at 10: the radius is a quarter of the side, so the corner reads the same at every size.
- A Status tag, a Tag and a Count tag keep their own radius in a table cell, in a card and in a menu. Do not change it to suit the neighbor.

**Keep a part's radius wherever it sits.** The Status tag is 4 in the table row and 4 in the session card.

<FoundationSection title="Button radius">

Every control except a button takes a radius of its height ÷ 4: a 24 chip rounds at 6, a 32 field at 8 and a 40 field at 10. Buttons are the named exception: 6 default and 4 compact, with focus rings at 8 and 6. This covers Button, Icon button, Link button, Split button, Filter button, Close button and Vertical button. Containers keep their own tokens: Card 16, Modal 12.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Do and don't">

<FoundationSubsection title="Nested corners">

An inner shape inside a padded container rounds at the container's radius minus the padding, like a session tile inside a card, or the rows inside a menu.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Rows inset 4 inside a 12 menu take 8, so the gap between the two curves stays even.">
<Menu rowRadius="var(--radius-control-md)" />
</Do>
<Dont caption="Rows at the menu's own 12. Their corners bulge toward the menu's edge, and the inset looks uneven.">
<Menu rowRadius="var(--popup-radius)" />
</Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Match the height">

Take the radius from the height of the control, not from the nearest look.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="24 high takes 6.">
<Control height="--item-height-sm" radius="--radius-control-sm">24 high, radius 6</Control>
</Do>
<Dont caption="8 belongs to a 32 high control; on a 24 high one it looks inconsistent beside its siblings.">
<Control height="--item-height-sm" radius="--radius-lg">24 high, radius 8</Control>
</Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">

- Radius is decoration. Never use a corner shape alone to signal state or meaning.
- The focus ring must follow the control's shape and keep 3:1 contrast against its ground.
- Keep padding at least as large as the radius so text and icons do not crowd or clip at a rounded corner.

</FoundationSection>
