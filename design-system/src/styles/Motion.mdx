export const CURVES = [
    ['standard', [0.2, 0, 0, 1]],
    ['entrance', [0, 0, 0.2, 1]],
    ['exit', [0.4, 0, 1, 1]],
];

export const Curve = ({ name, points }) => {
    const [x1, y1, x2, y2] = points;
    const s = 100;
    const path = `M0 ${s} C${x1 * s} ${s - y1 * s}, ${x2 * s} ${s - y2 * s}, ${s} 0`;
    return (
        <figure style={{ margin: 0, display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', gap: 'var(--stack-sm)' }}>
            <svg viewBox="-8 -8 116 116" role="img" aria-label={`${name}: cubic-bezier(${points.join(', ')})`} style={{ width: '100%', maxWidth: 'var(--image-xl)', minWidth: 'var(--image-lg)', background: 'var(--ds-surface)', borderRadius: 'var(--radius-lg)' }}>
                <line x1="0" y1={s} x2={s} y2="0" stroke="var(--ds-border-bold)" strokeDasharray="4 4" />
                <path d={path} fill="none" stroke="var(--ds-chart-brand)" strokeWidth="3" />
            </svg>
            <figcaption style={{ font: 'var(--body-small)', color: 'var(--ds-text-subtle)' }}>
                {name}<br /><code style={{ font: 'var(--code)' }}>{points.join(', ')}</code>
            </figcaption>
        </figure>
    );
};

<FoundationSection title="Overview">

Motion in PLUS has three attributes (duration, easing and property) and four principles, tuned for a calm tutoring product. Interactions are near-instant, entrances orient people and exits get out of the way. Playful motion is kept for moments that are not part of a task.

Motion is defined by six tokens: three durations (`short`, `medium`, `long`) and three easings (`standard`, `entrance`, `exit`), written as CSS variables such as `--motion-duration-short`. Components name a duration and an easing and add nothing else. They are built for Base UI's starting and ending style transitions.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Principles">

Four principles guide every motion.

<FoundationSubsection title="Human">
Movement should feel like a supportive teammate nudging, not a machine snapping. Warmth comes from soft landings and restraint, not from extra movement.
</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Clarity">
Motion tells a tutor what changed, where it came from and what to do next. Before adding it, ask: if I remove this, does the person lose information or context? If not, remove it.
</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Accessible">
Every motion has a reduced-motion form, and nothing is communicated by motion alone.
</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Performant">
A tutor in a live session is looking at something else. Frequent things (hover, press, a row, a tab) are near-instant; rare things (finishing a module, a first-run screen) may take a moment. Motion never adds friction.
</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Duration">

Pick the duration by how often the motion happens and how much of the screen it moves. Frequent things are short, things that appear are medium, and large surfaces are long. When unsure, go shorter.

<RefTable
    head={['Token', 'Duration', 'Use']}
    rows={[
        [<C>--motion-duration-short</C>, '100 ms', 'Frequent interactions: button hover and press, list item and table row, tab, checkbox, input and field states, tooltip.'],
        [<C>--motion-duration-medium</C>, '200 ms', 'Things that appear or change in place: popup, menu, flag, accordion panel, content swaps, a skeleton fading to content, chart transitions.'],
        [<C>--motion-duration-long</C>, '300 ms', 'Large surfaces: modal, blanket and drawer. The ceiling for anything a tutor meets mid-task.'],
    ]}
/>

- Exits are shorter than entrances: a modal or drawer enters in long and leaves in medium; a popup or flag enters in medium and leaves in short.
- A tooltip hides 100 ms after the pointer leaves. That delay is a component rule, not a token.
- Spinner rotation, the Skeleton sweep and character moments are loops or one-offs outside the token set. They never run inside a task flow, and a character plays once per view.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Easing">

The curve follows direction. Arriving elements use entrance, leaving elements use exit, and everything that stays on screen (color changes, repositioning, resizing in place) uses standard.

<FoundationSection title="Patterns">

Entrances and exits are not symmetrical: entrances orient people, exits confirm a dismissal and clear the way. Use one or two properties per transition, and prefer transform and opacity to width, height and top.

<RefTable
    head={['Pattern', 'Enter', 'Exit', 'Notes']}
    rows={[
        ['Hover, press, focus', 'short, standard', 'short, standard', 'Settle color and nothing else.'],
        ['Popup and Menu', 'medium, entrance', 'short, exit', 'Fade with a short slide from the trigger side.'],
        ['Tooltip', 'short, entrance', 'short, exit', 'Fade only, never moves. Keyboard focus shows it as hover does. Hides 100 ms after the pointer leaves.'],
        ['Modal on a Blanket', 'long, entrance, together', 'medium, exit, together', 'Move focus into the Modal as the enter starts, not when it ends.'],
        ['Drawer', 'long, entrance', 'medium, exit', 'Slides from its edge. A narrow-width sidebar, if one is specified, takes the Drawer motion.'],
        ['Flag', 'medium, entrance', 'short, exit', 'Travel is a short fixed distance, not half of the flag’s width. Announce errors at once.'],
        ['Accordion and Progress', 'medium, standard', 'medium, standard', 'Animate size and are documented exceptions to the transform-and-opacity rule. A Progress fill carries a label, so scaling it would distort the text.'],
    ]}
/>

- Reduce the number of properties in one transition before you shorten its duration.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Do and don't">

<FoundationSubsection title="Keep frequent motion near-instant">

A tutor presses buttons dozens of times in a session, so the response settles color and nothing else.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="Color only, short, standard. The next action is a second away."><Label>Button hover and press · short</Label></Do>
<Dont caption="Scale or bounce on press, or a medium ease across five properties. It slows the next action."><Label>Press with scale and bounce · medium</Label></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Make exits faster than entrances">

Entrances orient people; exits get out of the way.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="A confirm opens in long and closes in medium, so the tutor is back on the roster at once."><Label>Modal enter · long</Label> <Label>Modal exit · medium</Label></Do>
<Dont caption="A modal that leaves as slowly as it arrived keeps the tutor waiting."><Label>Modal enter · long</Label> <Label>Modal exit · long</Label></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Keep characters out of tasks">

Characters move in empty states, completion, onboarding and error screens, and nowhere a tutor is mid-task.

<DoDont>
<Do caption="One short celebration in the completion modal, then the character rests."><Label>Completion character, once</Label></Do>
<Dont caption="A character moving in the In-Session toolkit, over a form or while someone types."><Label>Character waving over a notes form, repeating</Label></Dont>
</DoDont>

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">

When a person asks for reduced motion, product motion is off and instant. It is set once at the token layer.

- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every `--motion-duration-*` token becomes 0 ms, so components that read the tokens comply without a branch of their own. Jump to the final state, so nothing is left half-transparent.
- Never flash, oscillate rapidly or sweep large areas of the screen, whether or not reduced motion is on.
- Replace a rotating Spinner with a static icon and the word Loading, so the status still reads. A Skeleton becomes a flat fill with no sweep.
- Charts draw their final state with no intro. Characters show the rest pose.
- Nothing is communicated by motion alone.
- Verify every flow is fully usable with all motion turned off.

</FoundationSection>
