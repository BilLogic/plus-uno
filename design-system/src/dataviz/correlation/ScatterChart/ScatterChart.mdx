## When to use

Use `ScatterChart` to look for a relationship between two numeric measures of
the same records: minutes of tutoring against score gain per student, sessions
attended against final grade. It also shows clusters and the outliers that sit
away from them.

For a third measure shown as size, use `BubbleChart`. For so many points that
they pile on top of each other, use `HeatmapChart`. For one measure across
named categories, use `BarChart`; for one measure over time, use `LineChart`.

A scatter shows that two measures move together, not that one causes the
other. Say so in the surrounding copy when the reader might assume it.

## Data shape

`data` is required: an array of series, each `{ name, data }`, where `data`
holds `[x, y]` pairs. `height` defaults to 300.

```jsx
<ScatterChart
    xAxisLabel="Tutoring minutes per week"
    yAxisLabel="Score gain (points)"
    data={[
        { name: 'Grade 7', data: [[30, 4], [45, 7], [60, 9], [90, 12], [20, 2]] },
        { name: 'Grade 8', data: [[35, 5], [50, 6], [70, 10], [10, 1], [100, 11]] },
    ]}
/>
```

A series may carry its own `color`; leave it out and the chart assigns one.

</div>

## Color and accessibility

- **Color is assigned for you.** One series takes `--ds-chart-brand`. Two or more take the categorical order, slot 1 first, never skipping a slot. Keep to a few series; past six, group the rest or split the chart.
- **Position carries the values.** Color tells series apart, and the chart sets no marker symbol of its own, so Highcharts' default gives each series its own marker shape too. A single-series scatter reads without hue; with several series, the legend and tooltip name a point's group.
- **Dense data hides points.** Markers are solid and the same size, so overlapping points stack into one dot. There is no transparency prop; when points pile up, use `HeatmapChart`.
- **No trend line.** The chart draws the points only. If the relationship is the message, state it in the header or description.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** The chart is a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type and axes, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation through the data. The chart takes no title of its own, so name it in the Section or Card header around it.
- **Contrast.** Chart colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens.

</div>

## States

`ScatterChart` draws what it is given and has no empty, loading or error state of its own. The caller chooses what to mount in its place:

- **Loading:** a `Skeleton` the height of the chart.
- **Empty:** a compact `EmptyState` saying what data belongs here.
- **Error:** a `SectionMessage` with `appearance="danger"` and a way to retry.

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={300} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Scores could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No scores yet" description="Scores appear after a session is logged." />
    : <ScatterChart data={data} xAxisLabel="Tutoring minutes per week" yAxisLabel="Score gain (points)" />}
```

- For one measure over time rather than two against each other, use `LineChart`.
