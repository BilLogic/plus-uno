<Title />

A Jumbotron is the one hero block at the top of a landing or overview page: a page-level title, one supporting line and up to two actions, with optional media. It is a composition, not a new surface. It puts the Section header at the page level (the title at Heading/large, the description at Body/default) together with an actions slot and an optional media slot, inside a card or a full-bleed band.

## States

A Jumbotron has one state. It is static, and its buttons carry their own rest, hover, pressed, focus and disabled states. It has no loading state, because it holds static hero content.

## Rules

- **Use the title as the page title.** It is Heading/large 24. Do not swap in a Display style to make it louder.
- **Give side media 944 or more.** Below that, use top media.
- **Center only with no media or top media.** Side media keeps the text at the start.
- **Fill the media slot at its ratio.** Images are 16:9 on top and 4:3 at the side. Do not stretch them.
- **Draw button icons from the new Icon set.** They are off by default.

</div>

## When to use

Use a Jumbotron when a landing or overview page opens with one message and one next step, such as a tutor home or a program overview. For one item among many, use Card. For a message the reader has to act on, use Section message. For rotating hero slides, use Carousel.

Colors follow the toolbar's Color mode and Contrast switches: the card sits on the raised surface and the band on the surface container, in every mode.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

## Accessibility

- **The title is the page’s h1.** A page with a Jumbotron has no other h1, and its sections are h2s.
- **The description is a paragraph,** not a heading, so the outline has one entry for the hero.
- **Media is content.** Give an image alternative text, or an empty alternative when it only decorates.
- **Text and buttons keep their contrast** in the card and in the band, in every Color mode and Contrast setting.
- **The block adds no landmark.** It sits inside the page’s main region.

</div>
