<Title>Frame</Title>
Frame is the app frame: the Sidebar beside the Page and the Panel, with no top bar. It has no seams: the Page runs to the window edges and meets the Sidebar and the Panel at hairlines. Every value is a token, and values read Medium, Large and X-Large where the layout mode changes them.

The examples on this page follow the **Color mode** switch in the toolbar.

### When to use it

Use Frame for every page of the app. It owns the frame sizes and the sidebar's behavior, so a page only supplies its sidebar, its breadcrumb and its content.

For a whole page with the PLUS sidebar and breadcrumb already filled in, start from a template. For a bare fill and elevation, use **Page**. For a region inside the content, use **Section**.

### Anatomy

1. **Header** is the Page's first row, 32 high on the Sidebar logo row's center line: the breadcrumb. While the Sidebar is closed, the toggle leads it at the Page's top left.
2. **Page** is a part. It is the content box on `--ds-surface`, running the full height to the window edges, flush, with no corner radius.
3. **Content** is the slot where Sections go. It is a 12-column grid, centered and capped at 1232.
4. **Sidebar** is a part. It holds the logo row with the toggle, the groups and the account row. It docks from 1024 and never docks below that.
5. **Panel** is an area on the right, full height, holding AI chat first. It is filled with the Panel parts (`PanelContainer`, header, body, footer). Its splitter sits on its left hairline.

There is no footer row. A page that needs actions at its foot places them inside the Page.

The Sidebar and Page have their own pages. This page covers how the frame places them.

### Variants and states

#### Sidebar = docked or collapsed

The Sidebar follows the Frame's width. At Medium (768) it is collapsed and the toggle shows the bars icon. At Large and X-Large it docks, 240 wide, and the toggle shows angles-left. Clicking the toggle, or pressing Ctrl+[, closes a docked Sidebar and opens a collapsed one.

Under 1024 the Sidebar never docks: opened by hand it floats over the Page as an overlay, closes on an outside press or Escape, and the Page does not move. The Page's top-left corner is rounded only while the Sidebar is docked.

Opening a modal closes a peeking or overlay Sidebar, and a docked Sidebar stays. While a modal is open the Sidebar does not peek, and Escape closes the modal only.

#### Panel = docked or overlay

The Panel opens from anything inside the Frame through `usePanelControl()`. The layout resolver places it, from the Frame's own width, so the content never drops under 720:

- A Sidebar opened by hand stays docked, and the Panel opens as an overlay if both do not fit.
- Both dock when the Sidebar, the Panel and 720 fit. With a 400 Panel that is from 1408.
- Otherwise a Sidebar left open by default collapses, and comes back when the Panel closes. With a 400 Panel the Panel docks alone from 1168.
- Otherwise the Panel opens as an overlay: over the Page on a Blanket that covers the Page and the Sidebar. A press outside it, or Escape, closes it.
- Under 1024 the Panel is an overlay 320 wide, and only one overlay is open at a time: opening the Sidebar closes the Panel, and opening the Panel closes the Sidebar. Under 768 the Panel is full width.
- With `sectionDetail`, the Sidebar collapses first so the list and the detail keep 960 side by side.

Docked, the Panel is the Page's white and meets the Page at a hairline. Its splitter resizes it from 320 to 640, but never past what keeps 720 beside a docked Sidebar: a drag resists past the limit and springs back, and it never collapses the Sidebar. The width and whether the Panel is open are kept for the tab in sessionStorage. A modal leaves the Panel open under its Blanket. The `panelPeek` option, off by default, peeks a closed Panel when a pointer rests on the right edge, with no Blanket.

### Accessibility

- The Page is the `main` landmark, and the `nav` inside the Sidebar is the navigation landmark. The box around that `nav` is not a landmark of its own.
- The toggle is a button. Open, it ends the Sidebar's logo row as "Close sidebar"; closed, it sits at the Page's top left as "Open sidebar". It reports whether the Sidebar is showing, points at it while it is open, and announces its shortcut, Ctrl+[ (Cmd+[ on a Mac). Focus follows a press to the toggle that is left.
- A peeking or overlay Sidebar closes on Escape, and focus returns to the toggle. A control that handles its own Escape, such as a menu, keeps it.
- Opening a modal from the peeking Sidebar returns focus to the toggle when the modal closes, because the trigger left with the Sidebar.
- Docked or peeking, the Panel is a complementary landmark named by `panelLabel`, after the Page in reading order, and it leaves focus where it is. When opening it collapses the Sidebar that held focus, focus moves into the Panel; closing it returns focus to what opened it.
- As an overlay, the Panel is a modal dialog named by `panelLabel`. Opening it moves focus to its first control and Tab stays inside it; the Page and the Sidebar under the Blanket are inert, so a keyboard or screen reader cannot reach what a pointer cannot. Closing it, by Escape, a press on the Blanket or its Close button, returns focus to what opened it.
- The splitter is a vertical separator named "Resize panel" that reports its width as its value. The left arrow widens the Panel by 16 and the right arrow narrows it, 64 with Shift; Home and End go to the narrowest and the widest.
- The docked Sidebar's edge, its account-row divider and the Panel's edge are `--ds-border`.
- In light increased contrast the page draws its edge ring in `--ds-border-surface`, so it stays distinct from the frame behind it.
