<Title />
The Drawer is a full-height panel at the edge of the screen, for a task that needs the page in view: the details of a row, a set of filters or a short edit. It sits in an Overlay at the right edge or the left edge, over the same scrim a Modal takes. It carries a title, a description, a body and a footer, like a Modal. The examples on this page follow the toolbar's Color mode switch.

### Purpose

A Drawer opens a panel beside the page so a person can work on one thing while the page stays in view. It dims the page with the scrim but does not hide it.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

### When to use

- Use a Drawer for a task that needs the page in view, such as the details of a row, filters or a short edit.
- For a task that blocks the page, or needs none of it in view, use a [Modal](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-modal--docs).
- For content that belongs on the page itself, use a [Section](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-section--docs) or a [Card](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-card--docs).
- A Drawer is never wider than <code>lg</code>. Past that the page behind it is no longer in view, and the task wants a Modal or a page of its own.

</div>

The Drawer pads 16 on every side and stacks its header, body and footer 16 apart. Header and footer inline space is 8. It draws the overlay pair, `--ds-surface-overlay` with `--ds-shadow-overlay`. It has no radius, because it meets the screen edge at the top, the bottom and its own side. The title is Heading/medium 20, and the description is Body/default in the subtle text color.

### Height

A Drawer runs from the top of the screen to the bottom, so it has no hug or fill choice. The body takes what the header and footer leave and scrolls past it. The footer stays pinned to the bottom edge.

### Relation to Overlay and Modal

A Drawer is placed in the [Overlay](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-overlay--docs) with an edge placement. The Overlay drops its pad and holds the Drawer at that edge, over the same scrim and the same layers a [Modal](?path=/docs/foundations-patterns-modal--docs) takes. The Drawer follows the Modal's title, width and footer rules. One Drawer shows at a time. A confirm raised from a Drawer is a Modal in a second Overlay above it.

</div>

### Rules

- Hold the Drawer full height at the screen edge: the right by default, the left for panels that read like navigation.
- Use a Modal width step, and never go wider than <code>lg</code>.
- Always place the Drawer over the scrim, in an Overlay. Never put it straight on the page.
- Show one Drawer at a time.
- Keep the title short, and add a description only when it adds something.
- Right-align the footer actions with the primary last. The Close button always shows.
- Choose a Modal when the task blocks the page or needs none of it in view. Choose a Section when the content belongs on the page itself.

</div>

### Do and don't

None. The Drawer has no do and don't pairs.

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

### Accessibility

- The Drawer is a dialog with <code>aria-modal</code> set to true. The title is its h1 and names it through <code>aria-labelledby</code>.
- Opening moves focus into the Drawer and traps it there. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle inside.
- Escape, the Close button and a click on the scrim close it, and focus returns to the control that opened it.
- A body that scrolls is a tab stop, so a keyboard can reach it.
- The scrim is a pointer shortcut. A keyboard closes the Drawer from inside it.

</div>
