### Anatomy

- **Frame** — pads `--surface-container-pad` 8 on top, right and bottom and 0 on the left, on `--ds-surface-sunken`, and stacks its two rows `--surface-container-stack` 8 apart.
- **Topbar** — the `Topbar` pattern in the `topbar` slot, `--layout-height-topbar` 32, padded 8 on the left by the topbar itself, so the sidebar toggle sits at (8, 8) at every width and never moves. The frame owns the sidebar state and the topbar draws the one toggle from it: `angles-left` while the sidebar is docked or floating, `bars` while it is closed. Docked, the topbar's start is sidebar-wide, so the breadcrumb lines up with the surface.
- **Sidebar** — `--layout-width-sidebar` 240, its rows 4 apart. It docks from 1024 (`Layout/sidebar-docked`), padded 8 at the sides, and ends in a 1px `--ds-border` divider flush against the surface (where increased contrast draws the surface edge, the edge lies over the divider, so one line shows); the row then pads 0 on the left (`--layout-pad-left-container`), 8 while it is closed.
- **Surface** — `--ds-surface`, edged by a `--size-surface-border` ring in `--ds-border-surface` (transparent, except in light increased contrast, where the frame turns white), radius `--surface-radius`, pad `--surface-pad` 16 below 1440 and 24 from 1440.
- **Content** — a 12-column grid (`--layout-columns`) with gutter `--layout-gutter` 16, capped at `--layout-max-width-content` 1232 (reading: `--layout-max-width-reading` 864) and centered, never under `--layout-min-width-content` 720. Children span all 12 columns; set `grid-column: span N` to span fewer.

</div>

### Behavior

- **Peek** — hovering the toggle or the frame's left edge opens a closed sidebar floating over the page: `--ds-surface-overlay` with `--ds-shadow-overlay`, no scrim, and the page does not move. An outside click, Escape, or the pointer leaving closes it.
- **Under a modal** — opening a modal closes a floating sidebar, and a docked one stays. While the modal is open the sidebar does not peek, and Escape closes the modal only. Focus goes back to what opened the modal, or to the toggle when that was inside the floating sidebar.
- **Dock** — clicking the toggle docks a closed or peeking sidebar at any width, and closes a docked one. Docked below 1024, the surface is narrower than the content minimum, so it scrolls sideways while the topbar stays put.
- Crossing 1024 returns the sidebar to that width's default.
