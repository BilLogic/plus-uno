<FoundationSection title="The switch">

<SoundSwitch />

One setting turns every sound off. It is on by default and kept across visits. Nothing is opt-in, and reduced motion does not mute sound. Sound follows the device's volume.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Pairing">

Every sound has an on-screen change that says the same thing: a Flag, a step checked, a message. Sound is never the only signal, so a person who cannot hear it, or has it off, misses nothing.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="The student rule">

A sound a student can hear is good news only. A concern never plays while a student can hear, and a shared moment, such as a goal reached, plays for both.

</FoundationSection>
