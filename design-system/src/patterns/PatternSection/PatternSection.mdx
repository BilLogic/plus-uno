A section is a span of the page grid that groups content under one heading. It has no fill, no pad and no size options: the Surface around it supplies the pad. The Section header comes first, then the body, then an optional footer, Section/stack-md apart. It mirrors `Pattern/Section` in Figma.

## Related

- For one object rather than a region, use `Card`.
- For a region that needs a fill of its own, use `Surface` or `Card`. A section is transparent, and the page shows through. `padding` and `background` are deprecated: they still work, and warn once in development.
- For how a section's content lines up across breakpoints, see **Layout & Grid**.
