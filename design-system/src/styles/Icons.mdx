<FoundationSection title="Overview">

The <DocsLink id="components-images-and-icons-icon">Icon</DocsLink> component renders a single glyph; components with icon slots (`Button`'s `iconBefore` and `iconAfter`) pass the glyph name through `Icon` (`fa-regular fa-<name>`, falling back to `fa-solid` where Font Awesome Free has no regular weight). A React node in place of the name renders as given.

```jsx
<Icon name="calendar-days" size={16} color="subtle" />
<Button appearance="primary" text="Add tutor" iconBefore="plus" />
```

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Principles">

<FoundationSubsection title="Use one recognized symbol per meaning">
Pick the plainest glyph most people read the same way (calendar for dates, bell for notifications, gear for settings) and keep one glyph per meaning across the product.

<Story of={IconsStories.OneGlyphOneMeaning} />
</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Pair icons with text labels">
A label states what the icon does and names it for screen readers. Leave an icon unlabeled only for a familiar action in a tight space, and give the control an accessible `label` in code.

<Story of={IconsStories.PairWithLabels} />
</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Sizes: 12 and 16">

A plain icon is 12 or 16, bound to `Size/Icon` tokens. An unsized glyph is 16, and it never scales with the text around it, headings included. `--fa-width: 1em` is set globally so every glyph fills its square box without adding line height. Angles and chevrons are always 12. Add breathing room with spacing tokens, never a larger icon size.

<Canvas of={IconsStories.Sizes} />

The swatches sit in a 48 preview frame for display; the icon's own box is its size, and the hit area comes from the control around it.

<FoundationSection title="Above 16, use a Tile">

16 is the top of the plain icon scale. A larger mark, tool logo or empty-state symbol belongs in a <DocsLink id="components-images-and-icons-tile">Tile</DocsLink> (16 to 48), which sets its size, radius and fill together and picks the glyph inside: 12 at Tile 16/20, 16 at Tile 24/32, 24 at Tile 40/48.

A glyph in a heading is a plain 16; it does not scale with the heading. A bigger mark beside a heading is a Tile placed on purpose and sized to the heading's line height: about Tile 32 for an h1, Tile 24 for an h2.

<Canvas of={IconsStories.LargerMeansTile} />

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Tile or bare icon">

A plain icon's box is the square of its size: 12 or 16. That box only sets how big the glyph draws. A container around it — a button, a field, a list row or a Tile — sets the padding, the background and the hit area. A clickable icon's hit area always comes from its control, never from the glyph.

- **Bare icon:** use when the glyph sits inline with text or inside a control such as a button, field, menu or list row, with no background of its own.
- **Tile:** use when the glyph stands for a thing (a file kind, a tool or a category), needs a colored ground, or needs to read larger than 16, as in empty states. Tile colors mark categories, never statuses, so a status icon (error, queued, success) stays bare even in a slot where Tiles sit.

There is no 20 or 24 plain icon. Plain icon sizes are 12 and 16 only. Tile sizes are a separate scale, and `Tile` picks the glyph size for each.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Font Awesome Free and color tokens">

Use Font Awesome Free 7.2 only: `fa-solid`, `fa-regular` and `fa-brands` (such as `fa-brands fa-figma` and `fa-brands fa-github`). Pro families (`fa-light`, `fa-thin`, `fa-sharp`, `fa-duotone`) and Pro-only names are unlicensed and render blank, so check a glyph name against the Free set first. Regular is the default weight; solid is used where Free has no regular glyph. Where one set of icons needs a glyph Free draws only in solid, that one glyph is solid and the rest stay regular. Always use current glyph names rather than legacy aliases (`calendar-days`, not `calendar-alt`).

<Canvas of={IconsStories.Families} />

Color icons with `--ds-icon-*` tokens (`--ds-icon-success`, `--ds-icon-danger`, or the text color beside the icon), never a raw hex value.

<Canvas of={IconsStories.Colors} />

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Do and don't">

<FoundationSubsection title="Use Free families and Free names only">

Solid, regular and brands are licensed here, including brand glyphs.

<DoDontStory of={IconsStories.FreeFamiliesOnly} doCaption="Free families (fa-regular, fa-solid, fa-brands) render everywhere." dontCaption="Pro families (fa-light, fa-thin, fa-sharp, fa-duotone) render blank." />

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Use 16 by default, 12 sparingly">

Icons are 16 beside body text. Keep 12 for angles, chevrons, validation and compact parts.

An angle opens a list or menu; a chevron expands content in place.

<DoDontStory of={IconsStories.IconSizingInControls} doCaption="A 16 icon leads the label; the trailing angle is 12." dontCaption="A 12 leading icon is too small beside 14 body text." />

</FoundationSubsection>

<FoundationSubsection title="Use a symbol people recognize">

Pick the glyph most people read the same way.

<DoDontStory of={IconsStories.RecognizableSymbols} doCaption="A bell for notifications." dontCaption="A gear reads as settings, so it misleads here." />

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Accessibility">

- Decorative icons and icons beside a visible text label carry `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Icon-only controls (`IconButton`) require a concise `label` prop so screen readers announce the action.
- `--ds-icon-*` tokens clear the 3:1 non-text contrast bar against their paired surfaces (<DocsLink id="foundations-visual-color">Color</DocsLink>).

</FoundationSection>
