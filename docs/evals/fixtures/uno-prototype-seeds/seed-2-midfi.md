# Seed PRD 2 (mid-fi ask) — "Museum exhibit-route planner"

Add a route planner visitors can use before entering a museum. Build an interactive draft to validate how visitors shape and revise a proposed route.

Requirements:
- Visitors enter their available time, interests, and mobility preferences
- The planner proposes an ordered route through matching exhibits
- Visitors can drag exhibits to reorder the route and see its duration update
