## Usage

Use `Flag` to confirm something that has already happened and needs no reply:
saved, sent, copied. `show` and `onDismiss` are yours; the component holds no
state. `FlagGroup` positions a stack of them.

### Appearance guidance

## Parts

1. **Icon.** The appearance glyph, decorative.
2. **Title.** What happened, in a few words.
3. **Timestamp.** Optional, subtle, after the title.
4. **Description.** At most one line.
5. **Actions.** Link buttons under the description.
6. **Expand toggle.** Only on a bold flag, which starts collapsed to its title.
7. **Dismiss.** The shared CloseButton, named "Dismiss flag".

## Best practices

### Match the appearance to the outcome.

Success for a finished action; error only when something failed.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Flag appearance="success" title="Session saved" autohide={false}>Changes appear for everyone on the session.</Flag>}
    doCaption="Success appearance for a saved session."
    dontExample={<Flag appearance="error" title="Session saved" autohide={false}>Changes appear for everyone on the session.</Flag>}
    dontCaption="Error appearance on good news."
/>

### Keep bold for the one message that must be seen.

A bold flag competes with the page; routine news stays quiet.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Flag appearance="information" defaultExpanded title="Notes synced" autohide={false}>Changes appear for everyone on the session.</Flag>}
    doCaption="Quiet flag for routine news."
    dontExample={<Flag appearance="information" bold defaultExpanded title="Notes synced" autohide={false}>Changes appear for everyone on the session.</Flag>}
    dontCaption="Bold fill on a routine confirmation."
/>

### Say what happened and what to do next.

A title names the event; the description gives the next step.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Flag appearance="error" title="Couldn't reschedule">Pick another time and try again.</Flag>}
    doCaption="Event in the title, next step below."
    dontExample={<Flag appearance="error" title="Error" />}
    dontCaption="A bare title with no next step."
/>

### Show a background failure as an error Flag with Try again.

When work fails out of sight, such as a sync or an autosave, use an error flag.
It stays until closed and carries **Try again** as its action, so the person can
retry without leaving what they are doing. A failure to load a view is not a
flag: it takes the view's `EmptyState`.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<Flag appearance="error" title="Couldn't sync notes" actions={[{ text: 'Try again' }]}>Your latest changes are saved on this device only.</Flag>}
    doCaption="Error flag with Try again; it stays until closed."
    dontExample={<Flag appearance="error" title="Couldn't sync notes" autohide delay={3000} />}
    dontCaption="A failure that times out before it is read."
/>

### Nothing important goes only in a flag.

The user can dismiss a flag before reading it, and that takes the only copy
with it. A rejected form submit stays on the form, in a danger `SectionMessage`
at the top with an error on each field.

### Turn off autohide for anything the user might need to re-read.

With `autohide={false}`, or `delay={0}`, the flag waits for its close button.

### Keep dismissible on.

It is on by default and is the only way to close a flag that is not timing out.
Turning it off with `autohide={false}` leaves a flag that never goes away.

## Accessibility

- **Only warning and error interrupt.** Those two appearances are `role="alert"`
  with `aria-live="assertive"`. Every other appearance is `role="status"` with
  `aria-live="polite"`, so a success flag waits for the screen reader to finish
  what it is saying. Every appearance is `aria-atomic`, so the flag is read as one
  message.
- **The timeout is a timing limit.** Content that disappears on its own has to
  be dismissible or extendable to satisfy WCAG's timing rule. The close button
  is what does that here, which is why it is on by default.
- **Announcement needs the region to already exist.** A flag mounted at the same
  moment as its container may not be announced at all. Render `FlagGroup`
  with the page and put flags into it.
- **The dismiss button is the shared `CloseButton`** in its default tone on the
  flag's surface, with an `aria-hidden` ×. The × and its focus ring are at least
  3:1 on that surface.
- **The appearance icon is decorative.** It is `aria-hidden`, so the title beside it
  carries the meaning; the color and icon should never carry it alone.
- **Every flag is one surface.** The title and the description keep the same
  text colors on every appearance, so no appearance needs a contrast exception.
