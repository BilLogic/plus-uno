## When to use

Use `AreaChart` when the amount over time is the point: tutoring hours logged
across a semester, sessions per week split by subject, enrolled students
stacked by grade. Stack the series (`stacking="normal"`) to show parts adding
up to a total, or as shares of 100% (`stacking="percent"`).

For a trend where the area under the line means nothing, use `LineChart`.
For precise comparison between periods, use `BarChart`. For composition with
no meaningful zero baseline, use `StreamgraphChart`. For a band between a low
and a high, use `AreaRangeChart`.

Unstacked areas overlap and hide each other. Keep those to two or three series,
or stack them.

## Data shape

`data` is an array of series, each `{ name, data }`, where `data` holds one
number per period. Put the period labels on the first series as `categories`.
`stacking` is `'normal'`, `'percent'` or `null` (the default, overlapping).

```jsx
<AreaChart
    xAxisLabel="Week"
    yAxisLabel="Sessions"
    stacking="normal"
    data={[
        { name: 'Math', data: [40, 46, 52, 55], categories: ['Wk 1', 'Wk 2', 'Wk 3', 'Wk 4'] },
        { name: 'Reading', data: [30, 32, 35, 41] },
        { name: 'Science', data: [12, 15, 18, 20] },
    ]}
/>
```

A series may carry its own `color`; leave it out and the chart assigns one.
`height` defaults to 300.

## Color and accessibility

- **Color is assigned for you.** One series takes `--ds-chart-brand`. Two or more take the categorical order, slot 1 first, never skipping a slot. Past six series, group the smallest into "Other".
- **Height carries the value, but only the bottom layer reads off the axis.** In a stacked chart every upper layer floats on the ones below it, so its own size is hard to judge. Put the series that matters most at the bottom, and use the shared tooltip for exact values.
- **Color is the only thing separating areas.** Every layer is a plain fill with no pattern, so keep the series count low enough that the legend can name each one.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** The chart is a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type and axes, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation through the data. The chart takes no title of its own, so name it in the Section or Card header around it.
- **Contrast.** Chart colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens.

## States

`AreaChart` draws what it is given and has no empty, loading or error state of its own. The caller chooses what to mount in its place:

- **Loading:** a `Skeleton` the height of the chart.
- **Empty:** a compact `EmptyState` saying what data belongs here.
- **Error:** a `SectionMessage` with `appearance="danger"` and a way to retry.

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={300} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Sessions could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No sessions yet" description="Sessions appear here once they are logged." />
    : <AreaChart data={data} stacking="normal" />}
```
