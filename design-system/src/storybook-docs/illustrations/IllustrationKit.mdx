<FoundationSection title="Fidelity">

Pick each example's fidelity by what the lesson depends on. Remove every word in your head: if the lesson still holds, draw it blocked out.

<RefTable
    head={['Fidelity', 'When the lesson is', 'How to draw it']}
    rows={[
        ['Concrete', 'A component’s look, states, behavior or wording (anatomy, states, content do and don’t, interactions).', 'Render a live story with real content, not a diagram.'],
        ['Real subject', 'One element inside a screen (which action is primary, a label, or one piece’s placement).', 'Draw that element with real words; keep the surrounding context blocked out.'],
        ['Blocked out', 'Structure across screens (layout options, which surface opens, alignment, phases, or variants side by side).', 'Use no words inside the figure; draw buttons as flat strips and put explanations in the caption.'],
    ]}
/>

Motion follows the same split: a component's own motion is a live story, a principle (easing, choreography, stagger) animates blocked-out shapes, and one element moving inside a screen animates the real subject over a blocked-out context.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Drawing rules">

- Paint with design-system tokens only: color, stroke and corner. Each part uses the token its real component uses from the role map in `paint.js`, so diagrams follow light and dark automatically.
- Show an edge on every surface against the hero's sunken ground. Flat layers take one hairline in `illustrationEdge` (halved in `HeroDrawing` so it stays one device pixel); layers with a shadow (Page, Card, Popup, Flag) read by that shadow. The real page edge is transparent outside increased contrast and the divider is about 1.1:1 on the ground, so neither is used. A page, screen or frame takes the `window` corner; a card, popup and modal take their own roles. `HeroEdges.test.jsx` holds edges to 1.5:1 against the ground in light and dark and fails any registered hero with a bare surface.
- Draw text as soft `--ds-skeleton` bars. Never use black or dark bars.
- Draw buttons only in shipped appearances: primary (the brand fill) on the subject, and default (the neutral fill) everywhere else.
- Keep one scale per set, equal margins around every drawing, and at most one accent per drawing, placed on the subject.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Primitives">

Each primitive takes a position (`x`, `y`), size (`w`, `h`) and `accent` flag. The top drawing is neutral and the bottom is accented (Button shows blocked-out above and real below). A card paints the raised surface and shadow, a Tooltip is flat with no shadow, and a Sticky header casts the overflow shadow over rows that scroll under it. Screen combines a Frame, Sidebar and Page, with the Sidebar docked, an overlay or none. Field also takes `invalid`, which paints its border with the error token. Flag takes `tone` (neutral, success, warning, danger or info). Tabs, Breadcrumb and Progress tracker take `count`; Tabs also takes `selected` and Progress tracker `current`. The gallery ends with the four motion primitives from `primitives-motion.jsx` and the six cover shapes from `shapes.jsx`.

<FoundationSection title="Contributing">

Every docs drawing, static or animated, comes from this one kit, and the motion layer lives beside the primitives in this folder.

- Extend an existing primitive before adding a new one.
- A new primitive takes `x`, `y`, `w`, `h` and `accent` (plus `label` when it supports real fidelity) and paints from `paint.js` only.
- Add a gallery entry (neutral above, accented below) and keep `IllustrationKit.stories.jsx` passing.
- Move animated primitives on the spring tokens and settle to a still frame under reduced motion.

</FoundationSection>
