<FoundationSection title="The land rule">

A sound starts at its motion token's 90% time minus its pickup, so its first note lands as the motion reads as arrived. Under reduced motion it lands with the reduced motion instead.

<LandRuleFigure caption="Top: a Flag's spatial.default spring. Bottom: Notify, its pickup notches, then its notes. The darker dashed line is the spring's 90%; the lighter one is where the sound starts." />

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="On a Flag">

A Flag slides in from the top right and Notify lands with it.

<Canvas of={FlagStories.LandsWithNotify} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Expressive moments">

Sound follows motion's Expressive moments and adds none. Step plays on every Finish, and Milestone on every Milestone. The list of those moments lives in Motion · Physics.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="In code">

```js
import { playSound, soundStartFor } from '@/components';

playSound('notify', { at: soundStartFor('spatial.default', 'notify') });
```

Call it when the motion starts. Pass `{ mode: 'reduced' }` or `{ mode: 'expressive' }` to time it to that mode's spring.

</FoundationSection>
