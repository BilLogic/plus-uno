<FoundationSection title="Drawing rules">

- Paint with design-system tokens only: color, stroke and corner. Each part uses the token its real component uses for that role, from the role map in `paint.js`. The kit follows light and dark on its own.
- A surface on a hero's sunken ground always shows its edge. The real edge roles are too faint there (the page edge is transparent outside increased contrast, the divider is about 1.1:1 on the ground), so a flat layer takes one hairline in `illustrationEdge`, the input border, and a layer with a real shadow (a page, a card, a popup) reads by that shadow, as the real ones do. A hero draws a unit larger than the kit does, so `HeroDrawing` halves the hairline and every line is one device pixel. A page, screen or frame takes the `window` corner; a card, popup and modal take their own roles. `HeroEdges.test.jsx` holds the role to 1.5:1 against the ground in light and dark (the base modes) and fails any registered hero with a bare surface.
- Pick each example's fidelity by what the lesson depends on. Remove every word in your head: if the lesson still holds, draw it abstract.
  - **Concrete:** the lesson is a component's look, states, behavior or wording, such as anatomy, states, content do's and don'ts, or an interaction. Use a live story with real content, not a drawing.
  - **Real subject:** the lesson is one element inside a screen, such as which action is primary, a label, or one piece's placement. Draw that element real, with its words, and keep everything around it blocked out.
  - **Blocked out:** the lesson is structure across screens, such as layout options, which surface opens, alignment, phases, or many variants side by side. Use no words, and draw buttons as flat strips.
  - Motion follows the same split. A component's own motion is a live story. A principle such as easing, choreography or stagger animates blocked-out shapes. One element moving inside a screen animates the real subject over a blocked-out context.
- Draw skeleton bars where text would sit. Words appear only on a real subject; the rest goes in the caption beside the drawing.
- Draw buttons only in shipped appearances: primary, the brand fill, on the subject; default, the neutral fill, everywhere else.
- Keep one scale per set and equal margins around every drawing, balanced by eye.
- Give each drawing one accent, and place it on the subject.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Primitives">

Each primitive takes a position, a size and an `accent` flag. Every part paints with the token its real component uses: a card with the raised surface and shadow, a text line with the Skeleton bar, a Flag with the overlay shadow, a Tooltip flat with no shadow, a Sticky header with the overflow shadow over rows that scroll under it. The top drawing is neutral and the bottom one is accented. Button shows its two fidelities instead: blocked out on top, real below. Screen is a Frame, a Sidebar and a Page, with the Sidebar docked, an overlay or none. Field also takes `invalid`, which paints its border with the error token; the gallery shows only neutral and accent. Flag's `tone` is neutral, success, warning, danger or info (the accented drawing shows success). Tabs, Breadcrumb and Progress tracker take a `count`; Tabs also takes `selected` and Progress tracker `current`. The last four, Chart axis and curve, Trail, Tap ring and Pointer, are motion's own: they live in `primitives-motion.jsx`, and `motion.jsx` animates them. Six more, Timeline, Waveform, Isometric stack, Logo construction, Spring trail and Path, are the cover shapes in `shapes.jsx`: the Logo construction draws from the same geometry file as the Logo docs, the Path's arrowhead stops at its target's edge, and the arithmetic is tested in `shapes-geometry.test.js`.

<FoundationSection title="Contributing">

Every docs drawing, static or animated, comes from this one kit. Template, pattern and motion pages all draw from it and add to it.

- Check the primitives above first. Extend one before you add another, the same way components grow.
- A new primitive takes `x`, `y`, `w`, `h` and `accent`, plus `label` where it has a real fidelity. It paints from `paint.js` only.
- Give it a gallery entry, neutral above and accented below, and keep the kit story test passing.
- Animated drawings move kit primitives on the spring tokens and settle to a still frame under reduced motion. The motion layer lives beside the primitives in this folder.

</FoundationSection>
