`FocusedPage` is the **page template for one task**: no sidebar, a topbar with Back and Close, the content in a reading column, and the actions in a footer. It is the `Frame` with no sidebar and a footer row. It mirrors the Figma `Pattern/Focused page`, with its parts `_Focused topbar` and `_Focused footer`.

### Anatomy

- **Frame** — the `Frame` with no sidebar: `--ds-surface-sunken`, pad `--surface-container-pad` 8, and three rows `--surface-container-stack` 8 apart.
- **Topbar** — a `header`, the page's banner, `--layout-height-topbar` 32. Back on the start (a secondary text `Button` with `arrow-left`), the title in the middle, and Close on the end (the `CloseButton`), `--element-inline-lg` 12 apart. The title is the page's one `h1`, at Heading/xsmall, on one line, truncated when it runs long.
- **Surface** — the frame's surface at reading width, and the page's `main`: its content is capped at `--layout-max-width-reading` 864, centered, and never under `--layout-min-width-content` 720. A long task scrolls inside it.
- **Footer** — a `footer` named "Page actions": the actions end-aligned, `--size-element-gap-sm` 8 apart, in a column as wide as the content and lined up with it. It stays put while the surface scrolls.

</div>

<div className="sb-ds-doc-section">

### Behavior

- **Back** returns to where the person came from: pass `onBack`, or `backHref` for a link. **Close** leaves the task: pass `onClose`. Without them there is no Back or no Close; if both would go to the same place, keep Close only.
- **Focus order** follows the page: Back, Close, the content, then the actions.
- Without `actions` there is no footer, and the surface takes the space.

## Usage

- **Use a focused page** for one task that takes more than a screen or a few minutes, which the person finishes or leaves, and where the rest of the app would only distract: writing a session note, setting up a session, reviewing an import.
- **Use `PageLayout`** when the person moves among pages: browse, compare, come back. It keeps the sidebar and the breadcrumb.
- **Use a `Modal`** for a short decision or a few fields that keep the page behind them in view. If it scrolls for long or has sections, it is a focused page.
- **Do** name the task in the title ("Add a session note"), and end the footer with the one primary action.
- **Don't** add a sidebar, a breadcrumb or the account to a focused page, and don't put several primary actions in the footer.
