<FoundationSection title="The patterns">

Seven pages and one group of six States pages. Each pattern is one page: its situations first, then the key cases drawn on templates, then the usage spine. Reach for one when you are unsure of a choice or challenging it; <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/templates-overview--docs">Templates and patterns</a> says how the layers fit together.

<ThumbTile hero="patterns-common-actions" title="Common actions">Which button, in which look, in which place, with which word, and acting on many rows at once.</ThumbTile>
<ThumbTile hero="patterns-filtering" title="Filtering" span="wide">How a person narrows a collection to the rows that matter, in five tiers.</ThumbTile>

<ThumbTile hero="patterns-search" title="Search" span="wide">How a person finds a record by typing, in one of three modes.</ThumbTile>
<ThumbTile hero="patterns-indicators" title="Indicators">The small labels that say how something is: its state, how many, how much it moved.</ThumbTile>
<ThumbTile hero="patterns-notifications" title="Notifications">What the product says back, and where: a Flag, a Banner, a SectionMessage, the Inbox.</ThumbTile>
<ThumbTile hero="patterns-states-empty" title="States · Empty">What a view shows when it has nothing to show.</ThumbTile>
