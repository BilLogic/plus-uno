## When to use

Use `AreaChart` when volume over time matters, such as tutoring hours across a
semester, weekly sessions by subject or enrolled students by grade. Set
`stacking="normal"` to stack parts into a total, or `stacking="percent"` for
shares of 100%; keep unstacked (`stacking={null}`) charts to two or three
series so overlapping fills stay legible.

When the area under the line has no meaning, use `LineChart`. For exact period
comparisons, use `BarChart`. For composition without a zero baseline, use
`StreamgraphChart`. For a low-to-high band, use `AreaRangeChart`.

## Data shape

`data` is an array of `{ name, data, categories, color }` series, with period
labels passed on the first series' `categories`. `stacking` is `'normal'`,
`'percent'` or `null` (default, overlapping). `height` defaults to 300. Omit
`color` to assign one automatically.

```jsx
<AreaChart
    xAxisLabel="Week"
    yAxisLabel="Sessions"
    stacking="normal"
    data={[
        { name: 'Math', data: [40, 46, 52, 55], categories: ['Wk 1', 'Wk 2', 'Wk 3', 'Wk 4'] },
        { name: 'Reading', data: [30, 32, 35, 41] },
        { name: 'Science', data: [12, 15, 18, 20] },
    ]}
/>
```

## Color and accessibility

- **Automatic series color.** One series uses `--ds-chart-brand`; two or more follow the [categorical order](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-color--docs) from slot 1 without skipping. Past six series, group the smallest into "Other".
- **Put the primary series at the bottom.** In stacked charts only the bottom layer sits on the zero baseline; upper layers float on moving baselines and rely on the shared tooltip for exact values.
- **Plain fills.** Because layers have no pattern, keep the series count low enough for the legend to identify each one.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** Renders a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type and axes, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation. Name the chart in its surrounding Section or Card header.
- **Contrast.** Colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens ([Foundations](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-foundations--docs)).

## States

`AreaChart` has no empty, loading or error state. Mount a `Skeleton` matching the chart's height, a compact `EmptyState` naming the missing data, or a `SectionMessage` (`appearance="danger"`) with a retry action:

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={300} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Sessions could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No sessions yet" description="Sessions appear here once they are logged." />
    : <AreaChart data={data} stacking="normal" />}
```

## Related

- For a trend where the total under the line means nothing, use `LineChart`.
- For composition over time with no meaningful zero baseline, use `StreamgraphChart`.
- For a band between a low and a high rather than a fill to zero, use `AreaRangeChart`.
- For parts of a total in a few separate periods, use `StackedBarChart`.
