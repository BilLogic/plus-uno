<FoundationSection title="Overview">

Tokens form a ladder. A primitive is a raw value, such as a palette step or a spacing step, and is never used in a feature. A semantic token names a job and points at a primitive: Global tokens apply anywhere, Components tokens belong to one component, and Templates tokens belong to the page frame. A screen binds the semantic token whose job matches, so one decision is made once and every mode follows.

Search matches part of a name; turn on Exact match to find one whole name. The filters and the search combine, and the count shows how many tokens remain. Select a name to copy it. A color role shows its Light and Dark value.
