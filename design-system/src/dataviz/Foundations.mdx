<FoundationSection title="Chart container and tooltip">

A chart sits in a Card: <C>--card-pad-compact</C> sets the chart inset, and the chart adds no outer padding of its own. The tooltip uses the Popup pattern's surface, shadow, radius, padding and layer tokens rather than built-in SVG shadow or corner options.

<FoundationSection title="Stroke">

Gridlines, axes, series lines and chart separators (such as gaps between stacked bar segments) use two component-tier stroke tokens:

<RefTable
    head={['Role', 'Token']}
    rows={[
        ['Gridline / axis line', <C>--chart-grid-width</C>],
        ['Series line, whisker, marker ring, segment separator', <C>--chart-line-width</C>],
    ]}
/>

Removing a border or line with `0` needs no token.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Type and numbers">

Every text slot in a chart — title, subtitle, caption, axis labels and titles, legend items, data labels, tooltips and empty-data notices — sets the Noto Sans body family (<C>--type-family-body</C>) and current text tokens — never a <C>'12px'</C> literal or a <C>var()</C> with a literal fallback. Titles and emphasized values use semibold (<C>--type-weight-semibold</C>, 600); labels and supporting text use medium (<C>--type-weight-medium</C>, 500). Nothing renders at 700 or 300, data-label text outlines stay off, and number slots set tabular figures (<C>tabular-nums</C>) so values align digit for digit without switching to a monospace font. Inside a tooltip, text stays in <C>--ds-text</C> while only the leading dot swatch takes the series color.

<Canvas of={FoundationsStories.TypeAndNumbers} sourceState="none" />

    rows={[
        ['Title and axis titles', <><C>--type-family-body</C> · <C>--type-weight-semibold</C> (600) · <C>--ds-text</C>; name the chart in its surrounding Section or Card header rather than an in-chart title</>],
        ['Subtitle, caption and no-data notice', <><C>--type-family-body</C> · <C>--type-weight-medium</C> (500) · <C>--ds-text-subtle</C></>],
        ['Axis tick labels', <><C>--type-family-body</C> · <C>--type-size-075</C> · <C>--type-weight-medium</C> (500) · <C>tabular-nums</C> · <C>--ds-text-subtle</C> — never a <C>'12px'</C> literal or a <C>var()</C> with a literal fallback</>],
        ['Data labels and legend items', <><C>--type-family-body</C> · <C>--type-size-075</C> · <C>--type-weight-medium</C> (500) · <C>tabular-nums</C> · no text outline · <C>--ds-text</C></>],

    ]}
/>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="States">

Interactive states are declared once in the shared theme so every chart responds the same way. Hovering a mark shifts its fill to that mark's <C>-hovered</C> chart token while the other series fade to <C>--ds-opacity-disabled</C>. Selecting a mark paints <C>--ds-background-selected</C> with a 1px <C>--ds-border-selected</C> outline, never a brand fill.

<Canvas of={FoundationsStories.States} sourceState="none" />

<RefTable
    head={['State', 'Token / rule']}
    rows={[
        ['Hover', <>The mark's <C>--chart-*-hovered</C> token (for example <C>--chart-categorical-1-hovered</C> or <C>--chart-brand-hovered</C>) on <C>appearance.fast</C></>],
        ['Inactive series', <><C>--ds-opacity-disabled</C> on non-hovered series while another series is hovered, on <C>appearance.fast</C></>],
        ['Selected', <><C>--ds-background-selected</C> fill with a <C>--chart-grid-width</C> (1px) <C>--ds-border-selected</C> outline, never <C>--chart-brand</C></>],
    ]}
/>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Motion">

Charts grow to their value on the default spatial spring (<C>spatial.default</C>), and hover or inactive state changes fade on <C>appearance.fast</C>. Under reduced motion, every entrance becomes the <C>appearance.fast</C> fade. Charts in one area arrive 20 ms apart within half a second, and nothing replays on redraw, resize or data update after the first render.

<Canvas of={FoundationsStories.Motion} sourceState="none" />

</FoundationSection>
