## When to use

Use `WordCloud` for a quick, impressionistic read of open text such as student
session feedback, tutor notes or survey responses. Strip stop words (`the`,
`and`, `of`) beforehand and cap the list at the top 50 to 100 words so smaller
terms remain legible.

When exact counts or comparisons matter, use `BarChart`. For terms grouped by
category and sized, use `PackedBubbleChart`. For co-occurring terms, use
`NetworkGraph`.

## Data shape

`data` is an array of `{ name, weight }` (both required), where `name` is the
word and `weight` is its count.

```jsx
<WordCloud
    data={[
        { name: 'Patient', weight: 42 },
        { name: 'Encouraging', weight: 35 },
        { name: 'Clear', weight: 28 },
        { name: 'Prepared', weight: 19 },
        { name: 'Fun', weight: 12 },
    ]}
/>
```

## Color and accessibility

- **Decorative color.** Words cycle through the [categorical order](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-color--docs) from `--ds-chart-categorical-1` and repeat past eight; hue encodes neither category nor count.
- **Word length distorts perceived weight.** Longer words occupy more area than shorter words of equal weight; provide exact counts in the tooltip, a table or surrounding copy when precision matters.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** Renders a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation. Name the chart in its surrounding Section or Card header.
- **Contrast.** Colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens ([Foundations](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-foundations--docs)).

</div>

## States

`WordCloud` has no empty, loading or error state. Mount a `Skeleton` matching the chart's height, a compact `EmptyState` naming the missing data, or a `SectionMessage` (`appearance="danger"`) with a retry action:

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={400} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Feedback could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No feedback yet" description="Words appear after students rate a session." />
    : <WordCloud data={data} />}
```
