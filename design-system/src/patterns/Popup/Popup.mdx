## Anatomy

A filter menu off the Filter icon. Every part once; the layer is padded 16 and rounded 12 on the overlay pair.

<Story of={PopupStories.AnatomyFilterMenu} inline />

1. **Trigger.** The control it hangs off. It shows the open state and, for the Filter icon, the count.
2. **Layer.** Hangs 4 below the trigger, aligned to the trigger's end edge because the trigger sits at the end of the bar.
3. **Rows or categories.** Menu rows; a list of checkboxes or radios; columns or stacked categories.
4. **Footer** (filter menu only). Reset (Default) and Apply (Primary), full bleed, half each.

## Usage

Use a Popup for content anchored to a trigger that leaves on an outside click or Escape: a menu of actions, a list to pick from, the filter menu behind the Filter icon, the profile menu, a short aside. It brings no Backdrop and the page stays usable.

## Best practices

### Hang a Popup 4 below its trigger, aligned to the trigger's edge on the side where it sits.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

A trigger at the start of a bar, such as the view picker under page tabs, gets a layer aligned to its start edge; a trigger at the end, such as Filter or More, gets one aligned to its end edge. So it grows into the page and stays on it. It flips above when there is no room below and stays 8 from the screen edge.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<EndAligned />}
    doCaption="More sits at the end of the bar, so its menu aligns to its end edge and grows into the page."
    dontExample={<StartAlignedAtEnd />}
    dontCaption="An end trigger with a start-aligned menu: it runs off the edge of the page."
/>

### Make a menu at least as wide as its trigger, from 160 up to 320.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

It hugs the longest item. The width is set once, on open, so it holds still while someone types. A long or loading list opens at 320. A long row: <DocsLink id="patterns-states-overflow">Overflow</DocsLink>.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<AsWideAsTrigger />}
    doCaption="The menu is at least as wide as its trigger, so their edges line up."
    dontExample={<NarrowerThanTrigger />}
    dontCaption="A menu narrower than the trigger it hangs off: the edges no longer line up."
/>

### Cap a Popup at 4-col.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Anything wider is a Panel or a Modal. Anything with inputs the person must finish, steps or a long scroll belongs in a Modal.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<AtCap />}
    doCaption="A content Popup at 4-col, the cap."
    dontExample={<WiderThanCap />}
    dontCaption="A Popup wider than 4-col: anything this wide is a Panel or a Modal."
/>

### Put fields the person must finish in a Modal.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

An outside click closes a Popup and loses what was typed.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<FieldsInModal />}
    doCaption="Fields to finish go in a Modal, over its Backdrop."
    dontExample={<FormInPopup />}
    dontCaption="A form in a Popup: an outside click loses what was typed."
/>

### Open one Popup at a time, each off its own trigger.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

A confirm Modal may open over a Popup and brings its own Backdrop. A Popup opened from a Modal sits above it; a Tooltip sits above everything.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<OneFlatMenu />}
    doCaption="One flat menu; a choice with two forms is two rows."
    dontExample={<PopupBesidePopup />}
    dontCaption="A menu opening a second Popup beside it: two layers off one trigger."
/>

### Leave the Backdrop to the Modal.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

A Popup floats on the overlay pair, `--ds-surface-overlay` with `--ds-shadow-overlay`, and the page behind it stays usable.

### Keep a menu's label short and move the detail to its description.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Start with the verb: "Reschedule", "Export notes". An option that waits on something says why in its description line, as visible text.

When a list applies, and the words Reset and Reset filters: <DocsLink id="patterns-filtering">Filtering</DocsLink>.

## Accessibility

- The trigger carries `aria-haspopup` and an `aria-expanded` that tracks the open state. A Filter icon's name says what is on: "Filters, 2 applied".
- A menu moves focus to its first row; Up and Down move, and Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger. A content Popup is a popup, so focus stays where it was and Tab reaches what is inside.
- A select list is a listbox or a group of checkboxes, and the chosen state is announced. The labeled view trigger reads "Status, Upcoming".
- In a filter menu, Reset and Apply are reachable after the categories. Apply announces the new count in words.
- A "Loading options" row is announced once, and "Couldn't load options" offers a Retry button.
