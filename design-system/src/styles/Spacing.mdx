<Title />

Spacing tokens name a job, not a size. Pick the pattern you are in (Surface,
Section, Card, Modal, Popover, Table, or an Element inside a component), then
the relationship (**pad** inside a box, **stack** between rows, **inline**
between things side by side), then the size. So the gap between a card's
title and its body is `--card-stack-md`, wherever the card sits. Radius and
the size roles have pages of their own.

### Principles

Five principles decide which tier a gap takes. Similar things share one gap,
a new group gets a bigger one, tiers grow from the component to the page,
headings and actions get room, and a modal breathes before its footer.

**1 · Similarity: the same kind of thing takes the same gap**

Items of equal weight, such as the fields of one form group, sit close
together with one stack.

<Canvas of={SpacingStories.Similarity} sourceState="none" />

**2 · Differentiation: a new group gets a bigger gap**

When the content changes kind, from a suggestion to the form controls, step
up a tier so the change reads at a glance.

<Canvas of={SpacingStories.Differentiation} sourceState="none" />

**3 · Rhythm: tiers, never one value**

Gaps grow with the box: Element and Card 4 · 8 · 12 inside a component,
Section and Modal 8 · 16 · 24 inside a region, Surface 16 · 24 · 40 between
regions.

<Canvas of={SpacingStories.Rhythm} sourceState="none" />

**4 · Hierarchy: headings and primary actions get room**

The heading and the main action read first because they have space around
them. Give them the larger stack of their tier, and never add space by hand.

<Canvas of={SpacingStories.Hierarchy} sourceState="none" />

**5 · White space: let a modal breathe before its footer**

Pad the modal on every side, and leave the largest gap of its tier between the
content and the footer buttons.

<Canvas of={SpacingStories.WhiteSpace} sourceState="none" />
</div>

### Tokens

Spacing has two layers.

- **Primitives** are the raw scale, named rem × 100: `--space-100` is 16,
  `--space-050` is 8. They are hidden in Figma, and nothing binds them.
- **Semantic tokens** point at a primitive and name a job, as
  `Collection/property-variant` in Figma, and the same words joined by dashes
  in code: `Card/pad-md` is `--card-pad-md`. A collection with a single
  property skips the variant (`--modal-pad`).

Name the relationship first, then pick the token. If you cannot name the
context a value belongs to, step back to the box that holds it.

### Three kinds of box

Every box on a page is one of three kinds, and the kind decides its padding.

- An **Item** has a minimum height, and its pad-x is larger than its pad-y.
- A **Container** has a background and one pad on every side, and its height
  hugs its content.
- A **Wrapper** has no fill and no pad. It only arranges its children with a
  gap or a grid.

<Canvas of={SpacingStories.ThreeKinds} sourceState="none" />
</div>

### Padding

An Item pads by its height, a Container pads by its size, and a Wrapper has no
pad.

**Items: height first**

The label stays 14/20 at every size, so pad-y is (height − 20) ÷ 2: 2 at 24
and 6 at 32. Pad-x is always the larger: 8 at 24 and 12 at 32. Controls come
in two heights only: 32 for everything, fields included, and 24 compact inside
table rows and inline toolbars. Labels (Tag, Status, Count) pad 4 across.

### Usage

How to build a feature that spaces like the rest of PLUS.

**Group first, then assign tokens.** Grouping is the first step to hierarchy.
Before you pick a token, group the content from the inside out; each step out
is one tier up. In a modal form:

1. Pair a control with what it changes, such as a field and its "Repeats
   weekly" switch: `--element-stack-md` (8).
2. Gather related fields: `--modal-stack-md` (16) between them.
3. Separate unrelated groups: `--modal-stack-lg` (24).
4. Separate the header from the body, and the body from the footer:
   `--modal-stack-lg` (24).
5. Set the actions side by side: `--modal-inline-sm` (8).

**Pick by context, not by number.** A Card token inside a card, a Section
token inside a section, Element tokens inside a component. Never borrow a
token because its number happens to match. When the card's tokens change, the
borrowed one does not follow.

**Never hand-space.** If a gap still looks wrong with the right tokens, step
to the next tier of the same group, such as `--section-stack-md` to
`--section-stack-lg`. If no tier fits, raise it as a token or pattern question
rather than adding space by hand.

<Canvas of={SpacingStories.NeverHandSpace} sourceState="none" />

**Bind the semantic token, never a primitive or a number.** Primitives are
hidden so nothing binds them, and a typed number breaks the moment a token
changes. A one-off value takes the nearest semantic token of its context, or
becomes a proposed token.

**Build features from patterns and slots.** Start from the pattern (Section,
Card, Modal, Popover) and keep it attached; never detach it. The pattern owns
the pad, the stacks and the size, and you add content only inside its slots.
If you copy a layout to get a variation, it needs a slot, not a new variant.

**No card inside a card.** A card groups one thing. Tiers inside it are
stacks and dividers in the card body, not more cards.

<Canvas of={SpacingStories.NoCardInCard} sourceState="none" />

**Let the content pick the size.** Visible empty space means a smaller size;
overflow or clipping means a larger one. Cards hug their content, with an
optional maximum height that scrolls the body. Modals hug or fill. Tables,
sections and tab groups grow to fit.
</div>

### Accessibility

- Keep the focus ring's room. The ring is drawn 2 outside a control, so a
  control never sits flush against a clipping edge, and Element and Card
  tokens leave room for it.
- Spacing groups content for sighted readers. The same groups need structure
  for everyone: a heading for each section, and a fieldset or a group label
  for related fields.
