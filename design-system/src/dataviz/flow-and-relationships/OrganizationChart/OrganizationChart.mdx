## When to use

Use `OrganizationChart` for reporting lines between people or roles, such as a
program team, lead-to-tutor supervision or support escalation paths. Each box
displays a name only; when titles, contact details or avatars are needed, build
the layout from UI components instead.

For non-people hierarchies such as subject → topic → lesson, use
`TreegraphChart`. For hierarchies sized by value, use `TreemapChart` or
`SunburstChart`. For non-tree relationships, use `NetworkGraph`.

## Data shape

`data` is an array of `[from, to]` pairs (`[manager, report]`). Because nodes
are keyed by name, every person's name must be unique or identical names merge
into one box.

```jsx
<OrganizationChart
    data={[
        ['Program Director', 'Lead Tutor, Math'],
        ['Program Director', 'Lead Tutor, Reading'],
        ['Lead Tutor, Math', 'Priya S.'],
        ['Lead Tutor, Math', 'Marcus T.'],
        ['Lead Tutor, Reading', 'Dana K.'],
    ]}
/>
```

## Color and accessibility

- **Depth-based color.** Colors mark row depth rather than individuals, and row position plus connector lines carry the hierarchy without hue.
- **Avoid wide or deep crowding.** Many boxes on one row shrink and truncate labels; split large organizations by team or increase `height` for deep trees.
- **Screen readers and keyboard.** Renders a labeled group with a screen-reader summary of its type, per-series and per-point labels, and arrow-key navigation. Name the chart in its surrounding Section or Card header.
- **Contrast.** Colors follow the page's color mode and increased-contrast setting; the tooltip uses the Popup pattern's background and text tokens ([Foundations](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-foundations--docs)).

## States

`OrganizationChart` has no empty, loading or error state. Mount a `Skeleton` matching the chart's height, a compact `EmptyState` naming the missing data, or a `SectionMessage` (`appearance="danger"`) with a retry action:

```jsx
{isLoading ? <Skeleton shape="rect" height={500} />
    : error ? <SectionMessage appearance="danger" dismissible={false} actions={[{ text: 'Retry', onClick: retry }]}>Team structure could not load.</SectionMessage>
    : data.length === 0 ? <EmptyState spacing="compact" header="No team members yet" description="The structure appears once tutors are assigned to a lead." />
    : <OrganizationChart data={data} />}
```
