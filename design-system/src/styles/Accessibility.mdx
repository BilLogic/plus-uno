<FoundationSection title="Overview">

WCAG 2.1 AA is the minimum bar for every PLUS screen. Tutors use PLUS on laptops during live sessions, often while sharing their screen with a student, so a screen stays legible at any zoom level and works under assistive technology.

Each foundation page owns the rules for its subject: contrast in [Color](?path=/docs/foundations-visual-color--docs), heading levels in [Typography](?path=/docs/foundations-visual-typography--docs#foundation-heading-levels), the focus ring in [Border](?path=/docs/foundations-visual-border--docs#foundation-focus-ring) and [Radius](?path=/docs/foundations-visual-radius--docs#foundation-focus-ring), and reduced motion in [Motion](?path=/docs/foundations-expression-motion-physics--docs#foundation-reduced-motion). Each component page adds the rules for its own name, role and keyboard behavior.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Heading levels">

Every page's outline starts with one `h1`, and a heading's tag is its place in the outline, never its size. A page whose name sits only in the topbar still has an `h1`, hidden visually with `VisuallyHidden` (never `display: none`). [Typography](?path=/docs/foundations-visual-typography--docs#foundation-heading-levels) and [Page](?path=/docs/templates-page--docs) own the outline rules.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Contrast">

Text clears 4.5:1 and icons, borders and the focus ring clear 3:1 against what they sit on, in light and dark (7:1 and 4.5:1 under increased contrast). Each text and icon token pairs with a surface or fill; [Color](?path=/docs/foundations-visual-color--docs) shows every measured pair.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Focus">

Focus is shown by the focus ring, never by a fill: the control's fill and opacity stay unchanged. [Border](?path=/docs/foundations-visual-border--docs#foundation-focus-ring) owns the ring's color, width and offset, and [Radius](?path=/docs/foundations-visual-radius--docs#foundation-focus-ring) owns its corner.

<FoundationSubsection title="Focus in overlays">

Opening a `Modal` or a floating `Panel` moves focus inside it and keeps Tab there; closing it returns focus to the control that opened it. Both do this themselves, and a new overlay gets the same behavior by building on Base UI.

</FoundationSubsection>

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Keyboard">

Every interactive element can be reached and operated from the keyboard.

- Tab order follows the visual reading order: left to right, top to bottom.
- Widgets follow standard keyboard patterns: arrow keys move through a menu or radio group, and Escape closes an overlay.
- New interactive behavior is built on [Base UI](?path=/docs/tech-stack-base-ui--docs) (`@base-ui/react`), which supplies these patterns.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Screen readers">

- Use semantic elements first (`button`, `nav`, `main`, `table`), and ARIA only when no element fits.
- Name any control that has no visible text with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- Announce content that changes without a page load through a live region (`role="status"` with `aria-live="polite"`), as the `Table` bulk-action count does.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Disabled">

A disabled component keeps its label so people can tell what is unavailable. [Color](?path=/docs/foundations-visual-color--docs) owns how disabled looks, and [Disabled](?path=/docs/patterns-states-disabled--docs) owns when to use it.

- Disabled elements are not focus targets, and none uses `aria-disabled` to stay focusable.
- State the reason a field or option is disabled in visible text beside it at full contrast, never in a tooltip on the disabled control.
- Keep submit actions enabled and explain what is missing when pressed; disable a button only while it is sending.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Forms">

[Forms](?path=/docs/patterns-forms--docs) and [Errors](?path=/docs/patterns-states-errors--docs) own form layout and validation.

- Every field has a visible label tied to its control (`htmlFor` to `id`).
- An error states what went wrong and how to fix it on the field through `errorMessage`.
- A required field takes `required`, and the form places "Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*)" above its first field.
- Related fields sit in a `Fieldset`, which renders a `fieldset` and its `legend`.

</FoundationSection>

<FoundationSection title="Charts">

Every chart describes its type, axes, series and points to screen readers, and its data can be read from the keyboard. A chart is a labeled group inside its section, not a landmark of its own. Give every chart a title or label, and never carry a series by color alone; see [Chart color](?path=/docs/data-visualizations-color--docs).

</FoundationSection>
