## Usage

Use Disabled when something on the page cannot be used right now. Reach for it last: an enabled action that explains is better than a dimmed one, read-only is better for content people must read, and hiding is right for what a role can never use.

</thead>

- For a value this person cannot change but must read, follow <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-states-read-only--docs">Read-only</a>.

## Best practices

### Keep actions enabled and explain what is missing when pressed.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

A disabled button gives no direction and leaves the tab order. Pressing an action that cannot run yet marks what is missing. A button disables only while sending. A form's submit follows <a target="_top" href="./?path=/docs/patterns-actions-and-input-forms--docs">Forms</a>.

### Disable a field or option only for a prerequisite the person can fix on the same screen.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Group waits on School; a time slot waits on a date. Anything blocked by something off this screen is enabled and explains, read-only, or hidden.

### Put the reason in visible text where the cause is.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

The field caption or the option's description line. When the prerequisite blocks the main action or several items, a warning `SectionMessage` above says how to turn it on. Self-evident cases need no reason: Back on the first step.

### Keep the reason in text on the page, never in a tooltip on a disabled control.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

A disabled control gets no events and is absent on touch, so a tooltip would never open for the people who need it most. A disabled control keeps its default behavior and leaves the tab order.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<ReasonInCaption />}
    doCaption='The reason as the caption: "Choose a school first".'
    dontExample={<ReasonInTooltip />}
    dontCaption="The reason in a tooltip on a faded field: it never opens on touch."
/>

### Hide what a role can never use.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

An action a tutor can never take, such as deleting a school, is left out, not dimmed. A breadcrumb level the person cannot open is left out. Only a change of role brings it back.

<DoDontPair
    doExample={<LeftOutOfMenu />}
    doCaption="An action a tutor can never take is left out of the menu."
    dontExample={<DimmedInMenu />}
    dontCaption='A faded "Delete school" that a tutor will never be able to press.'
/>

### Lock the Toolkit during onboarding and keep it visible.

<RuleTag kind="review" />

Tutors see what training unlocks. The Sidebar item shows a lock and a line that says what unlocks it; the line is Planned <PlannedTag />.

### Make content people must read read-only, not disabled.

<RuleTag kind="automatic" />

Disabled fades and leaves the tab order; read-only stays readable at full contrast. The two are never swapped.

## Accessibility

- A disabled control leaves the tab order and gets no events; the reason beside it is ordinary text a screen reader reads with the field, tied through the caption.
- The warning `SectionMessage` is announced when it appears.
- Disabled controls fade; the caption keeps full contrast, so the reason stays readable.
