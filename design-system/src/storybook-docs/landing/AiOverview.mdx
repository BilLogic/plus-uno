<FoundationSection title="Identity and trust">

Six rules hold for every AI surface in PLUS, for tutors and students alike.

1. **One AI identity, unnamed.** The AI shows as AI: the AI icon and the AI label. It has no persona name, no face or avatar, and no brand voice beyond a plain "I".
2. **Every AI answer is marked.** An AI message carries the AI icon. Generated content placed into the product, such as a draft or a summary, carries the AI label until a person edits or accepts it.
3. **Fallibility, once.** One line under the prompt input says "AI can make mistakes. Check important info." It is not repeated on each message.
4. **Sources when it draws on data.** An answer that uses records lists the sources it read.
5. **People confirm actions.** The AI proposes. Anything that changes data waits for the person to confirm it.
6. **Feedback on every answer.** Each AI answer offers thumbs up and thumbs down.

</FoundationSection>
